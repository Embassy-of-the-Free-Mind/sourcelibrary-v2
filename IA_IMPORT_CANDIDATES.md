## Notes on Completeness

- **Ficino Pimander (1481):** Standalone Hermetica translation. The 1570 edition (ID: `hin-wel-all-00001266-001`) is a multi-author compilation that INCLUDES Ficino's Pimander plus other Neoplatonic texts. Both are valuable; the 1481 is more focused.
- **Zosimos:** The 1814 Gruner edition is the most accessible scholarly edition with parallel Greek/Latin. Berthelot's collection (1888) is also strong but less complete.
- **Books of Jeu:** Embedded in Mead's Pistis Sophia (1896). There is no standalone "Books of Jeu" edition in IA from the pre-1928 period that I found. Mead's 1896 is the standard.
- **Valentin Weigel:** Primary Weigel texts exist in German (not thoroughly searched in IA). The Kingsford 1886 translation is a secondary/reception history source but still valuable.
