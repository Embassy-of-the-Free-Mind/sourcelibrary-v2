# Board Resolution — Open Source Licensing

## UNANIMOUS WRITTEN CONSENT OF THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF PLAYPOWER LABS, INC.

**Date:** _______________

The undersigned, being all of the directors of PlayPower Labs, Inc., a
Delaware corporation (the "Company"), acting pursuant to Section 141(f)
of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby consent to and adopt
the following resolutions without a meeting:

### RESOLVED:

**1. Open Source Release.** The Board hereby authorizes and approves the
release of the software known as "Source Library" (repository:
`sourcelibrary-v2`) under the GNU Affero General Public License,
version 3 or later (AGPL-3.0-or-later), effective upon adoption of this
resolution.

**2. Content Licensing.** The Board hereby authorizes and approves the
release of all machine-generated OCR transcriptions and AI-generated
translations produced by Source Library under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0).

**3. Database License Grant.** The Board hereby authorizes the grant of a
perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to Stichting
Wereldhart (Amsterdam, The Netherlands), operating as the Embassy of the
Free Mind, covering all database rights (including sui generis rights
under the EU Database Directive 96/9/EC) in the Source Library catalog,
as set forth in the License Grant Agreement executed contemporaneously
herewith.

**4. Retention of Ownership.** For the avoidance of doubt, the Company
retains all copyright and intellectual property ownership in the
software, content, and database. The foregoing resolutions authorize
licensing, not assignment, of these rights.

**5. Authorization.** Any officer of the Company is hereby authorized to
execute any documents and take any actions necessary to effectuate the
foregoing resolutions.

---

**IN WITNESS WHEREOF**, the undersigned have executed this consent as of
the date first written above.
