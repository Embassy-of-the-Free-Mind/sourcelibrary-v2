## Strategy

The beta launch on March 21 is the catalyst. It gives us:
1. **A thing to show.** Not a pitch deck, not a promise — a working library with 5,000+ books, AI translation, and open access.
2. **A reason to reach out.** "We just launched" is the best cold-email opener in fundraising.
3. **A deadline to prepare against.** One week to get everything in order.

The 3-month window (March 21 – June 21) is a concentrated push to secure founding donors while the launch energy is fresh. After June, shift to foundation grant cycles (longer timelines, formal applications).

## 5. The Press Release

### Angle options (pick one or blend)

**A. "AI unlocks 5,000 hidden Renaissance texts"**
Best for: Tech press, general media. Emphasizes scale and AI angle.

**B. "Amsterdam library opens its secret vault to the world"**
Best for: Culture/travel press. Emphasizes Embassy of the Free Mind, UNESCO, mystery.

**C. "For the first time in 500 years, you can read what the alchemists wrote"**
Best for: General public, social virality. Emphasizes access and wonder.

**D. "The biggest translation project since the Renaissance itself"**
Best for: Academic press, digital humanities. Emphasizes scholarly significance.

### Key facts for any angle
- 5,000+ books in Source Library (from Bibliotheca Philosophica Hermetica)
- AI-powered translation from Latin, German, Dutch, and other source languages
- Open access — free for everyone, no paywall
- Embassy of the Free Mind, Amsterdam — UNESCO-recognized collection of 25,000+ rare texts
- Texts span alchemy, Hermetica, Kabbalah, Rosicrucianism, early modern science
- Authors include Ficino, Agrippa, Paracelsus, Bruno, Dee
- Ancient Wisdom Trust / NAF fiscal sponsorship — tax-deductible donations

## 6. Key Talking Points

From `communication-strategy.md`, refined for founding donor conversations:

**The hook:** "We're using AI to translate the books that inspired Copernicus, Newton, and the Scientific Revolution — and they've been unreadable for 500 years."

**The stats:** 95% of Renaissance books lack translations. 70% haven't been digitized. Source Library is fixing both.

**The urgency:** "The technology exists now. The collection exists. The only thing missing is the funding to connect them at scale."

**The legacy:** "You become a patron of the Renaissance — the second Renaissance. Your name alongside the Medici."

**The AI angle (for tech donors):** "AI systems are being trained on what happened to make it online. We're making sure they learn from humanity's deepest thinking, not just Reddit."
