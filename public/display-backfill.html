Sharp resize + provenance compositing is the main CPU cost (~200ms/page). With concurrency=15, that's 15 parallel sharp pipelines sharing 8 cores. R2 is fast (free egress, ~50ms upload). Atlas is the wildcard — fine under low load, but page queries slow down when 300+ pipeline jobs are active.

Theoretical max on 8 cores: ~40 pages/sec (200ms each). Practical limit: ~12-15 pages/sec due to Atlas latency and R2 round-trips.
