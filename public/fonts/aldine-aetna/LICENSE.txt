Aldine Aetna — facsimile type traced from Pietro Bembo, De Aetna (Venice: Aldus Manutius, 1496).
Letterforms cut by Francesco Griffo, 1495–96. Traced by Source Library (sourcelibrary.org), 2026,
from the Biblioteca Nazionale Centrale di Firenze copy via Internet Archive.
