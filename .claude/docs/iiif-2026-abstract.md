## Abstract

The Source Library (sourcelibrary.org) is an open digital library on a mission to translate the Renaissance. Born from the UNESCO-recognized Bibliotheca Philosophica Hermetica (Embassy of the Free Mind) in Amsterdam, it curates primarily 15th–18th century texts in Latin, German, Hebrew, Arabic, and more. IIIF and compatible image APIs serve as a universal input layer, ingesting books from 13+ institutional providers including Gallica, MDZ, Bodleian, Vatican, e-rara, Cambridge, and the Internet Archive. Each book's page images flow through Gemini vision OCR, automated translation, illustration detection, and DOI-minted scholarly edition publishing — with the same downstream pipeline regardless of the source institution. The result: over 2,000 books identified as first English translations and nearly 70,000 extracted illustrations. IIIF's interoperability promise is transformative when the consumer is an AI model.
