## Guidance
For **Daoist/alchemical + classical Chinese**, prioritize the three clean, scriptable public-IIIF sources: **Kyoto RMDA (Seike)**, **NDL (items with real PIDs)**, **Harvard-Yenching**. (**Waseda was demoted 2026-08-28** — its IIIF turned out to be a 443-manifest highlights set with ~0 overlap with Daoist/medical subjects; see its corrected row. The 253-record candidate list from the audit is at `scripts/import/waseda-daoist-candidates.json`, actionable only via a kosho directory-walker.) **Bodleian Sinica** and **Cambridge** are strong second-tier for rarer European-held items. Budget extra engineering (headless browser / hard throttling) for **IDP, Gallica, Princeton, NCL-Taiwan**. For Daoist canonical *texts* (Daozang, Daozang jiyao), pair scanned editions from the IIIF sources with **Kanripo/CBETA** transcriptions — but Kanripo is a text/git harvest, not a manifest import. Avoid auto-importing from **ctext.org / NLC China / CADAL's own portal** (no public IIIF, anti-scraping or member-gated) — but CADAL content mirrored on **Internet Archive** (`universallibrary`) is fair game via the normal IA import path (see gap analysis below).

## Is NLC/CADAL-in-China actually needed? (gap analysis, 2026-06-06)
Sampled 50 of the 3,418 Siku-census works (seeded RNG, Wikidata zh labels) and checked item-level availability outside China:

- **49/50 (98%) accessible without NLC or CADAL membership.** 45/50 have open transcribed text in **Kanripo** (github.com/kanripo — 10,142 works, **3,477 explicitly sourced from the Wenyuan Ge Siku Quanshu**, i.e. effectively the whole SKQS corpus of ~3,461 works); 27+/50 have CADAL facsimile scans on **Internet Archive** (`universallibrary`). The single residual (尚絅齋集, Yuan wenji) is still in the complete Wenyuan Ge SKQS facsimile on ctext + the 1986 Taiwan Commercial Press reprint.
- **Conclusion: for the Siku corpus there is no NLC-only residue.** NLC's 中華古籍資源庫 matters for rare *editions* and unique manuscripts outside SKQS (Song/Yuan imprints, MSS), not for the works themselves. Don't route friends-in-China or proxy services at this corpus; use IA-CADAL + Kanripo + the IIIF sources above. For genuinely NLC-only rare editions, the durable path is institutional (CADAL membership is open to non-Chinese universities; NLC has co-digitization precedent with Harvard-Yenching — 1,100+ joint titles already behind Harvard IIIF).
- **Matching gotcha:** Wikidata zh labels use variant glyphs that defeat naive string match against Kanripo/IA (説/說, 録/錄, 黙/默, 寳/寶, 様/樣, 歴/歷, 戸/戶, 畧/略, 䟽/疏, 㴑/溯, 紀/記). Raw match rate was 72%; variant normalization rescued 11 of 14 misses. Normalize before matching. (Scratch scripts: `scripts/_tmp-chinese-gap-analysis.mjs`, results `scripts/_tmp-chinese-gap-results.json`.)

## Importer notes
- Our `/api/import/iiif` takes a manifest URL. Confirm the manifest resolves (HTTP 200, has canvases) before import.
- Handle **both IIIF Presentation v2 and v3** (Kyoto RMDA = v3; Waseda/Harvard/Cambridge/Bodleian = v2).
- These are scans → they enter the OCR→translation pipeline; Classical-Chinese OCR/translation needs a QA pass (cf. Siku census).
