HOW THE QUEUE WORKS (read these before you change anything):

- scripts/gpu/ocr-filler.sh (installed copy /root/bin/ocr-filler.sh, routes /root/bin/ocr-filler-routes.tsv): queues one OCR
  chunk when the queue is empty; log /var/log/sourcelibrary/ocr-filler.log; state /root/gpu-queue/ocr-filler/
  (<route>.DISABLED after 3 failed chunks in a row, <route>.EMPTY when a route has no planned pages left, PAUSE, runs.jsonl).
- An engine's real error is usually in the lane's work dir (e.g. /root/jobs/<lane>/work/<ts>/drive.log, serve-*.log),
  not in task.log.
