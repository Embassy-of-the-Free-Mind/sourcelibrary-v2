Hello Source Library friends,

Can I get your feedback?

I’ve had the most fun time building the Source Library, but I haven’t had the time to read all of the 22,069 books in there. If you come across something that could be improved, no matter how small, please let me know. It might be in the UI or it might be the content of the translation or maybe some of our book data is wrong.

I want you to know that I personally read the feedback on the Source Library every day. You are welcome to email me!

I also want to make sure everyone knows how to leave feedback in the site. You see, down on the bottom of every page there is a button to leave feedback.

And then, further down, here… and even here, on the footer.

That way, I know what page or service you are leaving the feedback on.

And if it is the translation you want to flag, there is a quieter one right under the English text while you are reading. That one already knows which book and which page you are on.

I really want to engage the reading community. Not everyone is interested in reading texts that are hundreds of years old… yet! I’d love to make the reading experience as pleasant and trustworthy as possible.

Or just reply and tell me your feedback. I’d love to know what you find useful and what you think needs improvement. And if there is something you like, tell me that too — I may want to quote a reader or two on the site, so let me know if you are happy for me to use your words.

— Derek
