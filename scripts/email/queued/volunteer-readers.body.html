Hello again,

Last time I asked you to tell me when something looks wrong, and you did. Now I want to ask for something bigger from a smaller number of you.

Almost every translation in the library was made by a machine. I think they are good. I do not actually know how good, book by book, and no amount of automated scoring will tell me, because the thing I want to know is whether the English says what the Latin says.

So: if you read Latin, or German, or Arabic, or Chinese, or anything else we hold, would you take one text you already care about and tell me how the translation holds up? Rate it, argue with it, mark the passages where it drifts. I would rather have one book read properly by someone who knows it than a thousand scored by a model.

The same goes for the blog posts. I write those quickly, and they surely contain things only a specialist would catch.

You do not need credentials for this, and you do not need to fix anything yourself. Use the same feedback button as everyone else, on whichever page you are reading — it tells me where you were, and I take it from there.

Just the language and the interest.
