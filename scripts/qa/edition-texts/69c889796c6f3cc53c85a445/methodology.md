## How this edition was made

Source Library made this edition from photographs of a printed copy, with the help of AI models at each step. No specialist has reviewed the transcription or the translation; read the English as a working translation, and check anything that matters against the page images.

1. **Page images.** The 248 page images come from Bibliotheca Philosophica Hermetica, photographed from the copy at the Bibliotheca Philosophica Hermetica. Source Library keeps its own archived copy of each image.
2. **Transcription.** Each page image was read by gemini-3.1-flash-lite-preview (164 pages), gemini-3-flash-preview (84 pages) (May 2026), which transcribed the Latin and marked the running heads, marginal notes and illustrations on the page.
3. **Translation.** gemini-3-flash-preview (242 pages) translated the transcription into English one page at a time, given the previous page's translation for continuity; the last page was translated on 3 May 2026. The footnotes come from this step: they are the translation's explanations, not the author's.
4. **Illustrations.** Every page carrying a figure was shown to the model again with its transcription and translation; the model boxed each figure, gave it a short title, and transcribed and translated the words engraved on it, which form the captions. A small cut is printed at roughly the share of the page it takes in the original.
5. **Setting.** The book was typeset with Typst. The Latin transcription is not printed here; each source page number in the margin, and the line at the foot of each page, links to the photograph of that page, and the transcription can be read beside it at sourcelibrary.org.

Corrections are welcome at sourcelibrary.org, and later versions of this edition will carry them.
