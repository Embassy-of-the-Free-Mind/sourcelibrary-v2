## How this edition was made

Source Library made this edition from photographs of a printed copy, with the help of AI models at each step. No Latinist has reviewed the transcription or the translation; read the English as a working translation, and check anything that matters against the page images.

1. **Page images.** The 1,036 page images come from the Internet Archive (item *utriusquecosmima01flud*), photographed from the copy at the Getty Research Institute. Source Library keeps its own archived copy of each image.
2. **Transcription.** Each page image was read by Google's Gemini model (gemini-3-flash-preview) in February 2026, which transcribed the Latin and marked the running heads, marginal notes and illustrations on the page.
3. **Translation.** The same model translated the transcription into English one page at a time, given the previous page's translation for continuity. Every page was translated again by 1 June 2026, after an error that had paired some pages with the wrong text was corrected. The footnotes come from this step: they are the translation's explanations, not Fludd's.
4. **Illustrations.** For this illustrated edition (October 2026), every page carrying a figure was shown to the model again with its transcription and translation. The model drew a box around each figure, gave it a short title, and transcribed and translated the words engraved on it; those translations form the captions. A small cut is printed at roughly the share of the page it takes in the original. The headpieces and tailpieces are the book's own, located by the model and checked by eye.
5. **Setting.** The book was typeset with Typst. The Latin transcription is not printed here; each source page number in the margin, and the line at the foot of each page, links to the photograph of that page, and the transcription can be read beside it at sourcelibrary.org.

Corrections are welcome at sourcelibrary.org, and later versions of this edition will carry them.
