Calibration Sitting

How this works (2 minutes to read, about 3 minutes a page)

36 pages from four languages. For each one, read the scan, then the transcription, then the English. Two model reviewers have already marked possible errors, shown below the texts without saying which model marked which. For each mark, say whether it is a real error, and fix its severity if it is wrong. Add any error they missed. Then put the page in a band.

  • Critical: a reader quoting this page would publish something the book does not say. That covers invented or wrong-leaf text, reversed meaning, a changed name, number or date, or a dropped line.
  • Major: misleading locally but recoverable, or a note or margin lost. Minor: the quotable words are right.
  • The texts are shown as stored. Tags like <page-num>, <meta>, <vocab> or <summary> are moved out of the reading text on the site, so they are not errors. Judge the words.
  • Some pages are deliberate test pages. Judge every page the same way.
  • Your answers save as you go. You can stop and come back; the page reopens where you left off.

Keys: J / K next and previous page, 1 2 3 band.
