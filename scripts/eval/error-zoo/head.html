Error Zoo

Every kind of thing that has gone wrong on a Source Library page, with real specimens. Classes come from the page-error taxonomy (44 classes, by lane: Image, OCR, Translation, Display, dErived). Counts and specimens come from every page-level review we hold: book_checks (19 QA methods, image-opened model readers, Oct–Nov 2026), the weekly audit (Opus, 23 rounds, 10–11 Nov 2026), the lane judge's served-arm verdicts and applied corrections. A reader's finding is a finding, not a verdict.

seriousmoderateminor

Counts are findings on about 11,300 reviewed pages across four label stores (the stats line gives the exact figures). Serious findings carry the reader's own class in 95% of cases; minor and moderate findings mostly did not, and were classed afterwards by legacy-string mapping, keyword rules and a small model (agreement with the model on a 400-item calibration sample is stated in the experiment log). The weekly audit's draw is the only one uniform over the library; the other stores are picked frames (most-read books, lane queues, repairs). Classes with no specimens were not seen.
