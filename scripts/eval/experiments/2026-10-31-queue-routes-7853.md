### Strata, weights, draw (`frame.mjs draw`, seed 78531)

- **Explicit strata**, per language: century band of the book's year (≤ 1600, 17th, 18th, 19th+, undated) × scan band. A book's scan band is `good` when at least half of its owed pages carry the stored read's own `<scan-quality>good</scan-quality>` tag, else `fair-poor`. That tag is on every Flash-Lite page. The book-level `scan_quality.score` is missing on many books, so it is reported but not used.
- **Allocation.** German 150 pages, French 60, proportional to owed pages by largest remainder. A stratum under 1% of its language's owed pages gets no page and is reported as uncovered (German 1.1% of pages, French 0.1%). Each covered stratum gets at least 2.
  - German: 17th good 25, 17th fair-poor 4, 18th good 77, 18th fair-poor 10, 19th+ good 14, 19th+ fair-poor 7, ≤ 1600 good 13.
  - French: 17th good 14, 17th fair-poor 2, 18th good 20, 18th fair-poor 2, 19th+ good 14, 19th+ fair-poor 3, ≤ 1600 good 5.
- **Draw.** Sequential Poisson sampling, PPS on owed pages. Each book gets one uniform (`makeRng(78531)` over all owing books in id order, drawn before exclusions, so the exclusion list cannot move the draw), key = U / owed. A stratum's books are taken in ascending key; the first n_h are the sample and the rest are its reserves, in order.
- **The page.** One owed page of the book, uniform from a stream seeded by the book id, with an image source. Its block is the page and the next 3 visible pages of the book (shifted back at the book's end); a book of under 4 pages is replaced.
- **Typeface** (post-stratum). One agy call a page (`agyFace`, #7835 T4, unchanged): fraktur / antiqua / mixed / none. A page tagged `none` (no printed body text), an unfetchable image (e-rara answers 403 to this box), or a failed tag is replaced by the stratum's next reserve, and counted. Tags run in stratum order, 2 at a time, until each stratum holds n_h usable pages.
- **Weights.** Horvitz–Thompson: a page stands for w = owed / π pages, with π = min(1, n_h · owed / M_h) (M_h is the stratum's owed pages). Inside a stratum the draw is self-weighting unless a book is big enough to be certain. Every weighted figure below uses w within the language. Unweighted k/n are reported beside it.

### Decision rule (per language, `analyze.mjs decide`)

1. **Best** = the route arm (1–4) with the highest weighted accept.
2. **Eligible** = best, and every arm within 5 points of best's weighted accept whose paired wrong-text pages against best, added minus removed, are under 2. Below 2 is #7835's line: "no more wrong text", allowing one page of noise.
3. **Route** = the eligible arm with the most accepted pages per agy call. An arm within 10% of that rate is a tie, and a tie goes to the cheaper arm in the order read4 < corr < read1 < gpu (gpu last: it also holds the GPU).
- The rule is applied to German (the primary cell) and French separately. Arms that did not run are left out and named. A primary contrast that is supported in best's disfavour is reported beside the rule's pick.

**A1.1 Co-primary outcome: severity-weighted defects per 1,000 words.**
- The measure is #7824's, with MQM weights: cosmetic 1, misleads 5, wrong_text 25.
- It is counted on the judge's own defect tags, per 1,000 words of the served text (`words()`).
- **Comparisons** (the same as for accept): arm 2 against arm 3, and arm 1 against arm 3, German primary, French exploratory.
- **Test.** Paired per page. The difference is the HT-weighted mean of per-page differences, with a book-cluster bootstrap 95% CI (one page per book, so books are resampled, within their strata, 4,000 resamples) and a Wilcoxon signed-rank p.
- **Holm** over the two contrasts. Holm over all four primary tests (2 outcomes × 2 contrasts) is also reported.
- Supported = Holm-adjusted p < 0.05.

**A1.2 Accept is the gate, not the objective.** An arm passes when its weighted accept is no more than 5 points below the best arm's.

**A1.3 Fidelity (1–5).** A secondary outcome: the HT-weighted mean per arm.

**A1.4 Decision rule (replaces §Decision rule's steps 2–3).** Among the arms that pass the gate, the lowest weighted defect rate per agy call wins; a tie goes to the cheaper arm.
- **How "per agy call" is read** (`decideV2`): the arm that removes the most severity per agy call, that is (the stored text's weighted severity per 1,000 words − the arm's) / the arm's agy calls a page.
- **Why that reading.** agy quota is the binding constraint, so this is the route that takes the most weighted defects out of the queue for a fixed number of calls. A literal "rate ÷ calls" would reward the arm that spends more calls.
- If no gated arm removes any severity, the arm with the lowest rate wins.
- **Ties**: an arm within 10% of the top is a tie, and goes to the cheaper arm in the order read4 < corr < read1 < gpu.
- The gated arm with the lowest severity rate (cost aside) is reported beside the pick.
- The first rule's wrong-text condition is dropped: wrong_text now carries weight 25 in the measure itself.

**A1.5 Re-score of earlier studies** (`rescore-severity.mjs`, no new judge call).
- **#7818:** from its committed verdicts (`correction-pass-7818/results/release`, rep 1). Arm P's texts are not committed, so each page's points are put on one common length: the page's current stored text for Arm P, and the Wikisource draft's characters / 6.5 for Arm W.
