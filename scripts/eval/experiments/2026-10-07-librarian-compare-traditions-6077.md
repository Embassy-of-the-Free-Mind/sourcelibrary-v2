**Verdict: not ready.** Only turn 2 passes everything; turn 3 passes with minor notes. Four turns fail at least one check. **Fixed in this PR** (tool and prompt), with unit tests:

1. **Outside voices.** `tradition-search.ts` `taggedOutside()` flags a passage whose book's own `faceted_tags.tradition` excludes the lane. The tool result then labels it "Outside voice … say whose reading it is". The prompt adds: an interpreter is named as an interpreter, and a doctrine no passage states stays out of the answer. Turn 0′ shows the first half working. The second half (biṭṭul without a source) still leaks.
2. **Quotation marks.** Quote marks only around words copied from a passage received this turn, each with its page link; a paraphrase gets none. Partly visible in 0′.
3. **Author links.** `compare_traditions` gave the model author *names* with no links, unlike `search`, so it built `/author/…` itself: 3 of the 8 answers had a 404. All three (0′, 4 and 5) ran after fixes 1 and 2. The tool now hands over the same `author_id || authorSlug(author)` link `search` uses. A probe over 18 passages in two queries: 18/18 author links answer 200. Not yet re-run through the model.
4. **Named authors.** When the reader names a thinker and no passage is by them, the answer must say so and must not file another author's passage under that name (turn 5).

**Not fixed here.** The lanes themselves: the Kabbalah shelf's top results are Crowley and Blavatsky; the Chan lane is modern writers about Zen, not Chan records. That is shelf membership, filed as #6193.
