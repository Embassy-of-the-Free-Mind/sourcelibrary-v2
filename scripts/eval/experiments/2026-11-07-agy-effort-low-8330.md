## 2026-11-07 · Should narrowed agy calls pass `--effort low`? (#8330)

**Why.** Narrowing agy's tool list (the `page-reader` / `page-translator` agents) raised how often a call thinks at
length: Arabic 6 of 86 calls vs 0 of 88. A token-only run of `--effort low` on the 20 Arabic pages cut that to 2 of 89.
This entry asks whether the saving holds on each lane, and whether quality holds with it.

**Rule, posted on #8330 before the low reads.** A lane switches on only if all three hold. (1) The judge catches every
planted control in that set. (2) Low's usable count is at most 2 of 56 below default on print, at most 1 of 20 on
correction and Arabic, and on translation no further below than default's two reads are from each other. Paired by
page, the pages worse under low may not outnumber the better ones by more than that margin. (3) Low's mean index over
its repeats is below default's mean, and at most one low repeat is above it. Index = (input + cache_read) + 6 ×
(output + thinking) a page. **The 6× is an assumption** (Gemini's list-price ratio), not a measured quota rate. On agy
1.5.3 `input` leaves out `cache_read`, so the two are added. `score.mjs`'s "uncached" column subtracts cache_read a
second time and is not quoted.

**Method.** gemini-3.8-flash-low, agy 1.5.3, main box, `--fresh`, `CANDIDATE_READS=off`, nothing applied. The low
arms are the same code with `AGY_EFFORT=low` (commit 01d07c8aa). Default arms are the stored #8330 reads, not re-bought:
print `de-la/once4` (read on the laptop; no call was cached in either arm), correction `correct/once`, Arabic
`arabic/once1` + `arabic-repeat/once-r{2,3,4}`, translation `translate/B2` + `C4`. For each OCR set one low run was
judged against its default in a fresh blind judge (#7522's `judge.mjs`, Opus via sealed `claude -p`). The 4 controls
are planted in a copy of one arm's read. On Arabic they went in the default arm's copy (`--arms=low1,once1`), because
low-r1's read of one control page was a Gemini filter cut. Correction's UNCHANGED answers were judged as the stored
draft (9 default, 7 low); the drafts were checked unchanged since the default run. Translation used the #7503 judge,
with default B2/C4 in slots A1/A1b (so the A1~A1b gap is the noise floor) and low r1/r2 in B2/C4.
Reads: `/mnt/HC_Volume_105839809/ab8330/effort/`. Results: `agy-read-file-7558/results/effort-8330/`.

**The translation judge missed a control again.** It is the same one both earlier runs missed: the drop on
`6a3c618c879008ba7e15ef34`, a sentence removed from a page that restates one idea several times. The judge called that
version "faithful and complete". It caught the other two drops and both bleeds. By the harness's rule this run is
`probe_broken`, not a result, and this entry does not count it as evidence for either arm. Three misses out of three
on that page mean this judge cannot see a dropped sentence on a repetitive page. Omission counts from it are a floor.

**Arabic additions doubled (3 → 6 faults) on 20 pages** with the usable count unchanged. That is too few to read as a
change, and it is not explained away here. It is the fault class to watch when the lane runs at low effort.

### Decision, lane by lane

- **Correction: on.** Controls 4/4; usable 20/20 both; mean index 28.5K vs 34.4K (−17%); one of three low runs
  (36.5K) is above default. That passes the rule, but the default is a **single** 20-page run, and Arabic showed that a
  single 20-page run can swing from 18.8K to 30.8K. The saving is likely real (output + thinking a page fell in all
  three runs: 1.6K, 1.5K, 2.0K vs 2.9K), but its size is not settled.
- **Arabic manuscripts at 1 a call: on.** Controls 4/4; usable 14/20 both, paired 4 worse and 4 better; mean index
  21.0K vs 23.7K (−11%), and all four low runs are below the default mean. Low runs also vary far less (20.6–21.4K vs
  18.8–30.8K): the long calls are what it removes.
- **Print at 4 a call: stays at default.** Quality holds (55 vs 56), but both low runs cost more (16.2K and 12.8K vs
  11.5K). Low effort gave more long calls (8 over 5K in 112 pages vs 2 in 56).
- **Translation: stays at default.** It costs more at low effort (14.1K and 12.7K vs 11.4K), and its judge run is
  probe_broken.

The switch is scoped to the cells tested: correction and Arabic-language books, each only at 1 page a call. A page
re-read alone after a refused block call is not included. Block calls (`--per-call` > 1) for either lane were not
tested and keep the default.

**What this can and cannot show.** 20 pages detect only a collapse, a drop of about 25–30 points in usable rate; 56
pages, about 10 points. A drop of a few points would pass unseen on every set. Print's default ran on the laptop and its
low arm on the box. Neither had a cached call, so the cost comparison is fair, but the hosts differ. How the
subscription pool charges these tokens was not measured. Why low effort lengthens print and translation calls was not
investigated.
