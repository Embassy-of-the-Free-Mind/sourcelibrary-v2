<warning>Handwritten East Asian brush script (semi-cursive kaishu). In each label, the character the draft read as "并" is the ligature 𦬇 for 菩薩, so it is expanded here. Drawings from the neighbouring panels are partly visible at the left and right edges.</warning>

<image-desc size="large" type="diagram" significance="high">An ink line drawing of a mandala. A square outer enclosure has a gateway on each side, and each gateway holds a wheel-like emblem. Inside are concentric circles and a square, with triangles pointing to the four gates. At the centre is an eight-petalled lotus, with a central disc and small flower and vajra motifs on the petals. Lotus blossoms, crescent moons, vajras, ritual vessels, offering stands and spears fill the spaces between the enclosures. Brush labels in vertical cartouches name the four gates, two on each side.</image-desc>
