## 五都滅亡彗星册赤占

<image-desc size="large" type="illustration" significance="high">A painted landscape in ink and colour. In the upper left of a pale grey sky is a red disc. A long red tail streaks from it up to the right. On the right a tall grey rock cliff rises, with dark trees and foliage on top. Below are a dark leafy tree and small blue, green and grey mountain peaks along the bottom edge. Brown stains mark the sky.</image-desc>

朱文公曰
五都滅亡彗星册赤此歲星之
精流而為変者
宋志曰
赤彗主滅五都

## 照明星占

<image-desc size="large" type="illustration" significance="high">A painted landscape in ink and colour. In the upper right of a grey sky is a white star drawn as a disc ringed with white petal-shaped rays. On the left is a rocky outcrop with dark trees and a bare-branched tree. Beside it, a building with tiered roofs stands on wooden stilts against a green hill. Blue, grey and green peaks are on the right. Brown stains mark the sky.</image-desc>

朱文公曰
照明如太白而光芒不行占以
為起覇而或為起徳
宋志曰
照明狀如太白光芒不行體大
白色而有角如三足上有九彗見
則兵大起天下有喪多變而大人凶
晋書曰
照明如太白光芒不行以為起霸
起徳之兆
