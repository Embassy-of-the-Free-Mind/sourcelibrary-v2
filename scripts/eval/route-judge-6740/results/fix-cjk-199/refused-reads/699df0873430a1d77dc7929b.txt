# 日暈在卯辰巳上占

<image-desc size="large" type="diagram" significance="high">Coloured painting of a solar halo. A solid red sun is circled by a blue ring and a thin yellow outer ring, with a dark blue haze spreading around it. Below is a landscape with a rocky cliff and a large tree on the left, a two-tiered pavilion with blue roofs, pine trees on a hill, a green peak in the middle distance, and blue and green rock pillars on the right.</image-desc>

朱文公曰

日暈卯辰巳方月內多雨

乙巳略例曰

月暈在卯辰巳方天子憂
民月內多雨
