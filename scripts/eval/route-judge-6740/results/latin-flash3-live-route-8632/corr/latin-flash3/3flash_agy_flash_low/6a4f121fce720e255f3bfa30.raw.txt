# Elenchus

## *Clarissimorum Virorum, qui Leobergâ orti & superius memorati sunt.*

### Philosophi & Poetæ celebriores.

(a) *M. Casparus Suevus Scholæ Leor. Rector & Instaurator.*
(b) *M. Laurentius Ludovicus Gymnasii Gorlicens. Rector.*
(c) *M. Nicolaus Gerstmannus Facult. Philosoph. in Academ. Lipsiensi Decanus.*

*Ejusdem Facult. Matricula nominat plures ex Leorinis Familiis, Reusneris, Wirthiis, aliis Magistros & Baccal. Primo Acad. illius seculo creatos.*

(d) *Nicolaus Reusnerus ICt. P. L. Com. Palat. Cæsar. in Argent. & Jenen. Acad. Antecessor.*
(e) *Elias Reusnerus Med. Lic. Hist. & Poet. in Acad. Jen. Profess.*
(f) *Hieronymus Arconatus Poeta, Sacr. Cæs. Majest. ab Ep. Bellicis.*
(g) *Valentinus Neukirch Poet. in Acad. Regiomont.*

### Medici.

(h) *Georgius Wirth. I. Med. D. Ludovici Regis Hungar. Archiater.*
*Georg. Wirth. II. Med. D. Pract. Viennæ. Georgius Wirth. III. Med. D. Caroli V. Imperat. & Philippi Hispan. Regis Medicus.*
(i) *Johannes Langius Med. D. IV. Com. & Elect. Palatin. Archiater, Autor Epist. Medicinal.*
(k) *Caspar Hoffmannus M. D. Elector. Brandenb. Archiater.*
(l) *Balthasar Gitlerus Med. D. & Prof. in Acad. Lips.*
(m) *Johannes Zeidlerus Med. Doct. & Prof. ibid.*
(n) *Georgius Bejerus Med. Doct. Duc. Sax. Archiater & Physicus Martisburg.*
(o) *Hieronymus Reusnerus Med. D. Phys. Imperial. Nördlingæ.*
*Bartholomæus Reusnerus Med. & Physicus Zittavien. Pater Bartholomæi Reusneri ICti & Ordin. Witteberg.*
(p) *Tobias Koberus Med. D. P. L. Physicus Patriæ.*
