<summary>This passage describes the ceremonial adornment and vestments used during the Feast of the Transfiguration in Constantinople. Special display protocols, including the imperial wearing of the jeweled loros and the exhibition of primary relics, were enacted specifically to impress visiting Saracen dignitaries.</summary>
