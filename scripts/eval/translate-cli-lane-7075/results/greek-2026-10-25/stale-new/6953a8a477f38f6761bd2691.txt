### LXXIX. (LXXX.)
#### To the end, concerning those who will be changed, a testimony for Asaph, a psalm concerning the Assyrian.

² Give ear, Shepherd of Israel, you who guide Joseph like sheep; you who sit upon the cherubim, shine forth! ³ Before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh, awaken your might and come to save us. ⁴ O God, restore us and make your face shine, and we shall be saved. ⁵ Lord God of hosts, how long will you be angry at the prayer of your servant? ⁶ You will feed us bread of tears, and will give us tears to drink in full measure. ⁷ You made us a dispute for our neighbors, and our enemies mocked us. ⁸ Lord God of hosts, restore us and make your face shine, and we shall be saved. <term>Diapsalma</term> <gloss>musical interlude</gloss>. ⁹ You brought a vine out of Egypt, you cast out the nations and planted it. ¹⁰ You cleared the way before it, and you planted its roots, and the earth was filled. ¹¹ Its shade covered mountains, and its climbing branches the cedars of God. ¹² It stretched out its branches to the sea, and its shoots as far as the river. ¹³ Why did you tear down its hedge, so that all who pass along the way pluck its fruit? ¹⁴ A boar from the forest ravaged it, and a solitary wild beast grazed upon it. ¹⁵ O God of hosts, return now, look down from heaven and see, and visit this vine; ¹⁶ and restore that which your right hand planted, and look upon the son of man whom you strengthened for yourself. ¹⁷ It is burned with fire and dug up; at the rebuke of your face they shall perish. ¹⁸ Let your hand be upon the man of your right hand, and upon the son of man whom you strengthened for yourself, ¹⁹ and we will never turn away from you; you will revive us, and we will call upon your name. ²⁰ Lord God of hosts, restore us and make your face shine, and we shall be saved.

### LXXX. (LXXXI.)
#### To the end, for the winepresses, a psalm for Asaph.

² Rejoice in God our helper, shout aloud to the God of Jacob. ³ Take up a psalm and bring the timbrel, the pleasant psaltery with the harp. ⁴ Blow the trumpet at the new moon, on the notable day of your festival, ⁵ for it is an ordinance for Israel, and a judgment for the God of Jacob. ⁶ He established it as a testimony in Joseph when he went out of the land of Egypt; he heard a language he did not know: ⁷ "I removed his shoulder from burdens, his hands were freed from the basket. ⁸ In tribulation you called upon me and I delivered you; I heard you in the secret place of the tempest, I tested you at the water of strife." <term>Diapsalma</term> <gloss>musical interlude</gloss>. ⁹ "Hear, my people, and I will speak to you; Israel, I testify to you. If you will listen to me, ¹⁰ there shall be no newly arisen god in you, nor shall you bow down to an alien god. ¹¹ For I am the Lord your God, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt; open wide your mouth and I will fill it. ¹² But my people did not listen to my voice, and Israel paid no heed to me. ¹³ And I gave them over to the practices of their own hearts; they will walk in their own practices. ¹⁴ If my people had listened to me, if Israel had walked in my ways, ¹⁵ I would have brought their enemies low in an instant, and laid my hand upon those who afflict them. ¹⁶ The enemies of the Lord lied to him."

<summary>Psalm 79 (80) pleads for divine restoration using the allegory of Israel as a transplanted vine ravaged by enemies, while Psalm 80 (81) summons the people to celebrate the festival, recalling God's deliverance from Egypt and urging covenant fidelity.</summary>
