->Aldus Manutius the Roman sends warm greetings to Antonio<-
->Codro Urceo.<-
<note>original: "Antonio Codro Urceo.S.P.D.", abbreviation for "salutem plurimam dicit"</note>

Most learned Codrus, we have recently gathered as many Greek letters as we could obtain, and, printed with our type, we are publishing them in two volumes. This is aside from the many letters of Basil, Gregory, and Libanius, which we keep at home to print as soon as the opportunity arises. Indeed, the authors whose letters we present number about thirty-five, as can be seen in the volumes themselves. We send these as a gift to you, who publicly teach both Latin and Greek literature in the most renowned academy of Bologna. We do this both so that you may show them to your students, so they may be all the more inspired to pursue higher learning, and so that they may serve with you as a <term>mnemosynon</term> <gloss>memorial</gloss> <note>original: "μνημόσυνον"</note> and a pledge of love from your friend Aldus. Farewell. Venice, the fifteenth day before the Kalends of May <note>April 17</note>, 1499 <note>original: "M. ID.", an unconventional Roman numeral for 1499</note>.
