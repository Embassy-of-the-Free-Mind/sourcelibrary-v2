**270** Number 953 Furtwängler; 20, 109 a (page 171) Röhl.

<note>An epigraphic transcription shows archaic Greek letters: POTEIDAN written in retrograde or irregular script.</note>

**274** Number 389 Furtwängler. Appears to be 20, 78 Röhl.

<note>An epigraphic transcription shows the Greek letters POT...AN with central damage.</note>

->Poseidon <note>original: "Ποτει[δ]άν"</note>.<->

Röhl reads nothing except **POT**. Furtwängler reads the end as **DAN**.

---

**275** Number 457 Furtwängler.

<note>A drawing of a fragment shows a hand holding a trident and a small portion of an inscription.</note>

->Poseidon <note>original: "Ποτ]ε(ιδάν)"</note>.<->

Neptune was depicted holding a trident. After the letter **B**, no trace of letters is visible, so that one must believe the painter by some chance left unfinished the name, in which he had intended to write the letters **ei** as **ΒΣ**.
