Only Claudius Pompeianus the elder never appeared, but sent his sons, while remaining away himself; for he preferred even to be killed for this rather than to behold the emperor, the son of Marcus <note>Emperor Marcus Aurelius, the father of Commodus.</note>, conducting himself in such a fashion. For among other things that we did, we would shout out whatever we were commanded, and especially these words continually: "You are lord and you are first, of all men most fortunate. Victor you are, and victor you shall be; from everlasting, <term>Amazonian</term> <gloss>an epithet Commodus took from his love for his concubine Marcia, whom he portrayed as an Amazon</gloss>, you are victor." But of the populace in general, many did not enter the amphitheater at all, and others departed after merely glancing inside, partly from shame at what was going on, partly also from fear, inasmuch as a report spread abroad that he would want to shoot a few of the spectators in imitation of Hercules and the Stymphalian birds. And this story was believed, too, because he had once gathered all the men in the city who had lost their feet as the result of disease or some accident, and then, after fastening about their knees some likenesses of serpents' bodies, and giving them sponges to throw instead of stones, had killed them with blows of a club, pretending that they were giants.
