Doubt marks. Wherever you are not sure you have read the page correctly, wrap exactly the uncertain word, number, name or letters in ⟦ and ⟧, with your best reading inside, for example ⟦word⟧. Use them for text that is faint, damaged, cut off or blotted, for text that could be read two ways, and wherever you may be writing what the text ought to say rather than what is printed. Mark only the uncertain characters or word, never whole lines, and give one reading, not alternatives. Do not mark text you read clearly. These marks are not tags: they may stand inside running text and inside any tag, and everything else about the format stays as above.
