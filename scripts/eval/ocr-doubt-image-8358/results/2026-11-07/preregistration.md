**Design and decision rule, stated before any call (#8358).**

**Test 1, doubt flags.** Pages: the 37 pages holding the 109 confirmed errors, plus 18 pages with no confirmed error from the carried books (one each, two to twelve pages away), for the flag rate on ordinary pages. Arms, all agy `gemini-3.8-flash-low`, narrow read_file agent, as `cli-ocr.mjs read` sends: **L** the live OCR prompt; **L2** L again (noise floor); **D** the live prompt plus one doubt sentence asking for `⟦…⟧` around unsure spans (outside the tag syntax). One wording, written once, not tuned. A blind Opus judge (sealed `claude -p`, image opened with Read) is given each read with the marks stripped and finds each known error spot in it: what the read says there, and whether that matches the page. The marks are then mapped back by position. For D it also checks the marked spans, mixed with an equal number of random unmarked spans (the base error rate), unlabelled. One random span per packet carries a planted one-character misread (judge control).

Measures: share of the 109 spots marked (all, carried 29, by stratum); the same among spots where D's own read is wrong (self-doubt recall); marks per 1,000 characters; share of pages with at least one mark (the gating rate); share of marks on text that matches the page, against random spans; harm = per-spot accuracy D vs L (paired), and D-vs-L text distance against L-vs-L2.

**Test 2, image at translation.** 18 pages with carried errors (18 books), 18 same-book pages with no confirmed error, and 12 of the #7611 planted-misread control pages. Source = the transcription that carries the error (the one the live English was made from, or the judged text for the flash-fill pages); stored OCR for the others. Arms, agy Flash-low, live translate prompt: **T** text only (as `cli-translate.mjs`); **I** the same plus the scan in the workspace, opened through read_file, and one sentence saying the transcription may hold misreads and to translate what the page shows where it clearly differs. Judge: blind Opus with the image, one output per call: each known spot carried/repaired/dropped, every other meaning-changing English error, and every false statement about what the leaf shows. Controls: 8 English outputs with a planted error, 2 wrong-page packets per arm, 5 repeats.

**What changes a decision.**
- Flags are a usable gate only if they mark **≥ 70% of the carried errors** AND fewer than **30% of no-error pages** carry any mark. Under 50% recall: flags are not a gate. Between: not adopted.
- Image translation is worth having only if I **repairs ≥ half of the errors T carries**, with **no more pages with new errors** than T (within the T-vs-T noise) and **at most one more page with a false leaf claim**.
- Both pass → flag-gated image translation. Image passes, flags fail → image everywhere stands on its price (tokens per page reported, input + cache_read). Image fails → neither.

**What these n can and cannot tell.** 29 carried errors on 18 pages: an observed 70% has a 95% interval of about 52–83%, so this separates "most" from "few", but not 60% from 80%, and no stratum comparison is meaningful (largest stratum 13). The harm side has 36 to 48 pages per arm, so it detects only large harm, around 4+ more pages with a false leaf claim. A null on harm is not proof of safety.
