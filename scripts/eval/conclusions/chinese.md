# Chinese: what our experiments have concluded

PRIOR ART: none fits — `scripts/eval/DECISIONS.md` is one row per route, `EXPERIMENTS.md` is a run log, newest first, and `experiments/index.json` is a machine index. None of them says what the Chinese runs add up to. This page is that summary, written for a reader who has seen none of the records (#8224).

*As of 2026-11-05. Covers the 157 experiment records that touch Chinese or Classical Chinese, plus four newer pieces: PR #8146, PR #8177, PR #8211 and issue #8169. Every number below was checked against the record it is linked to.*

## In five sentences

We are confident that the transcriptions of the Siku Quanshu manuscripts are close to the page. PaddleOCR-VL differs from an independent typed edition by a median 3.3% of characters (1,201 books), and on ordinary pages it does not make up text. We are just as confident that the English is the weak half. One Opus judge, reading 120 books against the images, found a major defect on about a third of translated pages, and on four in five pages translated by the older Gemini 3 Flash. Our readers do not appear to recall famous texts in place of reading our pages. The biggest thing we do not know is whether any route we can afford puts out English good enough to restart the stopped Siku translation lane, which has 1.3 million pages waiting. The test that should answer it (`zh-siku-route-matrix-7263`) has not reported.

## How to read this page

- **Confidence.** *Settled*: large n, a typed reference or by-eye check, and no conflicting record. *Likely*: the direction is clear, but the sample is small or the result comes from a single judge. *Weak*: a pilot, a confounded comparison, or an instrument near its floor. *Open*: no record answers it yet.
- **"Judged" is not "accurate".** Most quality numbers here come from an Opus judge reading the page image. On Chinese manuscripts that judge caught 45% of the pages a typed reference showed were bad (95% CI 29–62%, 40 pages; [judge-calibration]). So an "accepted" rate is a ceiling on quality, not a measure of it. Numbers scored against a typed edition (CER, character error rate) are accuracy. A CER difference also counts convention: two human transcriptions of the same Taishō pages differ by 2.87%, and by 0.28% once 41 standard variant-form pairs are folded ([human-ceiling]).
- **Read the bar, not only the share.** Each failure label below carries its threshold. Some thresholds are strict: "bad page" at 1% CER, or "wrong end to end", which counts a page as wrong if either the English has a major defect or the transcription has a misread.
- **By eye** means someone opened the page image. Only records that did so are labelled that way.

## Where records conflict

- **Opus against 3.8 Flash as translator.** The 2026-10-15 record said "Opus stays the hard-language translator". The 2026-11-04 record ([6729-not-opus]) shows production never did that for Chinese. We believe the measurement, that Opus reads better, and the later record's account of production. Both are right about different things.
- **How bad is 3 Flash English?** The observational strata put it at 80.9% wrong end to end. The paired test accepted 13 of 60 pages, about 22%, which is consistent. The size of the 3.8 Flash advantage is not: 8.8% wrong end to end in the strata, but only 33 of 58 accepted in the paired test. We believe the paired test, because the strata compare different books.
- **Transcription against translator as the source of error** (see the English section). The two records cover different strata, and we believe both.
- **Fresh read against correction** for print. The judge prefers fresh reads, and an outside check splits evenly. We treat it as unresolved.

## Evidence and practice disagree

1. **The default Chinese translation row is the worst-measured model.** `scripts/lib/translation-routing-table.json` row `flash-measured` routes Chinese to `gemini-3-flash-preview`. 3 Flash English is the worst stratum measured, and 3.8 Flash and Opus both measured better ([5274-strata], [6729-hardest]).
2. **Opus is not used for Chinese translation.** The measurement says Opus is best (n = 25). Production uses Gemini, for cost and quota ([6729-not-opus]).
3. **Print OCR uses Paddle, though Flash is more accurate.** This is a deliberate $0 choice, costing about 0.6 CER points ([6101-split]).
4. **The bulk Opus correction of print runs although its pilot missed its bar** (51/60 accepted and 6 wrong-text pages, where the bar allowed 4). It runs on the owner's decision of 2026-11-04 ([7263-gemini-reocr], PR #8067 open).
5. **The Siku translation lane is stopped, with 1,304,479 pages waiting**, and no route has yet been measured as good enough to restart it (#7914).
6. **The "accepted" rates that drive routing come from a judge with 0.45 sensitivity on Chinese manuscripts** ([judge-calibration]). The router reads them as quality.

## Records left undecided, and why

- [7263-gemini-reocr] (`2026-11-03-zh-gemini-reocr-cell-7263`): the print half was released on 2026-11-04. The Siku half (93,277 pages, about $258–534 of subscription quota at list price) is still owed, and it is the owner's decision.
- [5122] (`2026-10-26-pre-read-page-classifier-5122`): taking it means writing a new `pages.page_class` field to production data, which needs the owner.
