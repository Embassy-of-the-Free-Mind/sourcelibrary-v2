OCR Evidence Ledger

Which engine reads which kind of page, and how much each claim can carry. Every cell is graded by its number of books with a ground-truth reference, one page per book. A routing or withholding decision should cite a decision-grade cell, or say it is a judgement call.

How to read this
  • Error is character error rate against a ground-truth reference (0.010 = one character in a hundred wrong). The bar is the 95 % interval on the median, bootstrapped over books. Cells with no reference show only a proxy figure, the distance from the production engine's own reading, which cannot show that engine's errors and is never graded.
  • Exploratory: under 30 referenced books, or none. Directional: 30 to 49. Decision-grade: 50 or more. The head-to-head against production is graded on its own count of untied pairs: about 47 are needed to see a 70/30 split with 80 % power, about 85 for 65/35.
  • Placed is coverage: the pages the engine's output could be aligned to the reference at all. An engine that reads well but only places half the pages is a half-lane.
  • Loop and catastrophic (error above 0.5) are rates. A rate needs about 150 books for plus or minus 5 points, so nearly every rate here is an anecdote with an interval on it.
  • Still needed is the number of further referenced books before the cell is decision-grade.
