THE 153d RADICAL. 豸

**豹** *p'aou* (1) The name of one of the chiefs of the Shuh-sun clan. IX. ii. 8; iii. 7; iv. 2; v. 3; xiv. 3; et sæpe. His death is mentioned in X. iv. 8. (2) A viscount of Hoo. XI. xv. 3.

**貉** *mih* 厥貉,—see 厥.

**貍** *le* 貍脤,—see 脤.

**貜** *këoh* (1) The name of one of the chiefs of the Chung-sun clan. X. ix. 4; x. 3; xi. 6; xxiv. 1. (2) 貜且,—see 且.

THE 154th RADICAL. 貝

**貞** *ching* The name of a prince of Ts'oo. IX. v. 10; vii 8; x. 3, 8, 10; xii. 5; xiv. 6.

**負** *foo* 負芻,—see 芻.

**貫** *kwan* A city of Sung,—in the pres. dis. of Ts'aou, dep. Ts'aou-chow. V. ii. 4. But this identification proceeds on the supposition of 貫's being for 貰, in which case the pronunciation should be different.

**買** *mae* The name:—1st, of a prince of Loo, a son of duke Chwang, V. xxviii 2; 2d, of a great officer of Wei, IX. xvii. 3; xviii. 2; 3d, of a baron of Heu, X. xix. 2; 4th, of a great officer of Ch'in. XII. xiv. 14.

**費** *pe* (1) The name of an earl of Ch'ing. VIII vi. 7. (2) A city of Loo,—in the pres. dis. of Pe, dep. E-chow. It was the principal city of the Ke-sun clan. IX. vii. 4: X. xiii. 1: XI. xii. 5.

**賦** *foo* To levy or collect taxes. 田賦, a certain contribution levied for military purposes from the land in Loo. XII. xii. 1.

**賜** *tsze* To give to, to confer on. VIII. viii. 7.

**賴** *lae* A small State, whose lords were viscounts, surname unknown,—in pres. dis. of Shang-shing, Kwang Chow, Ho-nan. It was extinguished by Ts'oo, in X. iv. 6.

**賵** *fung* Presents for the burial of the dead;—specially of carriages and horses. I. i. 4: VI. v. 1.

**賻** *foo* Presents or contributions of money for the burial of the dead. I. iii. 4.

---

THE 155th RADICAL. 赤

**赤** *ch'ih* (1) Red. In the name 赤狄,—see 狄. (2) The name:—1st, of a prince, perhaps an earl, of Ts'aou, III. xxiv. 8; 2d, of a viscount of the Jung-man, XII. iv. 6. (3) 赤棘,—see 棘.

---

THE 156th RADICAL. 走

**起** *k'e* The name of a minister of Tsin. X. ii. 1; xi. 7.

**越** *yueh* (1) The name of a city in Wei, or, acc. to others, in Ts'aou. If not identical with 垂, it was near it;—see 垂. II. i. 4.
