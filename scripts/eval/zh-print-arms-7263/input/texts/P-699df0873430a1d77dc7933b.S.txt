<header>軍營雜氣占</header>

<image-desc>A detailed color illustration of a military encampment set against a backdrop of jagged, blue-tinted mountains. The camp features several structures, including a prominent building with a tiled roof and various red banners or pennants on poles. In the sky above the camp, a long, horizontal, greyish-white cloud formation stretches across, tapering at both ends.</image-desc>

朱文公曰

新出軍行師占雲逆可也而
順可擊

宋志曰

新出軍行師有雲從後來謂
如東行日雲從西來因隨而擊
之勝若赤雲從東來則我軍者
敵軍已行我軍當敗宜也兵固
守餘方倣此以類而推之

<header>吉凶氣占</header>

<image-desc>A color illustration of a fortified city gate or palace structure with multi-tiered roofs and red walls, surrounded by trees and mountains. Above the fortification, a distinct red, crescent or boat-shaped cloud floats, with a thinner grey cloud formation appearing above it.</image-desc>

朱文公曰

五色氤氳方蕭索輪囷是謂
慶世太平之應

宋志曰

凡雲紫赤色及若烟非烟若雲
非雲郁、紛、蕭索輪囷是謂
慶亦曰景雲喜氣太平之應見
則國有慶又曰雲含五色潤澤和
緩見於城上為景雲非氣非烟
五色氤氳為慶 卿雲又五色
為慶雲三色為矞雲外赤内青
亦為矞太平之應
