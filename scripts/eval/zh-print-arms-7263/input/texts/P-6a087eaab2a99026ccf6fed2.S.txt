<header>INDEX III. CHINESE CHARACTERS AND PHRASES.</header>

土
*too*
Roots, or the bark about the roots. I. xv. II. 2.

在
*tsae*
*Passim*. To be present. II. iii. III. 6. But generally it = to be in or on, followed by a noun. Sometimes it is simply the preposition, in, on, at;—as in III. i. II. 4. **在上**, to be on high, *i. e.*, in heaven III. i. I. 1; II. 1. **在下**, to be below,—on the earth. III. i. II. 4. **在中**, to be in the midst of,—within. III. i. V. 2. **在位**, in offices; **在服**, in the conduct of affairs. III. iii. I. 2. **在御**, = at one's side. I. vii. VIII. 2. **在公** = public duties. IV. i. [ii.] I. **在武丁孫子**, it rests in the person of Woo-ting's descendant. IV. iii. III. Sometimes the **在** is followed by the preposition **于**. II. iii. X. I, 2; *et al*.

圭
*kwei*
A rank-token of jade. I. v. I. 3: III. ii. VIII. 6; X. 6; iii. II. 5; IV. 1 (as used in sacrifice); V. 5; VII. 2; VIII. 5 (**圭瓚**, —see **瓚**).

地
*te*
The ground. II. ii. V. 9. The earth. II. iv. VIII. 6.

均
*keun*
To be equally adjusted. II. i. III. 5. To be fair. II. vi. I. 2. = to hit, to be equally matched. III. ii. II. 3. = a balance, that which adjusts. II. iv. VII. 3.

坎
*k'an*
**坎坎**, descriptive of the sound of an axe on a tree. I. ix. VII. 1—3. Of the sound of drums. II. i. V. 3. So the character singly, of blows on musical instruments. I. xii. I. 2, 3.

坐
*tso*
To sit. I. xi. I. 2, 3. = seats. II. vii. VI. 3.

垌
*keung*
Tracts of a State, far from the capital. IV. ii. I. 1—4.

坻
*ch'e*
An islet. I. xi. IV. 2: II vi. VII. 4.

坼
*ch'ih*
To split, to be rent. III. ii. I. 2.

垂
*shuy*
To drop, to let hang down. I. v. VI. 1, 2; viii. X. 2: II. viii. I. 4, 5.

垝
*kwei*
Ruinous. I. v. VI. 2.

垣
*yuen*
A wall. I. v. VI. 2: II. iii. VII. 2; v. III. 8: III. i. X. 4; ii. X. 7.

垤
*t'ëeh*
An ant-hill. I. xv. III. 3.

垢
*kow*
Filth. **中垢**, one's inward filthiness. III. iii. III. 12.

城
*shing*
A wall of a city; to build such a wall, = to fortify. I. i. VII. 1; iii. VI. 1; XVII. 1; iv. IX. 3; vii. XVII. 3: II. i. VIII. 3: III. i. X. 3; ii. X. 7; iii. VI. 7; VII. 6; X. 3.

<column-break/>

域
*yih*
(1) A boundary. IV. iii. III. (2) A place of tombs. I. x. XI. 2. Perhaps the meaning here is the enclosure or border of such a place.

埤
*pe*
Increasingly. I. iii. XV. 2, 3.

執
*chih*
To hold, to grasp. I. iii. VI. 4; XIII. 3; v. VIII. 2; vi. III. 1, 2; vii. IV. 1; VII. 1, 2 (**摻執**); XV. 1, 2; *et al*. To take in hand, to attend to. I. xv. I. 7: II. vi. V. 3: III. i. VII. 8: IV. iii. I. = to take. III. ii. VI 4. **執競武王**, full of strength was king Woo. IV. i. [i.] IX.

場
*yih*
The smaller divisions of fields; to make those divisions. II. vi. VI. 3, 4; III. ii. VI. 1.

基
*ke*
A foundation. II. ii. VII. 1: VII. iii. II. 9. The foot of stairs. IV. i. [ii.] VII. To be established. III. ii. VI. 6. To enlarge a foundation. IV. i. [i.] VI.

掃
*saou*
To brush; to sweep away. I. iv. II. 1; x. II. 2; xv. III. 3: II. i. V. 2: III. iii. II. 4.

堂
*t'ang*
(1) A hall; the principal apartment, to which you ascend by steps. I. vii. XIV. 2; viii. III. 3; x. I. 1—3; xv. I. 8: IV. i. [ii.] VII. (2) An open glade in a hill. I. xi. V. 2. (3) Name of a town in Wei. I. iv. VI. 2.

堅
*këen*
To be hard; strong. II. vi. VIII. 2: III. ii. I. 5; II. 3.

堪
*k'an*
To be equal to, to be able to bear. IV. i. [iii.] II.; IV.

報
*paou*
To respond to, to recompense. I. iii. IV. 2, 4; v. X. 1—3; vii. VIII. 3: II. v. VIII. 4; vi. V. 2; VI. 6; VII. 4. To be recompensed. III. iii. II. 6. **報章**, a work of recompense. II. v. IX. 6.

場
*ch'ang*
A stack-site. I. xv. I. 7, 8; III. 2: II. v. II. 4. But as the ground for the stacks was cultivated with vegetables, when not required for the other purpose, we find the character meaning a vegetable garden in II. iv. II. 1, 2.

堵
*too*
Fifty cubits' length of wall. II. iii. VII. 2; iv. V. 2: III. i. III. 6.

塒
*she*
Fowl-holes in a wall. I. vi. II. 1.

塗
*t'oo*
Mire. II. i. VIII. 4; vii. IX. 6.

塞
*sih*
(1) To stop up. I. xv. I. 5. (2) To be sincere; sincerely. III. iii. II. 4; iv. VI. 3: III. iii. IX. 6.

填
*t'ëen*
(1) To fill up. III. iii. III. 1. (2) For a long time. III. iii. X. 1; xi. X. 3. (3) Used for **瘨**, to be in distress. II. v. II. 5.

塵
*ch'in*
Dust. II. vi. II. 2, 3. **自塵**, in l. 1, = to raise a cloud of dust about one's self.

墉
*yung*
A wall. I. ii. VI. 3: IV. i. [iii.] VI. Fortified walls. III. ii. VII. 7, 8. To wall. III. iii. VII. 6.

壑
*k'o*
(1) To collect, to gather. I. ii. IX. 3. (2) To rest. III. v. V. 4; VII. 3. (3) = to be angry with. III. x. 6.
