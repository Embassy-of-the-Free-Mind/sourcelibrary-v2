<header>軍營雜氣占</header>
<warning>Handwritten Chinese regular script (kaishu)</warning>

<image-desc size="large" type="diagram" significance="high">A hand-colored illustration of a military camp set against blue and green mountains, with grey-roofed buildings, a red vapor shape and several weapons with red tassels. In the sky above the camp, a long, horizontal grey and white cloud stretches across the scene.</image-desc>

朱文公曰

新出軍行師占雲逆可屯而
順可擊

宋志曰

新出軍行師有雲從後来謂
如東行日雲從西来因隨而擊
之勝若赤雲從東来則我軍者
敵軍已行我軍當敗宜屯兵固
守餘方倣此以類而推之

<header>吉凶氣占</header>

<image-desc size="large" type="diagram" significance="high">A hand-colored illustration of a two-storey building with grey roofs and red pillars set into a hillside, with trees and rocky slopes before it. In the sky, a long red-tipped streak and a long dark grey streak run across the scene above a patch of yellow haze.</image-desc>

朱文公曰

五色氤氳兮蕭索輪囷是謂
慶世太平之應

宋志曰

凡雲紫赤色及若烟非烟若雲
非雲郁郁紛紛蕭索輪囷是謂
慶亦曰景雲喜氣太平之應見
則國有慶又曰雲含五色潤澤和
緩見於城上為景雲非氣非烟
五色氤氳為慶<unclear>西</unclear>卿雲又五色
為慶雲三色為矞雲外赤内青
亦為矞太平之應
