Veorr , &c.—The story of Thor's engagement
with the Great Serpent, is told differently in the
Edda of Snorro, which was written, or collated
rather, 180 years after the Edda of Sæmund, As
it may not be unentertaining to compare the two
together, it shall here be related:

Thor resolved to attack the serpent if an
opportunity offered: with this view he set out from
Asgard under the form of a young boy, in order to
go to the giant Hymer. When he got there, he
besought the giant to permit him to go on board
his bark with him when he went a fishing. The
giant answered, that a little puny stripling like
him, could be of no use to him, but would be
ready to die of cold when they should reach the
high seas, whither he usually went. Thor answered
him, that he feared nothing; and asked him what
