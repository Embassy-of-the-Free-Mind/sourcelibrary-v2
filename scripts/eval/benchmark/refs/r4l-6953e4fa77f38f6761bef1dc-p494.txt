Die Veneris Feb. 25. St. vet. Hor. 8 \tfrac{1}{2} P.M. Cometæ in p existentis distantia
à stella E erat major quàm \tfrac{3}{13} AE, minor quàm \tfrac{1}{5} AE, adeoque æqualis \tfrac{3}{14} AE
proximè; & angulus ApE nonnihil obtusus erat, sed fere rectus. Nempe si
demitteretur ad pE perpendiculum ab A, distantia Cometæ à perpendiculo illo
erat \tfrac{1}{5} pE.

Eadem nocte, horâ 9 \tfrac{1}{2} , Cometæ in P existentis distantia à stella E erat
major quàm \tfrac{1}{4\tfrac{1}{2 AE, minor quàm \tfrac{1}{5\tfrac{1}{4 AE, adeoque æqualis \tfrac{1}{4\tfrac{7}{8 AE, seu \tfrac{8}{39} AE
quamproximè. A perpendiculo autem à Stella A ad rectam PE demisso distantia
Cometæ erat \tfrac{4}{5} PE.

Die ♂ tis , Mart. 1, hor. 11. P.M. Cometa in R existens, stellis K & C accuratè
interjacebat, & rectæ CRK pars CR paulo major erat quàm \tfrac{1}{3} CK, & paulo
minor quam \tfrac{1}{3} CK+ \tfrac{1}{8} CR, adeoque æqualis \tfrac{1}{3} CK+ \tfrac{1}{16} CR seu \tfrac{16}{45} CK.
