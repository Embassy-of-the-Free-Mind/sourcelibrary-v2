 CHAP. XIX. 
 
 The Second Peace, made betwizt Charles Emanuel and the Vaudois, by the mediation of the Protestant Cantons, in the month of February 1664. which has continued till the year 1686. during which time, the Vaudois did signal Service to the Duke of Savoy.
 
 
 THE War of the year 1663. having had as ill Success as that of the year 1655. the Duke of Savoy would have been glad of Peace, but he durst not ask it of the Vaudois, for fear it should shew his weakness, or at least he should be obliged to grant them more than he had done by his former Patent, granted at Pignerol; because of the Advantages they had gained over him: For this Prince had drained his Revenues, ruined by these Wars a part of his Dukedom, lost more than 4000 men, and the Vaudois but sixty: They durst go no more into the Mountains to seek
