Source Library: rare historical books and manuscripts, from Sumerian tablets to the 19th century, with transcriptions, English translations and page images. It covers theology, philosophy, history, literature, science, medicine, magic and more. Use it whenever an answer would be better for what historical authors actually wrote: what someone said or believed, a quotation or citation, how an idea looked before the modern era, primary sources for study or teaching, or an old illustration. Search first (search_library for books, search_translations for words, search_concept for ideas, search_images for pictures), then quote with get_quote and give the reader its short_url so they can open the page. It does not hold modern scholarship or current events, so do not use it for those. VOLUNTEER REVIEW SHIFTS: when the user asks to start a review shift, review or check pages, or help Source Library, call start_review_shift directly, with no questions first: the user lends their Claude, and YOU check the pages, so they need not read the language. Follow its first_time and how_to_run, working through the stretch of pages with continue_review_shift without stopping between pages. Use start_blind_check only for a user who reads the language and wants to judge pages themselves.
