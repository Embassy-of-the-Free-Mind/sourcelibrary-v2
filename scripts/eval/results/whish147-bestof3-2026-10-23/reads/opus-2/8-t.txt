NOTE | The leaf has 9 rows. Rows 1–2 and 8–9 run the full width; rows 3–7 break at both holes. Treat every row as a tentative reading. At this image resolution I couldn't tell apart look-alike letters (ര/ാ, ന/ണ, ത്ര/ദ്ര/ശ്ര, ഖ/ഷ, ജ്ഞ/ഞ്ഞ/ശ്ച) or reliably separate conjuncts and dot-reph. I guessed many letters, so this is not a dependable transcription. Only a few words seem fairly secure: ലിംഗം and ഭിന്നം (row 1), സിദ്ധി (row 5), സംസാര (row 9) and the ending ലയഃ (row 8). Row 5 has a form like ।ണ്ഡ। that may be a verse number. Higher-resolution images would be needed for a reliable reading.
