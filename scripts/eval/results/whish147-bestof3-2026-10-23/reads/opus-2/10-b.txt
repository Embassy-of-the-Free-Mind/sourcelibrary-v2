NOTE | I could read rows 1–3 fairly well; they are the closing kārikās, 70–72. Row 4 is partly legible: the end of the verse and the start of the colophon. Rows 5–9 are a cursive colophon (a date by śaka letter-numbers, place and scribe names), and my reading there is a low-confidence copy of letter shapes and may be wrong in many places. I joined the overlaps by eye and only roughly. On rows 1, 2, 8 and 9 the text runs past the string, so I placed ‖ about where the string falls.
