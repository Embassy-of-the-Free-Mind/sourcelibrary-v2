ROW 9 | നാമാലൊചനമാ‖ത്രമിഷ്യതെവൃത്തിഃവചനാദാനവി‖ഹരണൊത്സൎഗ്ഗാനന്ദാശ്
NOTE | All 9 rows have ink; the leaf covers kārikās 23–28. The [illegible] spots are verse numbers or dandas, read as "ചെഷ്ടു!" in row 1, "ഡ്യൂഃ" in row 3, two marks like "ഃ" in row 5, and a squiggle after "ശ്ച" in rows 6/8. Many glyphs were ambiguous (especially in rows 1–4). Once I recognised the passage, it guided how I resolved them, so treat individual akṣaras, chillus and vowel signs as lower confidence than the overall text.
