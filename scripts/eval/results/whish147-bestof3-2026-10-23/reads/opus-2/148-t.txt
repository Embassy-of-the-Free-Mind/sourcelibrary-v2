NOTE | My confidence in this reading is low. I could make out all 8 rows letter by letter. Rows 1–3 and 7–8 run the full width; rows 4–6 are split into columns. Several letters can't be told apart at this resolution (ഷ/ഥ, ട/ഭ, ഞ/ഞ്ഞ, ത്ര/ക്ഷ), and the words formed by the left-column letters especially are guesses. I put ‖ where the pieces join, which is where the holes are. Text that appeared twice in an overlap is written once.
