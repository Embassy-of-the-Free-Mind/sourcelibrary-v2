NOTE | This is a weak reading, roughly 50–60%. The large letter forms are clear, but many conjuncts and vowel signs are guesses, especially in rows 2–4 and 8. The text is not checked against the Kārikā or any commentary. I read the leaf as having 8 rows: rows 1–2 and 7–8 run the full width with no break at the holes, and only rows 3–6 split into the three columns.
