NOTE | I opened all four pieces and found 9 rows. This transcription is low-confidence: I read many glyphs by shape alone, and conjuncts, vowel signs and reph marks are often unsure, so treat whole rows as tentative rather than letter-exact. Several readings (for example ക്ഷ്മി and ഖ) may be misidentifications. I joined p02 and p03 as one middle column and removed the overlap. The string crosses rows 1, 8 and 9 too, so I put ‖ there as well.
