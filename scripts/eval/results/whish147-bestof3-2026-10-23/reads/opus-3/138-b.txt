NOTE | The leaf has 9 rows and I'm not confident in most of this reading. About half the akṣaras are secure: the Nyāya terms (പ്രതിയോഗി, സമവായികാരണ, പ്രാഗഭാവഃ, അത്യന്താഭാവ/അന്യോന്യാഭാവയോ, സംയോഗസംബന്ധോ) and parts of rows 7–9. Many joins across the holes and the conjuncts in rows 1 and 3–6 are guesses. In row 9, വ and പ are hard to tell apart, so "ഘടവതി" could also be "ഘടപതി".
