ROW 1 | ഒചിതന്ത്രക്കസംഗ്രഹഃ‖രക്കസംഗ്രഹസ്സമാ[illegible]ശ്രീവെദവ്യാസാ‖യനമഃശ്രീഗുരവെനമഃ
NOTE | The leaf has a single written line along the top, in a thin later hand, and the rest of the leaf is blank apart from a "Royal Asiatic Society" stamp. The first letter (ഒ / ഷെ?) and the conjunct after "സമാ" (perhaps -പ്തഃ) are uncertain. "ക്കസംഗ്രഹ" really does appear twice, in p01 and p02 outside their overlap. The ‖ marks show where the holes fall under this single line, which does not actually break.
