NOTE | I could make out roughly 50–60% of the glyphs, and many of those readings are low-confidence, especially the ligatures and vowel signs. I counted 10 rows: rows 1–3 and 8–10 run the full width, and rows 4–7 are shortened beside the holes. Where neighbouring pieces overlapped I joined them at the shared letters. In row 1, some phrases look like they could be Sāṅkhyakārikā 1, but I wrote only what the strokes show, so treat this as a rough draft rather than a reliable text.
