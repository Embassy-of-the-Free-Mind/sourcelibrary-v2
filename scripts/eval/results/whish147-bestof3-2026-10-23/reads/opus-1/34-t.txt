NOTE | I could make out all ten rows and their layout, but my readings of the individual letters are very uncertain, probably wrong in many places. At the holes, the ‖ marks show where the pieces join, not exact letter breaks. Rows 1, 3 and 4 have gaps the scribe seems to have left; I kept the ones in rows 1 and 3. Treat this only as a rough first draft that needs checking against the original by a reader of Malayalam script.
