February 6. A committee was appointed for considering of proper questions to
be inquired of in the remotest parts of the world. It consi-led of the lord viscount
BROUNCRER, Sir ROBERT MORAY, Mr. COVENTRY, Dr. PETTY, Dr. GODDARD,
Mr. HENSHAW, Dr. WHISTLER, Mr. ROORE, Mr. BOYLE, Mr. WILKINS, Mr.
POVEY, Mr. EVELYN, Dr. CLARXE, Mr. AUSTEN, Mr. OLDENRURG, and Mr.
COLWALL. They were to meet on the Monday following at Gresham College.
It was then directed to be inquired, whether Dr. WALLIS, Dr. WILLIS, Dr.
FRAZIER, Dr. BATHURST, Dr. FINCH, Mr. SMITH, Mr. JONES, Mr. VAUCHAN,
Mt. NEWBURCE, and Dr. BAYNES desired to be of the society.
