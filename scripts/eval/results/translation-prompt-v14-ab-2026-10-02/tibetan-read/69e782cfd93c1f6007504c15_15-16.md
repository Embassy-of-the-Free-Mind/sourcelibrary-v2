The channel-petals of the six chakras branch out from the two channels of the Sun and Moon in their respective locations, like branches of a tree. From those, they further divide into many subtle channel-petals, totaling seventy-two thousand. The central <term>Avadhuti</term> channel abides in the center of the wheels like a hub, without channel-petals. However, it is said that from the <term>ushnisha</term> it branches into many petals like the shape of a parasol, pervading the entire body. 
Regarding how the winds move through those channels: simultaneously with the formation of the ten heart channels, the petals in the East and South-East, and those above and below, move in sequence: the "equally-abiding," the "upward-moving," the "pervading," the "naga," the "tortoise," the "lizard," the "Devadatta," the "Dhananjaya," and the "life-sustaining" and "downward-clearing" winds.

[W] The winds move in this way: the ten channels and ten winds cause all seventy-two thousand channels to be pervaded. Furthermore, from the left channel and left nostril, the winds of space, wind, fire, water, and earth move through the process of generation. From the right channel and right nostril, the winds of earth, water, fire, wind, and space move through the process of absorption. 
Their colors are also: green for space, black for wind, red for fire, white for water, and yellow for earth. 
Regarding the path and measure of their flow: the earth wind flows from the lower part of both nostrils to a distance of twelve finger-widths externally, and four finger-widths below the navel internally. The water wind flows from the side of the nasal septum thirteen finger-widths externally, and three finger-widths below the navel internally. The fire wind flows from the outer side of the nose fourteen finger-widths externally, and two finger-widths below the navel internally. The wind wind flows from the upper part of the nose fifteen finger-widths externally, and turns back internally from the navel <note>original: "pema" meaning lotus</note> itself. The wisdom wind flows at the end of the earth mandala...

From the right channel and right nostril, the winds of earth, water, fire, wind, and space move in a dissolving sequence <note>original: "sdud rim"</note>. Their colors are green for space, black for wind, red for fire, white for water, and yellow for earth. [Y] The winds flow; in that way, the ten channels and ten winds pervade all seventy-two thousand channels. From the right channel and right nostril, the winds of earth, water, fire, wind, and ether flow in a gathering sequence. [Z] The movement of the winds is as follows: In that way, the ten channels and ten winds cause all seventy-two thousand channels to be pervaded. Furthermore, from the left channel and left nostril, the winds of space, wind, fire, water, and earth move through the stages of generation. From the right channel and right nostril, the winds of earth, water, fire, wind, and space move through the stages of dissolution.

[W] ...the mandalas are these. According to that, during the sixty <term>chutsoe</term> <gloss>60-minute Tibetan hours</gloss> of a single day, there are twenty-one thousand six hundred inhalations and exhalations. For each of the twelve petals of the navel channel, there is a "great shift" or one <term>dujor</term> <gloss>zodiacal timing</gloss> consisting of one thousand eight hundred breaths. Since it flows for five <term>chutsoe</term> each, there are twelve shifts. 
When it moves through the six left channel-petals, the wind flows from the left nostril. When it moves through the six right channel-petals, the wind flows from the right nostril; this occurs by way of shifting between left and right for each <term>dujor</term>. In the transition between shifting from left to right and right to left, fifty-six and a quarter breaths move within the central <term>Avadhuti</term> channel. At that time, the wind flows equally through both nostrils. The central channel breaths for one full day total six hundred and seventy-five. 
As stated in the <term>Abbreviated Empowerment Tantra</term> <note>original: "Wang Dor-ten"</note>: At the precise transition when the <term>dujor</term> arises, it flows equally from the central channel; fifty-six with a quarter. In short, O Lord of Men, of the seventy-two thousand channels of inhalation and exhalation, six hundred and seventy-five extra occur during the night.

[X] ...is called "the mandalas." As it is said, during the sixty <term>chu tshod</term> <gloss>measures of time; Tibetan hours</gloss> of one day, there are twenty-one thousand six hundred movements of inhalation and exhalation. For each of the twelve petals of the navel channel, there is one "great shift" <note>transpiration</note> or "zodiacal conjunction" <term>dus sbyor</term> <gloss>astrological period</gloss> consisting of one thousand eight hundred breaths. Moving for five <term>chu tshod</term> each, these become the twelve shifts. Furthermore, when moving through the six left channel-petals, the wind moves through the left nostril; when moving through the six right channel-petals, the wind moves through the right nostril. This is how the shifts alternate between left and right for each zodiacal conjunction. In this way, at the transition of the shift from left to right and right to left, fifty-six and a quarter inhalations and exhalations move within the central <term>Avadhuti</term> channel. At that time, the wind moves equally through both nostrils. In one day, the breaths of the central channel total six hundred and seventy-five. As stated in the *Brief Introduction to the Empowerment*: "At the precise junction where the zodiacal conjunctions arise, [the wind] flows evenly from the central channel. Together with the 'feet' <note>fractions</note>, it is fifty-six." In short, regarding the seventy-two thousand channels: "The lord of humans, through inhalation and exhalation, [has] six hundred plus seventy-five more [breaths] during the day and night." Thus it is said.

[Y] ...at the mandalas. As it is said: during the sixty <term>chu tshod</term> <gloss>measures of time; hours</gloss> of a single day, there are twenty-one thousand six hundred flows of inhalation and exhalation. For each of the twelve petals of the navel channel-wheel, there is one "great shift" or "conjunction," consisting of one thousand eight hundred breaths. Since each flows for five <term>chu tshod</term>, this results in twelve shifts. Furthermore, when flowing through the six left channel-petals, the wind flows from the left nostril. When flowing through the six right channel-petals, the wind flows from the right nostril. This occurs through the manner of shifting between left and right for each conjunction. In the interval of shifting from left to right and right to left, fifty-six and a quarter inhalations and exhalations flow within the central channel. At that time, the wind flows equally through both nostrils. In one day, the breaths of the central channel amount to six hundred and seventy-five. As stated in the <term>Sekoddesha</term> <gloss>Summary of the Initiation</gloss>: "At the clear interval when the conjunction arises, it flows evenly from the central channel: fifty-six with a quarter." In brief, regarding the seventy-two thousand channels: "Lord of Humanity, the inhalations and exhalations are six hundred plus seventy-five in a day and night."

[Z] ...is called the "mandalas." As it is said, in the time of the sixty <term>chu tshod</term> <gloss>water-measures/hours</gloss> of a single day, there are twenty-one thousand six hundred movements of inhalation and exhalation. At each of the twelve petals of the navel channel-wheel, there is a "great shift" or one <term>dus sbyor</term> <gloss>astrological conjunction</gloss> consisting of one thousand eight hundred breaths. Since the winds move for five <term>chu tshod</term> each, there are twelve such shifts.
Furthermore, when moving through the six left channel-petals, the wind moves through the left nostril. When moving through the six right channel-petals, the wind moves through the right nostril. This occurs by means of shifting between left and right at each conjunction. At the junction of shifting from left to right and right to left, fifty-six and a quarter inhalations and exhalations move within the central <term>Avadhuti</term> channel. At that time, the wind moves equally through both nostrils. In one full day, the breaths in the central channel total six hundred and seventy-five.
As taught in the *Condensed Tantra of Empowerment*:
At the clear junction where the conjunctions arise,
The flow is equal through the central channel.
Fifty-six breaths along with a quarter portion.
In brief, for the lord of humanity, through the seventy-two thousand channels,
Adding seventy-five,
To the six hundred itself, during the day and night.
So it is said.
