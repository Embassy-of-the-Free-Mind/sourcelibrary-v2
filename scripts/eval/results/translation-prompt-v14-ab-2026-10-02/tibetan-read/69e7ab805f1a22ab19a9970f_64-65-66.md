"Childish beings" are those in whom the Path of the Aryas has not arisen. Their "sign" is in accordance with reasoning. These phenomena, as they appear, are "existent." According to reality and reasoning, they are "non-existent." That which is further to be abandoned is the basis for total affliction. Ignorance is the very object of the first conceptualization of the apprehended. It acts in accordance with total affliction. It "acts" in the sense of depositing, just as one deposits seeds for cleaning. Likewise, according to the object of the second conceptualization of the apprehended, which is the basis for the remedy to be adopted, it is deposited in accordance with conceptualization. 

Having thus established the conceptualizations of the apprehended, and beginning from these two, the teaching on meditating on them together with the objects of attachment as false and illusion-like is given by saying "knowledge and ignorance" and so forth. These two, knowledge and ignorance, are phenomena in which form and so forth are non-existent because they are not born in reality. Childish beings conceptualize "knowledge" and "ignorance" by the power of manifest pride. Any Bodhisattva who thoroughly knows through the sequence of hearing and so forth will "definitely emerge." By entering correctly from the bonds of error, he definitely emerges. Following the two conceptualizations of the apprehended, in order to show the first conceptualization of the apprehender, it says "Who" and so forth. Any Bodhisattva who apprehends the five aggregates as having a nature of substantially existent persons, through the power of reasoning, sees the aggregates as an illusion or a delusion.
