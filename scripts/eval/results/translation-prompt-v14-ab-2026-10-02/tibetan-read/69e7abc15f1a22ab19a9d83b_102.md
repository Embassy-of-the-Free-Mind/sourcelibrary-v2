The setting was in Shravasti. At that time, the Venerable Nyede <note>likely Upananda</note> was traveling through the countryside to guard the regions and came to a mountain hermitage. # The Sutra of the Lotus-Colored

The setting was in Shravasti. At that time, the Venerable Upasena was traveling through the countryside to provide protection and arrived at a mountain retreat. At that time, in that mountain retreat, there lived a householder who was wealthy, possessed much property, and had great enjoyments. His holdings were vast and extensive; he possessed wealth like that of the Son of Shravana <note>Vaishravana/Kuvera, the god of wealth</note> and rivaled the riches of the Son of Shravana.

[Z] —"May I please that Buddha, the Blessed One, who was the Brahmin youth Uttara <note>original: "bla ma"</note> predicted <note>by Buddha Kashyapa</note>. May I not displease him. Having been ordained in his teaching and having abandoned all afflictions, may I manifest Arhatship. Just as my female preceptor <term>upadhyayi</term> <gloss>mkhan mo</gloss> was declared by the perfectly and completely enlightened Buddha Kashyapa to be the foremost of those who have initiated perseverance, so may I also be declared by Shakyamuni, the King of the Shakyas, to be the foremost of those who have initiated perseverance," so she prayed. Monks, I have attained a state perfectly equal to, with equal power, equal deeds, and equal methods as the perfectly and completely enlightened Buddha Kashyapa. Thus, she has pleased me and not displeased me. By being ordained in my own teaching and abandoning all afflictions, she has manifested Arhatship, and now I have declared her the foremost of those who have initiated perseverance.

Regarding the one called "Lotus-Colored" <note>original: "padma'i mdog"</note>: The setting was in Shravasti. At that time, the venerable Upananda <note>original: "nye sde"</note> was traveling through the countryside of Janapada <note>original: "yul srung byed"</note> and arrived at a mountain retreat. At that time, in that mountain retreat, there was a householder who was wealthy, possessed much property and great enjoyments, had vast and extensive possessions, and was endowed with wealth like that of the Son of Shravana <note>Vaisravana/Kubera, the god of wealth</note>, even rivaling the wealth of the Son of Shravana.

'—may such a precious person be born, and may I please him! May I not displease him!' The other girl made this prayer: 'May I be your sibling wherever you are born. By this root of virtue, wherever these two are born, may I become their hairdresser and attendant, and may I please their precious son! May I not displease him!' Thus she made such a prayer.
