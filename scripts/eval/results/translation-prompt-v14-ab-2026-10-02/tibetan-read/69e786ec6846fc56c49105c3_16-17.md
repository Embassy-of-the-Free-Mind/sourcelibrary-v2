[W] From the <term>dharmadhatu</term> <gloss>sphere of reality</gloss>, emanate the assembly of the mandala gates. For the supreme principal figure, <term>Chechok</term> <gloss>Supreme Glory</gloss> in union of father and mother, emanate the ten subduers of the arrogant directional protectors. From <term>Vajra Heruka</term> <gloss>Diamond Blood-Drinker</gloss> in union, emanate the eight great <term>Gauri</term> goddesses. From <term>Yamantaka</term> <gloss>Manjushri Lord of Death</gloss> in union, emanate the twelve directional protectors with their retinues. From <term>Hayagriva</term> <gloss>Lotus Powerful One</gloss> in union, emanate the eight great wrathful suppressors. From <term>Vajrakilaya</term> <gloss>Vajra Youth</gloss> in union, emanate the ten terrifying wrathful males and females. From the <term>Unsurpassed Heruka</term> in union, emanate the fourteen ladies of wisdom. From the <term>All-Gathering Arrogant One</term> in union, emanate the eight classes of great powerful arrogant spirits. From the <term>Powerful Black One</term> in union, emanate the eight classes of fierce spirits surrounded by their retinues. For the <term>Siddha Vajradhara</term> <gloss>Accomplished Vajra Holder</gloss>, there are the eight types of accomplished <term>Vidyadharas</term> <gloss>Knowledge Holders</gloss>. In the outer courtyard are the twenty-eight <term>Ishvaris</term> <gloss>Powerful Goddesses</gloss>. Along the outer surrounding iron mountain wall are the <term>Dakinis</term>, <term>Gings</term>, <term>Lankas</term>, Mothers, Sisters, <term>Bharmas</term>, and messengers, along with hundreds of thousands of vast <term>Dakini</term> hosts, who are the natural guardians of the command of glory. Having thus clearly visualized the essence, bless the body, speech, and mind. Grant empowerment with the five wisdoms, and having invited them from their natural abode, offer worship, praise, and entrust them with enlightened activities. Alternatively, leave the mind in the state of <term>dharmata</term> <gloss>suchness</gloss>, uncontrived and naturally luminous. For whoever enters and has devotion for this authentic path, they will be joined to the level of spontaneous accomplishment. There is no doubt this will occur in a single lifetime. Thus it was said. In the <term>Tantra of the Gathering of the Sugatas</term> <gloss>Deshek Dupa</gloss>, this is the ninth chapter, the summary of the authentic accomplishment.

The skilled practitioner generates them in the southern direction.
The Bhagavan Padma Heruka,
With red-black, blue, and white faces.
Holding a lotus, a club, a battle-axe,
And the thousand worlds and so forth;
Acts as the holder and possesses the consort.
Generate them on the western spoke.
The Bhagavan Karma Heruka,
With green-black, white, and red faces.
A double-vajra, a sword,
A battle-axe, and so forth likewise;
Having seized them, together with Krodhishvari,
Generate them with devotion in the northern direction.
