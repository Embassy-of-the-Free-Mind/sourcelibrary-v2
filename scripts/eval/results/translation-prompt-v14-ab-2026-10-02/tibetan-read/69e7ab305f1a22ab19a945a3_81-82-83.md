<summary>Subhuti explains that all past, present, and future Buddhas attain enlightenment through the Great Vehicle and the Perfection of Wisdom. He defines the Perfection of Wisdom as an intellect that has "gone far beyond" all conceptual categories, including the five aggregates and even the perfections themselves.</summary>
