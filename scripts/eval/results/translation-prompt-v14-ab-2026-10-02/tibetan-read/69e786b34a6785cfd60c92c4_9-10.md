"...since there is no inside or outside, how can there be [a distinction between] meditative equipoise and post-meditation?"

Having heard the meaning of that request, the Buddha Beholding a Hundred Thousand was pleased by the golden lotus and gave this answer: "In this very primordial wisdom awareness, there has never been meditative equipoise or post-meditation from the beginning. For a great meditator who lacks this definitive meaning, post-meditation arises in the mind of the five senses."

By that which was spoken, doubts about meditative equipoise and post-meditation were cut. Because the request was made, certainty was found. Therefore, primordial wisdom was manifested.
