… radiant, is visualized. Have the disciple meditate on the awakening mind and have them say: *Om sarva yoga citta utpatayami* <gloss>Om, I generate the mind of all yoga</gloss>. 
Touching the vajra to the heart, say: *Surate samaya stvam ho! Siddha vajra yatha sukham!* <gloss>O great joy, may the pledge be firm! Accomplish the vajra according to happiness!</gloss> 
Saying *Hum* with the action vajra, lead the disciple inside the curtain. Placing the vajra on the disciple's head, the master says: "Do not disparage this; do not violate the pure pledges. You shall not go to the hell realms." By saying *Om panca amrita udka tha*, they are placed under oath. 
Then, the wisdom beings are brought down according to the mandala tradition. Saying *Om pratitstsa vajra ho*, the flower is offered and the deity is identified. Saying *Om pratigrihna hum*, the flower garland is placed on one's own head.

The ascetic conduct is obtained. It is also appropriate to perform the Master empowerment at this point. Below, the concluding rituals will appear together. May it be virtuous. 
Tashi Shok <gloss>may there be auspiciousness</gloss>. 
The secret empowerment is as follows: The disciple offers a mandala and one *zho* of gold as the basis for the empowerment and makes a prayer. The Guru, with the support, assumes the pride of Chakrasamvara and consort. Inside the curtain, having blessed the vajra and lotus, the awakening mind is descended through the union of the "all-pervading," and it is given to the disciple. The near-essence mantra of the male and female deities is also recited. 
Then, saying *Aho sukha* <gloss>O, bliss!</gloss>, the disciple meditates that the channels and wheels are filled with the white and red awakening mind, and that great bliss pervades everything. By this, the stains of speech are purified. The secret empowerment is obtained. One is empowered to meditate on the completion stage of the channels, winds, and drops <term>bindu</term>.
