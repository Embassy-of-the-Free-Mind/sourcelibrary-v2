Resting their knees on the ground and joining their palms, they addressed the Blessed One as follows:

As soon as they spoke, the Blessed One said, "Come, monks!" and their hair and beards fell away of their own accord, and they became monks wearing religious robes. Then the Blessed One taught and clarified the Dharma as was appropriate, whereby all their desires were exhausted, and they became <term>Arhats</term> <gloss>enlightened beings who have overcome all delusions</gloss>. That great assembly rejoiced and was delighted by what the Blessed One had spoken.
