The Leader has taught that the meditation on the virtue of Loving-kindness is the focus for sentient beings.

Everything that attains realization is the power of the Tathagata." 
Then, after the gods of the Great Virtue realm <note>Subhakrtsna gods</note> spoke of this samadhi, they praised the Blessed One with these verses: 
"All these hosts of Great Virtue gods have reached the end of the Dharma. I take refuge by bowing my head to the Teacher whose senses are peaceful and who knows peace. Those wise ones who honor the Buddha, who know the meaning and have crossed the ocean of cyclic existence; I praise the Sage, the Guide of Men, the one skilled in the way, the Leader who leads the leaders. Here, there is no fixed 'Sage-Dharma' residing anywhere. Just as a magician knows his illusion, so you, O Sage, know it well. Just as a wise man understands that various limbs and echoes arise in the mountains, likewise, O Sugata, O Rishi, you view the world including its gods and men. Here, there is no sentient being, no life-force, no nourisher, no person. You, Supreme Man, have dismantled perceptions and established emptiness. O Best of Two-Legged Beings, foremost of sentient beings, you also meditate on immeasurable Compassion. This intention of the Victorious Buddha is difficult for all beings to realize. The Guide has spoken that meditating on the virtue of Compassion takes sentient beings as its object.
