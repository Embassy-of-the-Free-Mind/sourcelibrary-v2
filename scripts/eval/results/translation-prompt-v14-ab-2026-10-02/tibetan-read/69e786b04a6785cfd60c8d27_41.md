Through such certainty of decision, meditation and non-meditation are liberated in their own place. Since everything has become the truth body, because mind and objects are inseparable, the limit of space is not indicated, it is indeterminately perfect. Regarding the mind, appearances, and the phenomena of things, whatever memories of the mind appear, non-substantial awareness is clear as the truth body. It is free from the terminology of "meditation" and "non-meditation." Therefore, the primordial wisdom of mind and objects has dawned.
From the Great Perfection, the Wheel of Primordial Wisdom, the Secret Tantra of the Heart, this is the thirteenth chapter, Teaching the Conduct of the Self-Arising Primordial Wisdom of Mind and Objects.
Then, the supreme conduct of the <term>vratacharyā</term> <gloss>disciplined conduct</gloss> was spoken to the assembled host. For the sake of the realization of the view, coarse conduct must be cut through. There is a pith instruction for not being bound by it. Therefore, it is called self-liberated conceptual thought. Listen to this, Essence of the Assembly! A yogi who is shown by the certain scriptures may even use his mother and sister. What need is there to mention the women of others? Even if one tortures or kills sentient beings, or if one steals the wealth of the Sangha and the stupas, primordial wisdom dawns without the obscuration of conditions, just as the lord of hell is without downfall. In the supreme conduct of the <term>vratacharyā</term>, the sacred bonds of guarding are not contradicted; this dharma that accords with scripture and logic is necessary. Listen to this, Vajrasatva! Those who practice dharma in the present age, who desire a peaceful, gentle, and soft continuum, do not have the pith instructions.
