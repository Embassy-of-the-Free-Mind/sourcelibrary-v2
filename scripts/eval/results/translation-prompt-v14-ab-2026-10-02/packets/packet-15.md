The measurement of a body seeks the solidities and thicknesses of bodies, such as how many feet the magnitude of a body contains. So that you may gain some knowledge of this, I will set forth the method or practice of measuring certain bodies, and first, indeed, the sphere. Therefore, if you wish to investigate the solidity of a sphere, multiply its diameter cubically (that is, multiply it by itself once square, and multiply the product again by the diameter), which cube exceeds the thickness of the sphere: when the excess is removed, however, the solidity of the sphere is left. By the gods, they believed that I had suffered terrible things, and at the same time they wished to see justice done for what they had witnessed regarding others, for he was bold and vile and no longer <margin>3</margin> to be restrained. Since these things are so, all that was appropriate for me to guard has been justly preserved for you, and I am present to prosecute, as you see, now that someone is bringing the case forward. I am here, men of Athens, having received much money which I could have taken to avoid prosecuting, yet I did not take it, and I have endured many <margin>4</margin> entreaties and favors and, by Zeus, threats.
