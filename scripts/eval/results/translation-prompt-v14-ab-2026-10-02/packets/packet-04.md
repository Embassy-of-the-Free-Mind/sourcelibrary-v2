## XII.

It is therefore established that the fanatical opinions of the Paracelsians, Weigelians, Mystics, Quakers, and others who are commonly called by the name of the illuminated, agree in this: that besides the body and the rational soul, there exists in man a certain substantial part divinely granted to him, and, as most of them do not disguise, derived from the divine essence itself. The same doctrine regarding the three parts of man is explained at great length by Felgenhawer in *The Forecourt, in the Holy and Most Holy of the Temple of God* <note>original: "Vorhoff / im heiligen und Allerheiligsten am Tempel GOttes"</note>; the individual readings of this fanatical booklet sufficiently declare the reasons for the individual parts and the dreams of the fanatics concerning them. It is also worth consulting the *Decent General Doctrine of God* <note>original: "anständige allgemeine GOttes Lehre"</note> of Bartholomaeus Sclei; as well as the *Book of Testimony of the Hidden Field-Treasure* <note>original: "Buch des Zeugnisses des verborgenen Ackerschatzes"</note>, whose author calls himself HIEL; the *Single-Essence Life of God* <note>original: "einwesige Leben GOttes"</note>; as well as the *Thorough Instruction* <note>original: "gründlicher Unterricht"</note> of Matthes Weyer, who was highly enlightened by God, and similar monstrosities of books written by those furies, which are truly unworthy of wasting good and irrevocable hours reading.

## XIII.

Moreover, this doctrine concerning the threefold part of man occupies a primary place in their own <term>mataeologia</term> <gloss>vain or empty discourse</gloss>. For the author of *The Forecourt* claims, most ineptly, in Chapter XII, that this contains the foundation not only of all Christianity, but of all wisdom whatsoever that exists in heaven and on earth. Yet, if it should happen to escape someone that he possesses five senses, or what the nature of sight, hearing, or the rest might be, must it therefore be decreed that they cannot perform their own function? I certainly do not think so. Meanwhile, Andreas Christophorus Rafelius extols this dogma with such great praise that he does not fear to speak of it in *The New Year’s Trumpet* <note>original: "Der Neujahrs Posaune"</note> in this manner: *This is actually the true bedrock, and the certain foundation upon which the house of our Christianity must be placed, laid, and founded if it is to endure: and from this everything—preaching, reading, writing, hearing the Word of God, singing, praying, good*

### LES EXPÉRIENCES MAGIQUES DU GROUPE INDÉPENDANT D'ÉTUDES ÉSOTÉRIQUES.

En 1889, nous avons fondé à Paris le *Groupe indépendant d'études ésotériques*, destiné à contrôler les expériences concernant les forces encore inconnues en action dans l'homme et dans la nature.

Les premiers mois furent consacrés à la propagande et les travaux purent être fructueusement poursuivis dès le début de l'année 1891. A ce moment le groupe était divisé en sections techniques dont l'une s'occupait de Magie pratique. Personnellement nous avions fait quelques expériences suivies de succès, ainsi que notre ami F. Ch. Barlet ; mais ces expériences avaient besoin d'un contrôle que la suite des études pouvait seule fournir. L'année 1891 fut consacrée aux études préliminaires, et au commencement de 1892 plusieurs essais, accompagnés d'entraînement végétarien et concernant la vision des élémentals, furent entrepris et couronnés de succès, principalement en ce qui touche les expériences de M. Delfosse. Au milieu de l'année, des faits rapportés dans les grimoires furent soumis à un contrôle expérimental et M. Michelet, un des commissaires, put même certifier le fait que le diamant pur empêchait le passage de l'action magnétique de l'aimant. Ce fait rapporté par Agrippa et inconnu des physiciens modernes est familier aux marchands de diamants, ainsi qu'il fut constaté. En même temps, une expérience d'évocation consciente fut tentée par M. M. H. et, malgré des oublis et des fautes nombreuses, cette expérience donna des résultats très curieux et très instructifs. Nous en rapporterons les principaux détails d'après les opérateurs eux-mêmes.

### THE MAGICAL EXPERIMENTS OF THE INDEPENDENT GROUP FOR ESOTERIC STUDIES.

In 1889, we founded the *Independent Group for Esoteric Studies* in Paris, intended to supervise experiments concerning the still-unknown forces in action within man and in nature.

The first months were devoted to propaganda, and the work could be pursued fruitfully from the beginning of the year 1891. At that time, the group was divided into technical sections, one of which dealt with practical Magic. Personally, we had conducted several successful experiments, as had our friend F. Ch. Barlet; but these experiments required a control that only the continuation of studies could provide. The year 1891 was devoted to preliminary studies, and at the beginning of 1892, several trials, accompanied by vegetarian training and concerning the vision of elementals, were undertaken and crowned with success, mainly regarding the experiments of Mr. Delfosse. In the middle of the year, facts reported in the <term>grimoires</term> <gloss>books of magic spells and spirit conjuration</gloss> were submitted to experimental verification, and Mr. Michelet, one of the commissioners, was even able to certify the fact that a pure diamond prevented the passage of the magnetic action of a magnet. This fact, reported by <term>Agrippa</term> <gloss>Heinrich Cornelius Agrippa, a 16th-century occultist</gloss> and unknown to modern physicists, is familiar to diamond merchants, as was confirmed. At the same time, an experiment of conscious evocation was attempted by M. M. H., and, despite numerous oversights and errors, this experiment yielded very curious and instructive results. We will report the main details according to the operators themselves.
