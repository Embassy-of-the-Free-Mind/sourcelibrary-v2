### XXV.
In this case, however, if the obligation of the principal is changed by a subsequent agreement, for instance, if it is agreed that the principal, which was due to be repaid on the next Calends, is to be paid in divided installments, the obligation of interest will also be considered changed, even if nothing was expressed regarding them.
