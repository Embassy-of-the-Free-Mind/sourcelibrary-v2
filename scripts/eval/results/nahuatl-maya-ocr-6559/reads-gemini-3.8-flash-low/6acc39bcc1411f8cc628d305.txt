# In illo tempore.
Ibat Jesus in ciuitatem que vocatur Naim etc̃.

<image-desc size="medium" type="decorative" significance="low">Large calligraphic initial Y in dark red/crimson ink with flourishes spanning four lines</image-desc>
Yniquac nican tlalticpac monemitinya tote°. i. x°. Ceppa ompa mouicaya ynaltepetlipan ytoca yocan Naim, quimouiquilique ynitlamachtilhuan yuan occequin cenca miectlacafl inquimouilique. Yniquac altepetenamitl itlan omaxitito, ipan moquixtito micqui, yeuiquitoaya Ynin micqui cetlacatl icnociuatzintli çan icenteconetiz, quichoquititinuh, yuan miequintin cololhuitiuij. Auh iniquac oquimottili tote°. quimocnoittili quimolhuili. Ciuatzintle macaxicho ca niman itlan ommaxiti, commotzitzquili intlapechtli. Auh inyeuantin quinapalotiuia ocmomanque.
