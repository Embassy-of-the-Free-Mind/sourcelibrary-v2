- p.233: generic "then the great master bowed to the buddhas of the ten directions…" built on the refrain
  དེ་ནས་སློབ་དཔོན་ཆེན་པོ་དེ་ཉིད་ཀྱིས་; the leaf is 7 dense cursive lines with ༔ every few syllables.
