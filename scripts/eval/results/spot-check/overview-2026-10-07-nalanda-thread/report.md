## Per book

- **vajrayana** · show · [Kanjur Khri brgyad stong pa Ka](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/69e7ac2e5f1a22ab19aa4e9c) — This is a Neyphug manuscript volume of the Perfection of Wisdom in 18,000 lines, with two leaves photographed per image. The transcription is of the right leaves and follows the manuscript closely, with only scribal-level slips. The English is a close, literal rendering that a reader can trust for content.
- **vajrayana** · show_with_caveat · [Neyphug Kanjur mDo sde Ka](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/69e7ab985f1a22ab19a9a963) — This is the Bhadrakalpika (Fortunate Eon) Sutra, with its long verse catalogue of the thousand Buddhas, from a Neyphug manuscript Kanjur. The transcription matches the leaves well. The English renders names and sense reliably, but large Tibetan numbers (bye ba, khri phrag) are often converted to the wrong order of magnitude, so the assembly, stupa and lifespan figures cannot be trusted.
- **vajrayana** · show_with_caveat · [Kanjur 'Bum Nga](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/69e7ac095f1a22ab19aa2934) — This is a volume of the Perfection of Wisdom in 100,000 lines, two manuscript leaves per image. The transcription is of the right leaves but is noisier than in sister volumes: the refrains are misread and a negation is inserted, and the English mostly corrects these silently. The English is literal and dependable for sense, but one note blames the manuscript for an OCR error, and a quarter of the leaves have no translation.
- **vajrayana** · show · [Neyphug Kanjur mDo sde Na](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/69e7aba75f1a22ab19a9bc2a) — This is a Neyphug manuscript sutra volume holding the end of the Samdhinirmocana and the Suryagarbha sutra with its dharanis. The transcription is of the right leaves and is accurate. The English is careful and literal, with occasional slips in names and in the Sanskrit forms of names.
- **old-javanese-hindu-buddhist** · show_with_caveat · [Kakawin Sutasoma (Old Javanese Buddhist epic)](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/69e01110c47b5c9f882143be) — Mpu Tantular's Sutasoma in a 1994 romanised typescript. The transcription matches the page apart from the margin folio numbers. The English is a fluent first draft that misnames characters and garbles who does what in difficult stanzas, so it must not be quoted without checking the Old Javanese; the edition's rights are also unclear.
- **old-javanese-hindu-buddhist** · show_with_caveat · [Het Râmâyana op Javaansche tempel reliefs](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/6a195dcd3e125aa8d6dffc7a) — Kats's 1925 photographic catalogue of the Rāmāyaṇa reliefs at Prambanan, with Dutch and the original English side by side. The transcription is of the right pages and mostly exact. Where the 1925 English exists, readers should trust it over the AI English: on page 9 a misread word reverses Rāma's reasoning in the AI translation.
- **old-javanese-hindu-buddhist** · show_with_caveat · [De Bodhisattwa's van den Mĕndut](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/6a195c693e125aa8d6dff7fc) — Krom's 1918 study of the Mendut Bodhisattvas, transcribed accurately with a reliable English translation. The record also holds pages from a separate article on the temple's roof restoration, which the title does not mention. One dropped negation on page 13 turns Krom's 'why not Java?' into its opposite.
- **old-javanese-hindu-buddhist** · show_with_caveat · [De legende van Kuñjarakarṇa volgens het oudst bekende handsc](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/6a1969a43e125aa8d6e0071f) — Kern's 1901 edition and Dutch translation of the Kuñjarakarṇa, the Javanese Buddhist hell-journey legend under Vairocana. The Dutch transcription is exact and the English follows it closely. On the translation pages, notes that look like Kern's footnotes may be invented and must be checked against his printed notes.
- **javanese-indonesian** · do_not_show · [[Collective volume with mostly Sufi texts in Malay, Arabic (](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/6a20326418654bf8e1902bec) — A seventeenth-century Malay (Jawi) collection of Shams al-Din al-Sumatrani's Sufi treatises on the martabat and divine attributes. Plain prose openings are transcribed roughly and translated readably, but the colophon's title is misread and the English replaces it with an invented one, and the diagram openings are only partly transcribed, with repeated and templated blocks translated as doctrine. The English cannot be trusted for citation.
- **javanese-indonesian** · show_with_caveat · [[Juzʾ (part) of Qurʾān; from Java] Or. 7092](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/6a2032bf18654bf8e19036c9) — A provincial Javanese Qur'an manuscript (parts of al-Baqara, al-Ma'ida, al-An'am). The transcription follows the leaves line by line but normalises the scribe's spelling to the printed Cairo text and once replaces a scribal variant with the standard reading; the English is a sound standard Qur'an translation. One page carries 25 verses recited from memory in a continuation note.
- **javanese-indonesian** · show · [[Wird. A collection of Awrād for the seven days of 7 days of](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/6a2032e518654bf8e1903b96) — A small illuminated 1879 collection of daily litanies (awrad) made of Qur'anic verses, with Malay rubrics telling the reader which day each is recited. The transcription matches the leaves word for word, including the marginal correction and catchwords, and the English is a correct Qur'an translation with verse references. The Malay rubric is slightly misread on one page.
- **javanese-indonesian** · show_with_caveat · [Muqaddimah fi ʿilm al-ʿArabiyah (mutammimah) li masāʾil al-a](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/6a20331d18654bf8e19042cb) — A nineteenth-century copy of al-Hattab's Mutammimat al-Ajurrumiyya, the advanced Ajurrumiyya grammar used in Southeast Asian Islamic schools. Most lines are transcribed correctly and the English is a clear grammatical translation, but on two of four pages the OCR garbles or replaces a phrase, once inventing a rule the author did not write and once supplying a clause from the standard text that this copy lacks.
- **indian-buddhist-jain** · show_with_caveat · [The Conception of Buddhist Nirvana](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/6992590b8812793469fe0503) — This is Stcherbatsky's 1927 study of the Madhyamaka absolute, with a Devanagari Prasannapada text bound after it. The transcription is of the right pages and is accurate. The English pane is mostly Stcherbatsky's own words with added glosses and occasional invented headings. Its translation of the Sanskrit is usable but has slips such as 'rabbit's horn' for a donkey's horn.
- **indian-buddhist-jain** · show_with_caveat · [The Creed of Half Japan - Japanese Buddhism](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/6953c9c777f38f6761bdefa4) — Arthur Lloyd's 1911 account of Japanese Buddhism, transcribed accurately from the University of California copy. The English pane is not a translation but a modernised paraphrase that sometimes changes the author's wording and tone, so a scholar should read the transcription. It belongs more naturally with Japanese than with Indian material.
- **indian-buddhist-jain** · show_with_caveat · [Vedanta Sutras, Part 2: Commentary of Shankaracharya (SBE Vo](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/699246bebc722ec0ee812869) — Thibaut's 1896 English translation of Sankara's Brahmasutra commentary (SBE 38). The transcription is of the right pages and accurate. The second English pane is an AI rewrite of Thibaut that sometimes drops the opponent's paragraph or adds sentences, so a scholar should read the transcription and ignore the 'translation'.
- **indian-buddhist-jain** · show_with_caveat · [Narada Bhakti Sutra (Sacred Books of the Hindus, Vol. 7)](https://sourcelibrary.org/book/699259218812793469fe2a44) — Sacred Books of the Hindus vol. 7: Narada's and Sandilya's bhakti sutras with Svapnesvara's commentary, and a Bhagavata verse anthology with Sridhara's glosses. The transcription is of the right pages. Its English is reliable, but its Devanagari has verse-number and word misreads. The English pane is trustworthy where it copies the printed English. Where it handles the Sanskrit commentary, it leaves parts untranslated, garbles them, or adds a verse not on the page.
