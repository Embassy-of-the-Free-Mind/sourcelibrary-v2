books, seeded, round-robin over severity. Every page was re-classified live just before its write. 20 were
written and none skipped; each has a `page_revisions` row (reason `hidden-meta-move-5376`) and a `sweep_log` row.
Rows: `pilot-2026-10-03-applied.jsonl`.

**Check.** For each page I opened the page image and read the head of the page against the moved text
("read from image"). I also fetched the rendered page at sourcelibrary.org/book/<id>?page=N and confirmed the moved
text shows in the page body, outside any script payload. 19 of 19 pages in visible books render it. #0 is in a
hidden book (`visible: false`), so its page returns 404. On #6 and #15 the R2 copy of the image is one leaf
behind (printed page 116 for 117; page 4 for 5). On those two I read the Archive scan the page record names
(`photo`); that is a separate image defect, noted below.

**The miss shape.** On both misses the payload IS the page's own text, and the reader now sees it. The
problem is what comes in front of it: a lead-in from the previous page, which the reader meets twice. On #4
it is that page's own text, translated again; on #19 it is a paraphrase. The scan's rule looks at the
payload as a whole, so a short lead-in ahead of a long own-text payload does not move the class. Neither
case is invented text.

**A $0 signal for the #4 shape.** `headInPrev` is the share of the payload's first 40 words (as trigrams)
found in the previous page's translation. It is recorded on every row and is evidence only. On #4 it is 1.0,
on #19 0.18, and on #3 (a correct page) 0. Over the full dry run of the remaining candidates (2026-10-03,
read-only), **9,582 are eligible**: headInPrev ≥ 0.6 on 103, 0.3–0.6 on 492, 0.1–0.3 on 1,765, < 0.1 on 7,222.
Skipped live: 189 in-body, 178 held, 133 payload gone, 27 changed since classification, 27 with no
translation, 1 already unwrapped by #5148, 3 whose live class is no longer own-text.

**Side finding (out of scope).** On 2 of the 20 books (69aec1eece4dead60877abde, 69e8b29f2ff2a8dc09e78458)
the R2 image `archived/<book>/<n>.jpg` is the leaf before page n, while the OCR and the `photo` scan agree
with each other.

**Rollback.** `node --env-file=.env.production.local scripts/maintenance/move-hidden-meta-own-text.mjs --rollback --pages=<id,…|all> --apply`
restores each page from its `hidden-meta-move-5376` revision, but only where the live text is still the
moved text. The dry run of `--pages=all` finds 20 of 20 restorable.
