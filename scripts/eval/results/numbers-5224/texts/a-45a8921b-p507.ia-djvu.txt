Barry, William Henry

Lawyer; b., Nashua, N. H., March
13, 1878; s. Patrick and Honor (Moran) Barry; ed. parochial and public schools, Nashua; Holy Cross College, A.B. 1898; Boston Univ. Law
School, LL.B. 1901; admitted to the
bar and in practice in Nashua to the
present time; Catholic; Democrat;

Coal and Coke Co.; member Fraternal Order of Eagles, B. P. O. E.,
