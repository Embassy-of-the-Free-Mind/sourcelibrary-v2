Cain, John Leavitt
Physician; b., Goshen, N. H., Sept. 26, 1856; s. George W. and Cynthia J. (Leavitt) Cain; (reared in Croydon where he removed with his parents in childhood); ed. public schools, Kimball Union Academy, Meriden, 1880; Dartmouth College and Dartmouth Medical School, 1884; post-graduate course in Bellevue Medical College, N. Y.; commenced practice in Grantham in 1883, continuing four years, when he removed to Newport where he has since remained, establishing a wide practice; Liberal; Democrat; member of board of health for twenty years; member A. F. & A. M., Knight Templar and Shriner; I. O. O. F., and other orders; Sullivan
