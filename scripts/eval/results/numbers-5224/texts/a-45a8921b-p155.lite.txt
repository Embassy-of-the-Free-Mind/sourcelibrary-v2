**Wellington, Leonard**
Lawyer; b., Walpole, N. H., Sept. 12, 1841; s. William and Achsah (Kidder) Wellington; ed. Walpole schools, Mt. Caesar Seminary, Swanzey, Bernardston, Mass., Academy, Kimball Union Academy, Meriden, Albany, N. Y., Law School, 1865; studied in office of the late Don H. Woodward of Keene; admitted to the bar in 1865 and has practiced there since; in partnership with Mr. Woodward ten years from July, 1866, since then alone; Congregationalist; Republican; member, Keene board of health, fifteen years; solicitor for Cheshire County, 1869-71; member, Lodge of the Temple, A. F. & A. M., Keene; m., Jan. 19, 1870, Harriet Lyon Chandler; two sons, Clarence E., b. April 11, 1872, and Lyon Chandler, b. Jan. 24, 1879. Residence, Keene, N. H.
