Not the least of the attractions of the Cape are the excellent facilities for yachting. The retired shipmasters, as well as the. pleasureseekers, own handsome yachts and engage in the sport. Regattas are
sailed each season at various points around the shore, under the auspices of the Cape Cod Yacht Club, in which nearly every town is represented. The past summer has been marked by the several yacht races
at Buzzards Bay, Nobscussett, and along the sound, many of the visitors having large and beautiful yachts for their private use.
