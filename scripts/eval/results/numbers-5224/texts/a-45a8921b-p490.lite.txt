**Runnells, Ellsworth Potter**

Soldier (twin brother to preceding); b., Concord, N. H., April 7, 1894; s. Everett Hazen and Clara Frances (Potter) Runnells; ed. Concord schools;

B; decorated with the Croix de Guerre, Dec. 17, 1918, standing beside his twin brother who received the American Cross at the same time (this is believed to be the only instance where twin brothers were simultaneously
