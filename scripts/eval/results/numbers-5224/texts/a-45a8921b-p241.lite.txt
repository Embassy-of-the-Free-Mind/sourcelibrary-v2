**Tufts, Edith Souther**

Registrar of Wellesley College; b., Dover, N. H., Jan. 29, 1862, dau. Charles Augustus and Anne Blanchard (Souther) Tufts; ed. public schools of Dover, N. H., Wellesley College, B.A. 1885, M.A. 1895, studied at Yale University, 1894–5; teacher at Dana Hall School, Wellesley, 1885–94, 1895–8, Norwich Free Academy, Norwich, Conn., 1898–1900, Commonwealth Avenue School, Boston, 1900–02; instructor in Greek at Wellesley College, 1902–3; registrar, Wellesley College, 1903–; Episcopalian; member, Boston College Club, Boston Wellesley College Club. Residence, Dover, N. H.
