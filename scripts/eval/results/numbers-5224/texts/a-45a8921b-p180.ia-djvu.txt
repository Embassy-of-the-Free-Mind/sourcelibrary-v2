Herbert, John

Lawyer; b., Wentworth, N. H., Nov,
2, 1849; s. Samuel and Lydia Maria

Rumney, N. H., and Boston, Mass.;.

of New Ipswich Appleton Academy

1875; began practice in Boston in 1880,
