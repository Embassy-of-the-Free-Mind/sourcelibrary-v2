**Runnells, Everett Hazen**

Farmer and contractor; b., Concord, N. H., June 7, 1851; s. Deacon Hazen and Sarah E. (Corliss) Runnells; sixth in descent from Sergt. Samuel Runnells, of Bradford, Mass., before 1710; fifth in descent from Lieut. Samuel Runnells and fourth in descent from Lieut. Samuel Runnells, Jr., of Boxford, Mass., both of whom served in the French and Indian Wars, 1755-6; grandson of Joseph Runnells, who served in the Revolution; ed. Concord schools; one year (1868), at Oberlin College, Ohio; Congregationalist; Republican; supervisor of check list, Ward 4, several years; m., Oct. 20, 1880, Clara Frances Potter of East Concord, N. H.
