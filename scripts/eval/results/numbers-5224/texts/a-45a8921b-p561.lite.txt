**Bowker, Edgar Marshall**
Lawyer; b., Lisbon, N. H., April 18, 1876; s. Mitchell H. and Laura P. (Brooks) Bowker; ed. Whitefield, N. H., high school, 1893; George Washington Univ. Law School, 1903; admitted to N. H. bar, 1905, and in practice in Whitefield, N. H., since; Baptist; Republican; justice White-
