**Junkins, William Oliver**
Physician and surgeon; b., Berwick, Me., May 13, 1845; s. Alexander and Elizabeth Leighton (Staples) Junkins; ed. common schools, Eliot and South Berwick, Me., academies, Bowdoin College Medical Department, 1870; located in practice at Kittery Point, Me., in 1870, soon removing to Greenland, N. H., where he continued twenty years; removed to Portsmouth in 1891 remaining in practice there till 1917; Methodist; Democrat; mayor of Portsmouth, 1895, 1896; U. S. pension examiner under Presidents Cleveland
