Who was the Buddha?
The evidence that these questions were constantly
disturbing the minds of the disciples ever since the
Master's entrance into Parinirvana, is scatteringly
revealed throughout the Buddhist texts both Southern
and Northern. The regret of the immediate followers
that they did not ask the Buddha to prolong his
earthly life, while the Buddha told them that he
could do so if he wished, and their lamentation over
the remains of the Blessed One, "How soon the
Light of the World has passed away!" l — these
utterances may be considered the first drops foreboding
the showers of doubt and speculation as to his
personality.
