**Britton, William John**
Lawyer; b., Wolfeboro, N. H., June 18, 1872; s. John and Margaret (Macauley) Britton; ed. public school, Brewster Free Academy, Boston University Law School; law student with W. D. H. Hill; admitted to the bar, June 22, 1905, and has since practiced in Wolfeboro; Progressive Republican; moderator and town clerk of Wolfeboro, holding the latter office fourteen
