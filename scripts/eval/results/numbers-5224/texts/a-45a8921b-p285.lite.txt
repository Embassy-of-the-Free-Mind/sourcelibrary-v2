**Shurtleff, Merrill**
Lawyer, b., Colebrook, N. H., March 10, 1876; s. William J. and Mary J. (Merrill) Shurtleff; ed. public schools, Holderness school, 1887-8, Dartmouth College, A.B., 1892; read law in office of Drew, Jordan & Buckley, Lancaster; admitted to the bar in 1896, and continued with the firm, in practice (now Drew, Shurtleff, Morris & Oakes); Episcopalian; Republican; N. H. Fish and Game Commissioner, 1902-8; ex-judge Lancaster Police Court; U. S. Commissioner for New Hampshire; member A. F. & A. M., Lancaster Club; m., June 14, 1897, Emilie Porter, Lancaster; children, William Porter, b. April 29, 1898; Merrill, Jr., June 11, 1902. Residence, Lancaster, N. H.
