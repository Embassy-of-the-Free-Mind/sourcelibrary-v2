**Hartford, Fernando Wood**

Publisher; b., Chateaugay, N. Y., Nov. 14, 1876; s. Mark and Eliza J. (Silver) Hartford; ed. public schools and Morse Business College, Manchester, N. H.; his first newspaper work was done at the age of ten years, as a carrier for the *Manchester Morning Union*; at thirteen engaged with the *Union* as an office boy, subsequently serving as a bookkeeper and general utility man until at sixteen, he was sent to Portsmouth, at $9.00 per week, to establish a circulation in that part of the State; subsequently he served for fifteen years in various clerical capacities, at the Portsmouth Navy Yard, attaining the position of chief clerk to the purchasing paymaster—this early connection accounting for the deep interest he has ever since taken in the Navy Yard and its work; publisher, *Portsmouth Herald*, since 1891, acquiring the *Daily Chronicle* and *N. H. Gazette* (weekly) in 1893, all of which he has since published; Congregationalist; Republican; mem-
