**Hadley, Charles John**
Law and Real Estate; b., Weare, N. H., Aug. 25, 1845; s. John L. and Elizabeth L. (Cilley) Hadley; ed. public schools; Baptist, Democrat; removed to Iowa in 1865 where he studied law and was admitted to the bar in 1871; in 1877 returned to New Hampshire and engaged in agriculture in his native town; held various town offices in Weare and represented the town in the legislature of 1889; removed to Manchester twenty-five years ago, and was for twelve years engaged with A.
