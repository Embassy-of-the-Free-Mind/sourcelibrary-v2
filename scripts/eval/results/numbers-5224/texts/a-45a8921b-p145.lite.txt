**Fletcher, Robert**
Educator, civil engineer; b., New York City, Aug. 23, 1847; s. Edward H. and Mary A. (Hill) Fletcher (both from Cavendish, Vt.); ed. public schools, College of City of New York (three years); U. S. Military Academy, West Point, 1868; second lieutenant, U. S. Artillery, serving at Brownsville, Tex. and Fort Trumbull, New London, Conn.; instructor in mathematics, U. S. Military Academy, 1869-70; resigned to become senior professor and director, Thayer School of Civil Engineering, at Dartmouth, 1871, serving to the present time; consulting engineer on water works and sanitation; engineer in charge of construction of Hanover water works, Enfield, N. H., water works, reservoir for Hartford, Vt., water works, steel bridges—four spans each—across Connecticut River at West Lebanon and White River at Hartford, Vt.; conducted part of the New Hampshire-Vermont Boundary
