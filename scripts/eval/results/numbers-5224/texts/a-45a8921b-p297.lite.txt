**Johnson, Jesse**
Lawyer; b., Bradford, Vt., Feb. 2, 1842; s. Elliot and Sarah (Taylor) Johnson; ed. Dartmouth College, 1863; read law and engaged in practice in Brooklyn, N. Y.; U. S. District Attorney, Eastern District of New York, 1889-93; member New York constitutional convention, 1894 (chairman committee on Cities); justice supreme court of New York, 1897-8. Author "Testimony of the Sonnets as to the Authorship of the Shakespearean Plays and Poems," 1898; "Glimpses of Europe," 1906; m., 1st, Sarah E. Russell, Brooklyn, d. 1897; 2d, 1902, Adaline Pritchard, Worcester, Mass.; retired some years since. Residence, "Brookside," Orford, N. H.
