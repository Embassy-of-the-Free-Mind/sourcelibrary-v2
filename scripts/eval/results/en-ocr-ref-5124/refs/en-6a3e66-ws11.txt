59, Note.—The effect of local attraction on the pendulum is so great, that it has rendered the experiments made with that instrument for the purpose of ascertaining the compression of the earth very uncertain. Mr. Baily, President of the Astronomical Society, has devoted much attention to the investigation of this subject. He finds that the experiments of Captain Foster, whose early loss is so justly lamented, give a compression of \frac{1}{289\dot 48} ; those of Captain Sabine give \frac{1}{288\dot 40} ; the mean of the French and Russian experiments give \frac{1}{267\cdot 23} ; from the mean of the whole Mr. Baily deduces the compression to be \frac{1}{285\cdot 26} ; but even this is not conclusive.
