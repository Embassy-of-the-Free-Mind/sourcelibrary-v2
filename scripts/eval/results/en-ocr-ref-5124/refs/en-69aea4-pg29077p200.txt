321 De top.
C.P. iii. c. 8, 'habens inter se cisternam, cujus camera
lateritia sustinetur columnis marmoreis circiter sexaginta'; cf.
Die byzant. Wasserbehälter , pp. 59, 222-23. The bath of
Kyzlar Aghassi Hamam may represent the bath built by the eunuch
Nicetas, in the reign of Theophilus, and was probably supplied with
water from the cistern beside it (Banduri, vi. p. 133).
