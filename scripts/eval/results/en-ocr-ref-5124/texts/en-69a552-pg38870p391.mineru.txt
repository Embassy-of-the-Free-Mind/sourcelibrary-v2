And remember it is not the only trouble your Crown colony system can give; it has a few glorious opportunities left of further supporting everything I have said about it, and more. But I will say no more. You have got a grand rich region there, populated by an uncommon fine sort of human being. You have been trying your present set of ideas on it for over 4oo years; they have failed in a heart-breaking drizzling sort of way to perform any single solitary one of the things you say you want doņe there. West Africa to-day is just a quarry of paving-stones for Hell, and those stones were cemented in place with men's blood mixed with wasted gold.

Prove it ! you say. Prove it to yourself by going there— I don't mean to Blazes—but to West Africa.
