FAIRY TALES FROM BRAZIL

"It is my house," said the stag. "I built
it myself."

"I made the clearing for the house," said
the tiger, "I built the sides and made the
door and window."

"I started the clearing," said the stag.
"I laid the foundations and put on the roof
of dried grass."

The stag and the tiger quarrelled all
night about whose house it was. At daybreak they decided that they would live together there.

The next night the tiger said to the stag,
"I'm going hunting. Get the water and
have the wood ready for the fire. I shall
be almost famished when I return."

The stag got the wood and water ready.
