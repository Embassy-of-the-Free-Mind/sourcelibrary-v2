You have understood, my son, the manner of rebirth itself: when the number ten is present, the intellectual generation is composed, and it casts out the number twelve. <note>The ten divine powers, introduced on the previous page, replace the twelve vices; the "intellectual generation" is the spiritual birth of the mind.</note> And this we have beheld from the generation itself. Whoever, therefore, through the kindness of the generation that is according to God, lets go of bodily sense, knows himself to be composed of divine things; and having become unswerving, he rejoices with his whole mind in divine power. **TATIUS.** O father, I conceive it, not by the sight of the eyes but by the activity of the mind, which works through the inmost powers. I am in heaven, in earth, in water, in air. I am in animals, in trees; in the body, before the body, and after the body. <note>Tat describes the mystical experience of rebirth: the reborn mind feels itself present in all things and in all times.</note> But tell me this as well: how are the avenging darknesses, twelve in number, driven back by the ten powers? What is the manner of it, Trismegistus? **TRISMEGISTUS.** This tabernacle, O son, is built from the zodiac circle, which consists of that number twelve. <note>The "tabernacle" or tent is the human body, shaped by the twelve signs of the zodiac, which give rise to the twelve vices.</note> And its numbers are eleven, yet one and all-formed according to the ideas of nature, for the wandering and circuit of man. <note>original: "Numeriq; undecim sunt: unus omniformisq;"; the Latin is obscure here and may reflect a faulty reading. The sense seems to be that the twelve, though many, form a single nature taking every shape, which leads man astray.</note> Thus those avengers are in some way separated from one another, yet in acting they are in some way joined together: see how recklessness cannot be separated from anger. Rightly, therefore, according to right reason, they withdraw only so far as they are driven back by the ten powers, that is, by the number ten. The number ten, O son, is the begetter of the soul.
