<summary>Croll, following Paracelsus, argues that diseases arising from the stars must be cured by remedies with an astral or magnetic affinity, such as water-pepper, goldenrod, dragonwort and comfrey, and that diseases caused by incantation must be repelled magically. He grounds this in a sympathy between heaven and earth: as the Sun draws moisture upward, man and lower things draw influences from above.</summary>
