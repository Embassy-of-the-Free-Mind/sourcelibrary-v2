# Ten tantra-commentary pages whose English is longer than the Tibetan, read by eye (#5797)

Every page here was **read from text**: the stored Esukhia e-text of the side beside the stored English (05:35Z QA dump, before the #5797 repairs). Nobody looked at the woodblock images.

**Selection** (seed 5797): pages in v1–73 (tantra commentary; v74–79 are being drafted) with ≥ 60 syllables and English words per syllable > 1.0, one page per volume, 10 volumes. That class has 12,179 pages, median ratio 1.06, p90 1.185. Five pages were drawn from the whole class (v3, 18, 33, 44, 63; ratio 1.00–1.13). Five came from its long tail, ratio ≥ 1.25 (v13, 21, 26, 49, 64; 497 pages), where any added content should be easiest to see.

**Answer: the extra length is glossing and framing, not added content.** No page has a passage that is not on the side. The length comes from:
- `<gloss>` / `<note>` on terms, including Wylie transliterations in gloss tags (v26, v49);
- verse lists turned into expository prose ("As for the directions: …", "From the passage beginning … up until the end of …" spelled out on every item) (v13, v21, v64);
- explanatory notes.

On top of that: 3 notes that are wrong on their own terms, 1 inverted nuance, 1 wrong number, and 2 misread ritual verbs. These are the same kinds of error as in the 84000-scored sample.
