**1. The work**

This is a bilingual scholarly edition of the Septuagint, the Greek Old Testament. The Greek follows the Sixtine (Roman) text and is set facing a literal Latin translation. Jager edited it, and Firmin Didot printed it in Paris. The front matter (dedication, prefaces, papal brief) is in formal ecclesiastical Latin. Translate the Greek base text, and use the Latin version only as an aid.

**3. Layout**

Text pages carry the Greek and the Latin side by side, divided into verses that follow the Vulgate numbering. Alphabetic letters mark inversions of order. Supplements from Alexandrinus and other sources sit at the foot of the page, also divided into verses. Scholia record variant readings. Translate the running heads and roman page numerals once each, with the heads as plain labels. Leave Greek quotations inside the Latin prose in Greek, and add a translation in brackets.
