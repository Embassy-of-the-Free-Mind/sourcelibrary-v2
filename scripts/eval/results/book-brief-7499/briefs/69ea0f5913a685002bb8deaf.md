**1. The work**
A first-person *relación* (narrative report) addressed to the Crown by Álvar Núñez Cabeza de Vaca, in sixteenth-century Castilian prose. This is the Spanish original, in an edition with modern accents but archaic forms kept (*paresció*, *ansí*, *matallas*, *trujeron*). The register is plain, documentary and sober, with formulaic devotional phrases. Translate into clear, slightly formal English and keep the first person.

Some pages carry a running head; translate it but do not repeat it inside the body.
