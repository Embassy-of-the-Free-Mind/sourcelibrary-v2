## 1. The work
An anonymous German mystical-devotional treatise in short numbered chapters, in a 1523 printing. Early New High German prose: plain, sermon-like and scholastic in argument, with frequent "Nun möcht man sprechen… Antwort" objection-and-answer. It is an original vernacular text, not a translation. It is preceded by a short signed preface and a chapter register.

## 3. Layout
The page carries running heads ("Die deütsch / Theologia", "Register", "Vorred."), folio numbers, signatures and catchwords. Omit signatures and catchwords; keep the chapter headings ("Das .II. Capitel." → "Chapter II"). Marginal notes are short printed glosses: render each one as [Margin: …] at the point where it appears. Translate Latin tags (e.g. *Ex ore infantium*) and keep the original in italics. In the Register, keep the chapter numbers and folio references.
