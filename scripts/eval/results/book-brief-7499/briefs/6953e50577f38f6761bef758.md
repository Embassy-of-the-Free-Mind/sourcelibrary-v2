## 1. The work
A Latin philosophical treatise by Francis Bacon: the *Novum Organum*, preceded by the prefatory matter of his *Instauratio Magna* (a proem and a preface), in a later Italian reprint with a printer's dedication. The text is learned Neo-Latin prose with long periodic sentences and frequent metaphor. It is the base text, with no commentary.

## 3. Layout
The text is single-column prose. Running heads (PRAEFATIO.), page numbers, signatures and catchwords should be omitted. Words hyphenated across a page break should be completed. Title and dedication pages should be set centred, line for line. Italic proem, headings and scriptural quotations should be kept in italics. Long ſ is an ordinary s. A stray Spanish page from another bound work may appear; translate it as printed, rendering its Latin quotations too.
