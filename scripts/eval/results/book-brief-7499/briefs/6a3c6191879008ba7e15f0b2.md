**1. The work**
A Chinese Buddhist scholastic commentary in literary Chinese: Fazang's line-by-line exegesis (義記) of the *Awakening of Faith in the Mahāyāna* (大乘起信論), whose root text is attributed to Aśvaghoṣa. The register is technical and analytic, with dense section divisions and question-and-answer passages. The base text is quoted in segments, and each segment is followed by the commentary.

**3. Layout**
The root-text lemmata stand on their own lines. Render them in bold, and render the commentary that follows as plain text. △ marks a major section division; keep it as "△". Small interlinear notes (e.g. 但明以下二十字…) go in square brackets. Omit the running heads and folio numbers (起信論義記卷…, digits). Sūtra and treatise quotations go in quotation marks.
