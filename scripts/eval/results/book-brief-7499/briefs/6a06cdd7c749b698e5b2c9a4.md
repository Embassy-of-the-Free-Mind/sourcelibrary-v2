## 1. The work
A handwritten Arabic manuscript copy of al-Nafzawi's *al-Rawḍ al-ʿāṭir fī nuzhat al-khāṭir*. It is an erotological treatise in chapters (*abwāb*) that mixes prose instruction, anecdotes and quoted verse. The register is plain Middle Arabic prose with formulaic religious invocations. This is the base text, with no commentary. Translate frankly and accurately, without euphemising or adding explanations.

## 3. Layout
- **Text:** a single column of continuous prose in black ink. Chapter headings and story openings (وحكي, حكاية) are rubricated; render them as headings or bold.
- **Verse:** set as couplets, with hemistichs separated by ∴ or a gap. Render each as one line per verse, using " / " between hemistichs.
- **Margins:** marginal words are scribal insertions or corrections. Insert them where they belong and mark them [margin: …].
- **Deletions:** omit struck-through text.
- **Omit:** catchwords, page numbers and library stamps.
