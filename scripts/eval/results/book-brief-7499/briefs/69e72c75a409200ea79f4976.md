## 1. The work
A woodblock edition of the *Zhuangzi* with interlinear side-annotations (旁注), volume 5. The base text is the Classical Chinese *Zhuangzi*. A later annotator adds small-character glosses in terse Classical Chinese, and critical marks on style and diction. Translate both layers. The base text is literary prose; the glosses are compressed and philological.

## 3. Layout
Large characters are the *Zhuangzi* text. Small characters set beside or between columns are the annotator's glosses. Render each gloss in square brackets directly after the word or phrase it explains. The ○字法○ and 句法 marks are editorial and go in brackets as given above. Omit the running heads and fold-margin text, such as the chapter title, folio numbers and the publisher's hall name. Some OCR is garbled; flag it as [illegible] and do not guess.
