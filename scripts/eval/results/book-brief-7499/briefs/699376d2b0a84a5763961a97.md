## 1. The work

A volume of a critical edition of Plato's dialogues, edited by Ioannes Burnet. It contains the Greek base text (Attic prose dialogue, conversational register) and editorial matter in scholarly Latin: a preface, a table of dialogues, a list of manuscript sigla, and a terse critical apparatus at the foot of each text page. There is no commentary or translation.

## 3. Layout

- **Main text:** Greek dialogue. Marginal Stephanus page numbers, section letters (a–e) and line numbers should be kept in square brackets. Speaker labels (ΣΩ., ΚΛΕΙ.) become SOCRATES, CLITOPHON. Running heads (ΠΛΑΤΩΝΟΣ / dialogue title) are omitted.
- **Critical apparatus:** Keep it as notes. Keep the Greek readings and the sigla (A, A², F, D, M, a, f) unchanged. Translate the Latin abbreviations: om. = omits, add. = adds, secl. = brackets, in marg. = in the margin, s. v./suprascr. = written above.
