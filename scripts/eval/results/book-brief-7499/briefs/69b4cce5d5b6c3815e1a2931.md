## 1. The work
A Latin prose handbook of lay Christian devotion by Desiderius Erasmus, cast as a letter of spiritual counsel built on an extended military metaphor. This printing adds an editor's dedicatory letter, commendatory Latin verses, a subject index and printed sidenotes. The register is learned humanist Latin, rich in classical allusion and scriptural citation. Translate it as the base text.

## 3. Layout
Prefatory pages hold a dedication, verse (keep the lines as lines), and an alphabetical index giving Roman-numeral folio numbers. Text pages carry a running head ("Folium II"), signatures and catchwords; omit these. Translate the printed sidenotes as [Margin: …] at the point where each falls. Mark handwritten annotations as [Ms. note: …]. Expand abbreviations silently. Keep Greek words and add the English in brackets.
