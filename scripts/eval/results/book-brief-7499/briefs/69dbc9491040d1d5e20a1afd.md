## 1. The work
An early printed edition of Cicero's Latin prose treatise on moral duties, written as a letter to his son and addressed to him directly ("Marce fili"). Supplementary pieces follow it. The register is polished classical philosophical prose. This is the base text, not a commentary. The printer's Latin rubrics are added, and the print uses heavy scribal abbreviation.

## 3. Layout
The text runs as a single column of continuous prose. Red rubrics, which are chapter headings such as "De iniusticia", introduce sections; render each as a bold heading on its own line. Expand abbreviations silently. Keep Greek words in Greek, with the English in brackets. Set quoted verse (Ennius) as indented lines. Ignore library stamps, catchwords and marginal scribbles.
