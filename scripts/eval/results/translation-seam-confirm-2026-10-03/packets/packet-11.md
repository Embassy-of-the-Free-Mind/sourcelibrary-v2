### *Would there have been a political government in the state of innocence?*

Augustine answers in the negative, in book 19 of *The City of God*, chapter 15, as does the blessed Luther in chapter 2 of *Genesis*. For such despotic dominion and servile subjection, joined with the resistance of the will and with hardship—as it is now—would not have had a place in the state of innocence. However, it is likely that there would have been a paternal rule with the intention of counseling and loving, and a filial subjection. Lord Gerard, in the said treatise, number 26.

### *Should Kings be considered more "political" than subjects?*

Both those who command and those who obey are to be called political, since both are required for the constitution of a polity. But the former are political <term>kat' exochen</term> <gloss>preeminently</gloss>, and the latter less principally.

And upon his family and his noble companions, and grant them abundant peace. O people, fear God, and approach Him, may God have mercy on you, with your prayer in sobriety and tranquility, and make it with dignity and adornment. Praise your Lord on this great day, and gather yourselves in His remembrance through your prayer. Exalt God for how He has guided you, and draw near to God Almighty on this great day with your sacrificial animals. Glorify the rites of God, your Lord, and make them of your best offerings. Seek forgiveness, for piety is in your hidden hearts; for what reaches God of these deeds is only what is sincere. For He, Glory be to Him, the Almighty, says: "Their meat will not reach God, nor will their blood, but what reaches Him is piety from you." Thus He has subjected them to you that you may glorify God for that to which He has guided you; and give good tidings to the doers of good. May God make us and you among those whose hearts are filled and adorned, and who do not trade the Hereafter for the life of this world.

I seek refuge in God from the accursed Satan. "And the Day when We will remove the mountains and you will see the earth prominent, and We will gather them and not leave behind, from among them, anyone. And they will be presented to your Lord in rows, [and He will say], 'You have certainly come to Us just as We created you the first time. But you claimed that We would not make for you an appointment.'" May God bless me and you with the Great Quran; He is Generous, Noble, a King, Kind, Compassionate, and Merciful. I say this, while you listen, and I seek forgiveness from Him, for He is All-Forgiving. God is Most Great, God is Most Great, God is Most Great, God is Most Great, God is Most Great. Praise be to God, a praise as He has commanded. I bear witness that there is no god but God, alone with no partner, in defiance of those who deny and disbelieve, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and messenger, the master of humanity. May God bless him and grant him peace as long as an eye sees and an ear hears news.
