On the day Zen Master Qiefu of Yanzong Monastery in Fuzhou ascended the hall, a monk asked, "The master has ascended the jeweled throne; what should be discussed?" The master said, "Open your ear canals and put it in." The monk asked, "Why did the ancients say that it is a place where neither ears nor eyes can reach?"
