READING ORDER FOR THIS PAGE. The text is Hebrew, written and read from right to left, and that includes the order of columns. If the page has two or more columns of text side by side (for example two columns of Rashi script, or a main text beside a commentary), transcribe the RIGHT-HAND column first, completely, from its top line to its bottom line; then the column to its left, completely; and so on to the leftmost column. Never begin with the left-hand column, and never interleave lines from different columns. Before you write, look at the image and identify which column is on the right-hand side of the page.
