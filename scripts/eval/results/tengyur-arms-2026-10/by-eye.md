**E, the Pro second pass: all 7 pages it changed.**

1. **v3 p116 (Toh 1183, f. 59a). Improved.** The Tibetan is རྫས་མ་ལུས་པ་ནི་མ་ཡིན་ཏེ། དཔག་ཏུ་མེད་པའི་ཕྱིར་རོ, "but not all the substances, because they are immeasurable". B had "for their actual substances are beyond measure", with the negation folded away. E restored "but not all their substances". The judges agree (E above B, both).
2. **v4 p440 (Toh 1189, f. 221a). Unclear.** For ལྷ་དང་གཅིག་ཏུ་གོལ་བའི་གནས, E changed "straying from being one with the deity" to "straying into being one with the deity". Both readings of གོལ་བ are defensible here. The judges tie.
3. **v4 p459 (Toh 1189, f. 230b). Improved: a grouping error, not a negation.** The Tibetan reads །གཉིས་པོ་ས་སྤྱོད་མར་འགྱུར་རོ། …གསུམ་པོ་མཁའ་སྤྱོད་མ་ཡིན་ནོ: two goddesses are earth-goers and three are sky-goers. B grouped them three and two. E's regrouping is right.
4. **v93 p198 (Toh 3808, f. 100a). Improved.** བཅོམ་ལྡན་འདས་ opens a sūtra quotation as a vocative ("Furthermore, Lord, …", as 84000 has it). B made the Blessed One the speaker, twice. E's "O Blessed One, …" is right. In the ref test, both judges flagged this page in arms A and B.
5. **v93 p343 (f. 172b). Improved.** The Tibetan is འཇིག་རྟེན་ལས་མི་གཡོ་མི་འཕགས་མི་འདའ་ … ཀུན་བརྟགས་པ་ལས་ཞེས་བྱ་བའི་ཐ་ཚིག: "'does not move from … the world', where 'world' means the imagined". B had "these are beyond what is mentally constructed", which reverses it. E's "from what is mentally constructed" is right.
6. **v93 p449 (f. 225b). Made worse: a reversal introduced.** The block reads ས་བརྒྱད་པ་ལ་ཕྱིར་#ལྡོག་པར་འགྱུར་བ་གཉིས་པ, and the `#` is the e-text's collation mark at exactly that word. 84000 reads "a second not turning back … at the eighth level". That is the doctrine: the eighth bhūmi is the irreversible stage. B followed it ("non-retrogression"). E "corrected" it to the literal "retrogression". Both judges flagged E's sentence as a reversal and ranked B above.
7. **v93 p570 (f. 286a). Improved.** The Tibetan is འཕགས་པ་བྱམས་པས་ … བསྟན་པའི་ཕྱིར་ … ཞུས་སོ: Maitreya *asked* so that the classification would be shown. B made Maitreya the one who shows it. The judges tie.

Tally: 5 right, 1 wrong, 1 undecided. The one wrong edit is the dangerous shape: a stronger model trusting the literal block over a reading that the collation mark flags.

**D, the glossary: two reversals that B does not have.**

8. **v93 p557 (f. 279b).** The Tibetan is ཆོས་རྣམས་ཡོད་ན་མཉམ་པ་ཉིད་ཅེས་བྱ་བ་ཡང་ཡོད་དོ, "if dharmas exist, 'sameness' also exists". D has "if equality exists, then phenomena must also exist": the condition and the consequent are swapped. Both judges flagged it, and B is right here.
9. **v93 p134 (f. 68a).** The Tibetan is གཟུགས་ཅན་གྱི་ཆོས་རྣམས་ནི་ཡུལ་ན་མི་གནས་པའི་ཕྱིར་རོ, "material dharmas do not reside in an object". D has "for material things do not lack a location in an object", which is the same double negation the chained arm A produced on this page in the ref test. B ("does not abide in an object") is right.
