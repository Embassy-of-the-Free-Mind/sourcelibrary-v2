NOTE | Most of the leaf can be read, but it is uncertain in places. The [illegible] spots are mainly verse-number or daṇḍa marks between verses. Some cramped conjuncts are best guesses: the rephs in rows 3, 6 and 8, സ്സി/ദ്ധി in row 6, and the ending of row 1 (വ്യക്തന്തഥാ).
