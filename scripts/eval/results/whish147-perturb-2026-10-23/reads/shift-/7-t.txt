NOTE | I could read very little of this leaf, and this transcription shouldn't be used as a reading. The leaf has 9 rows. Rows 3–7 are split at both holes, and rows 1–2 and 8–9 run unbroken across the leaf; I still placed ‖ in rows 8–9 where they pass the holes. I'm fairly sure of the layout and of a few words (ലക്ഷണ്ണം, ചതുഷ്ടയസ്യ, വിഷയാണാ). Most other glyphs are uncertain, though, and many of the ക്ക/ദ sequences only mean "a character is here", not what it says.
