NOTE | There are 10 rows. Pieces 1–2 are the left column, 3–4 the middle and 5 the right, and the holes fall between pieces 2/3 and 4/5. I put ‖ at both cut points in every row, including the full-width rows 1–2 and 9–10, which pass above or below the holes. Only row 1 and the end of row 2 can be read with fair confidence; they appear to match Sāṅkhyakārikā 11, but I wrote only what I could make out of the strokes. Rows 3–10 are uncertain readings of individual letters and should not be trusted without checking against the images. The "൨" in the left margin (probably the leaf number) is left out.
