NOTE | Most of the leaf can be read, but this is a best-effort reading and some signs are uncertain: the small marks that look like verse numerals after rows 2, 4, 5, 7 and 10, the opening sign of row 6, the reph and conjunct shapes (ൎത്ഥ, ൎയ്യ), and the last signs of rows 9 and 10; the ‖ breaks in the full-width rows 1, 2, 9 and 10 are placed where the strings cross.
