# Collation of leaf.jpg against SK 29–34

This half-leaf has 9 lines, and every line runs on into the missing right half. A string hole interrupts lines 3–7. I could read verse text with reasonable confidence only on lines 1–3 and 8–9. The edition marks a typo in SK_29 (*kaṛaṇa*, *s^ aipā*) and an odd *a-tathā* in SK_30. I only list one of these as a difference where the leaf itself shows that part.

Leaf (left of the hole): […]*ṣṭā dṛṣṭe tathāpy*

The edition's *tri-vidhaṃ daśadhā bāhyaṃ trayasya viṣayākhyam* is also one foot short metrically. Still, I can only see *-ntaḥ*, so this is uncertain.
