Notes on the readings:

- **Line 1, SK_52.** After "na vinā" the leaf clearly has bhāvena (ഭാവെന), not liṅgena. The following akṣaras run liṅga- then three or four signs ending in a visarga, then liṅgā begins again. I read this as liṅga-nirvṛttiḥ, i.e. the two members are transposed relative to the edition, but the akṣaras between liṅga and the visarga are not fully legible.
- **Line 2, SK_53.** The first akṣara after the verse-divider is a conjunct I read as tat-, but it is not clear.
- **Line 3, SK_53.** The leaf writes the sandhi as bhautikas sargaḥ with doubled sa, where the edition has bhautikaḥ sargaḥ. I could not confirm a visarga after kavidha.
- **Line 5, SK_55.** Immediately after the hole the leaf reads duḥkha svabhāvena. Only about two akṣaras fit under the hole after jarāmaraṇa, and the right half of line 5 plus line 6 cannot hold the full second half of SK_55 before "-di viśeṣa-bhūta" begins line 6. The leaf therefore appears to have skipped from the first duḥkhaṃ to the second (prāpnoti cetanaḥ puruṣaḥ aliṅgasyāvinivṛttes tasmād duḥkhaṃ). I cannot see the hidden portion, so the omission is inferred from the visible text and the line length. The anusvāra on duḥkha is not clearly visible.
- **Line 7, SK_57.** The vocalic ṛ in vivṛddhi is not clearly distinguishable from i.
- **Line 8, SK_57.** The first akṣara of the line is unclear; it should be -ṣa of puruṣa but I cannot confirm it.
- **Line 9, SK_58.** The leaf plainly has lokaḥ with ka. The edition's logaḥ is an edition error.
