Plate questions asked by the brief:
- Is the plate described? Yes, on all 5 real plates, sometimes loosely (B4 "curved object").
- Is the caption/legend transcribed? Never as text for B3 and B5, whose labels appear only inside a description. The B1 table was transcribed, and the B2 labels were invented.
- Is text invented for the picture? Yes: B2 (label runs, the "Coptic" meta), B3 ("garden-dweller", "Tree of Christ"), B5 ("Depression", a false book title, "Azure Dragon").
- Is the facing text page correct? Yes for B1, B3, B4 and B5. B2 p95 is hallucinated. B6's text is one leaf off from every image.
- Does the plate-page translation contain anything not on the leaf? Yes in B2, B3 and B5. B1's contains less than the leaf.

NEW classes proposed:
- english-source-abridged: when the source is already English, the "translation" condenses or drops cells and sentences instead of copying them. (This matches the short stratum's commentary-abridged-to-summary; one class.)
- neighbour-stub-read-as-damage: a guard strip or adjoining leaf is reported as illegible water-damaged text.
- invented-context meta: the translator asserts facts about the previous or next page that are not on the leaf (e.g. "after the previous page's Coptic").
- plate-labels-only-in-description: printed words on a plate or diagram are paraphrased in <image-desc> but never transcribed, so they are unsearchable and unquotable.
- edition-banner-misread-as-title: a running banner or edition mark (欽定四庫全書) is misread as, and translated into, a different work's title.
- isolated-glyph-translated-as-word: a lone mark or character is given a confident English meaning.
- legacy-raw-OCR: an old engine OCR (no tags, noise) coexists with an LLM translation that silently corrects it.

Surprises:
- Book 6 is a text-only pair with a one-leaf image/text offset. It entered the "illustrated" stratum only because of a wrong page_type. The offset is the more serious finding, and it needs the two-sided arbitration (IIIF leaf vs R2 copy) before anyone repairs anything.
- Book 2 p95 is the worst case: fluent English "Euclid" built on hallucinated Greek, with nothing on the page telling the reader.
- The packet's tr_len does not match what is stored: Book 4 p321 says tr_len=0, yet a translation is present.
