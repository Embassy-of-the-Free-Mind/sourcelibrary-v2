**What the ten pages show.** A's defects are clause-sized: a reversal or wrong term in the middle of the
page (P060, P057, P052, P101), and on block pages whole clauses on the wrong side of the break (P083,
P053). B's defects sit at the very edge of the page (a split syllable, a broken-off last word) or are a
genuine ambiguity of the Tibetan (P021). P102 is the same reversal in both arms. In P060 (A only) and
P102 (both arms) the model "repairs" a statement whose logic looks backwards. That reversal comes from
the model, not from the lane.
