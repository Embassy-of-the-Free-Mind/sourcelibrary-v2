### ¶ Of the Squares.

The Squares remoue into the
fourth place from them, that is lea-
ping ouer two, right forwarde or
sydelong, as from the place of. A. to any
of these spaces .L, .M. .P. .R. flyinge
they maye remoue after the Knightes
draught, but that they must passe foure
spaces, as from .D. to. Y. or. Z. &c.
And this for the marching and remo-
uyng of the men, where note, that with
theyr flying draughte they can take no
man, but if neede be helpe to besiege
a man.
