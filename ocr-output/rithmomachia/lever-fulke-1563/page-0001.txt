# THE MOST NOBLE
## auncient, and learned playe, called the Philosophers game, inuented for the honest recreation of students, and other sober persons, in passing the tediousnes of tyme, to the release of their labours, and the exercise of their wittes.

->Set forth with such playne precepts, rules, and tables, that all men with ease may vnderstande it, and most men with pleasure practise it.<-

->by Rafe Leuer and augmen:ted by W. F.<-
