### ¶ Of takynge by Addition.

The takyng by Addition is all one
with the first kynde of play, in all
respectes, sauing that some require
the men that shoulde take by Addition
to stande in the next spaces to him that
is taken, either directly, or cornerwyse,
but the former waye is better.
