# The Philosophers game.

## ¶ To finde the midle betwene the first and the last.

Multiplie the first by the last , then
double the producte , and deuide
the whole by the first and the laste
added together, the quotient is then the
mydle number. As hauyng .6. and .12. I
woulde knowe the mydle in Musicall
proportion. First I multiplie one by the
other, the product is .72. that doubled is
144, this deuided by .18. which is the ad-
dition, of .6. and 12. geueth the quotient
8. so haue I .6. 8. 12. in musicall proporti-
on: And thus must you worke to fynde
out the thyrde in musicall proportion.
But if you had rather playe then
worke , this table folowing
shall serue your
torne.

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | 4 | 6 |
| 6 | 8 | 12 |
| 12 | 16 | 24 |
