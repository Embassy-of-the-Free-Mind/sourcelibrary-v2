# ¶ Of multiplex.

MULTIPLE proportion, is when a great number conteyneth a lesse number manye tymes, and leaueth nothinge, as . 8. conteyneth . 2 fower tymes and nothing remaineth. 16. conteineth. 4. &c, this proportiō semeth best to agree with roundes because the one number conteyneth the other and nothinge remaineeth as the fyrste order of roundes be.
