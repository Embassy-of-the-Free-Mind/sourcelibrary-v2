<sig>C.iij.</sig>
<meta>Catchword: The</meta>
<vocab>roundes, cossicall signes, roote, quadrate, triangles, Philosophers game, Rythmomachia</vocab>

The second order of roundes founde out as before, be not all marked with <term>cossicall signes</term>, but onely .4. and .9. with the <term>roote</term>, and .81. with the <term>quadrate</term>. The rest haue none, because amonge their aduersaries men there is none that can be cossicall roote to them in such maner as this game requireth.

The first order of triangles (hauyng the same numbers that haue bene taught before) do all lack the cossicall signes, except onely .6. which is signed with the roote.
