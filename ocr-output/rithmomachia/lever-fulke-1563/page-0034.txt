## Of the kings.

<image-desc>A woodcut illustration depicting two pyramidal game pieces (from the board game Rythmomachia) placed upon a grid of six squares. The pyramid on the left, which the text identifies as the "king of odde," consists of five tiers with the numbers 16, 25, 36, 49, and 64 visible on its faces. The pyramid on the right, the "king of euen," consists of six tiers with the numbers 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, and 36 visible on its faces.</image-desc>
