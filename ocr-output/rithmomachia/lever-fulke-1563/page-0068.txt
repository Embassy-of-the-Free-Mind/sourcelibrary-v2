<header>The Philosophers game.</header>
<meta>Catchword: The</meta>

**S**o .2. referred to .4. is a rote of a square, referred to .8. it is a roote of a cube. 2. referred to .16. is the roote of a foure squared quadrate. 2. referred to .32. is the rote of a surfolide. 2. referred to .64. is the rote of a quadrate of a cube. These numbers muste haue the proper cossicall signes. Also one number hauing diuers relations, may haue diuers cossicall signes, as 9. referred to .81. being roote, hathe the signe of a roote <term>℞</term>, but beyng referred to .3. it hath the signe of a quadrate, for it is a quadrate of .3. and is thus signed. <term>ℨ</term>. and so of the rest that haue like relation.

### ¶ The marking of the men.

**T**he first order of roundes in bothe nūbers, must haue the signe of the rote upō them al after this maner.
