## ¶ Of the partes of thys Game.

He that wyll learne thys game, any 
of the three waies, muste firste be 
enstructed of these sixe partes. The 
table as the fielde. 2. the menne and the 
numbers of them as the hoste. 3. the 
placynge of them, as the encampinge. 4. 
the order of playe and remouynge the 
men, as the marchynge and fyghtynge 
5. the manner and lawes of conqueryng 
and taking. 6. and last of al the triumphe 
after the victorie,
