### ¶ Of the placing, encamping or letting in araie.

To retorne againe to the plaine and
easye playing of this game, next to
the armie & their armour, follow
ether the order of their battel or encam-
ping. ¶ Whiche bicause it is more playne
and easely seen with the eye, then lear-
ned by the eare, I referre thee unto the
table where the battell is appoynted in
suche order as thys kynde of playe
requireth.
