## The Philosophers game.

as I woulde knowe what is the midle
number in proportion betwene .5. and
25. first I adde .5. to .25. that is .30. the half
of thirtie is .15. whiche is midle in pro-
portion betwene .5. and .25. so haue
I .5. 15. 25. in Arithme-
ticall propor-
tion.
