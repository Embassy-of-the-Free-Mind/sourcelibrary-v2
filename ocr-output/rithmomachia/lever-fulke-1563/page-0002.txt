<image-desc>A woodcut portrait of Robert Dudley, Earl of Leicester, depicted from the waist up. He is shown in three-quarter profile, wearing a flat hat, a starched ruff collar, and highly detailed plate armor decorated with patterns. He wears a heavy decorative chain with a prominent medallion. The portrait is set within an architectural oval frame adorned with scrollwork, acanthus leaves, and two winged cherubs at the upper corners. A blank, curled scroll lies across the bottom of the oval frame.</image-desc>
