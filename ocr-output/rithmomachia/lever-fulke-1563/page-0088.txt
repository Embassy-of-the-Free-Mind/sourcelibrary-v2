### ¶ Of takyng by deceypt or
### lying weyght.

To take by deceypt or lying weight,
is to take by addition, not as before
when the aduersary standeth with
in the draught of two men which being
added make the iuste number of the ad
uersary, but when the .2. numbers that
are to be added, stande in the next spaces
to the aduersarie. For to take by deceipt,
looke in the table that was set forth for
takyng by addition in the first kynde of
playinge.

### ¶ Of taking by Obsidion.

By Obsidion all men may be taken,
when foure men besiege the aduer
sarye, standynge in the foure nexte
