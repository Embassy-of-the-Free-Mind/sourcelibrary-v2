# The diffinition

<image-desc>Decorative drop cap 'T' with floral and foliate ornamentation.</image-desc>That moſte auncient and learned playe, called the Philoſophers game, beinge in Greeke termed <term>ρυθμομαχια</term> ,is as much to ſaye in Engliſhe, as the battell of numbers . Numbers be either euē or odde, wherfore the euen parte is againſt the odde, either parte hauinge a kyng, whych being taken of the aduerſaryes part, and a triumphe celebrated within his campe, the game is ended.
