# The Philosophers game.

## ¶ Of the greater tri-
## umphe.

**T**he greater victorie is, when foure
numbers be broughte together,
whiche agree in two proportions,
either Arithmeticall and Geometricall,
or elles Arithmeticall and Musicall, or
elles Geometricall and Musicall. Of
these three coniunctions the greater
triumph consisteth, of the which
the table folo-
sweth.
