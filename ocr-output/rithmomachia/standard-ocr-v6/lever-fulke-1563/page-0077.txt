### ¶ Of takyng by Multiplication.

Takyng by multiplication doth differ. For in this kynde of playng, it is thus. When your man standeth so, that beyng lesser then your aduersaries man, you may multiplie your man by the voyde spaces betwene them, and the product is all one to the aduersarye, you maye take hym up, not remouynge into his place, except you espye hym so, before you remoue your man.
