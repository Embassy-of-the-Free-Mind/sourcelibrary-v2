Euery number maye be taken for a rote, as .2. this number multiplyed in it self is a square as .4. The quadrate or square multiplied by the rote geueth a cube or solide square, as .4. multiplied by .2. geueth .8. that is a cube. Multiplie the cube by the roote, so haue you a squared quadrat, as .8. by .2. geueth 16. which is a quadrate of a quadrate. Multiplie the square or quadrat of quadrat by the rote, and the product is the sursolyde, as .2. tymes .16. is .32. whiche is a sursolide. Multiplie the sursolide by the rote, and the product is the quadrate of a cube, as .2. times .32. is .64. which is a quadrat of a cube. So haue you the rote quadrat, cube, quadrat of quadrat, sursolide, quadrat of cube .2. 4. 8. 16. 32. 64.
