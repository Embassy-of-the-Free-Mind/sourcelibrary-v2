# The bookes ver-
# dict.

VVanting I have bene long truly,
In English tongue many a day,
I do confesse it very truly,
Yet though I came late on way,
And playd my part in Latine verse,
Making men mery with my rehearse,
I care not now I do professe,
Unto the world in many a guyse,
I shew my selfe in English dresse,
VVho list my name to advertise,
And I am sure it is no sinne,
To speake in English to my kinne.
