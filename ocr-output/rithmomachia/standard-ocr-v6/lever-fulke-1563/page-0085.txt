## ¶ Of the order of the battell.

THe order of battell is after the firste
maner, but not so farre from the
bordes end, namely the 4. squares
standynge in the plattes nearest to the
bordes end the rest accordingly
->joyned to them, as in the<-
->firste kynde of<-
->playing.<-
