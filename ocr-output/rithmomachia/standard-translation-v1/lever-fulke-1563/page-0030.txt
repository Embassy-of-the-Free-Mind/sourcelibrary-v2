# Superpartient <note>original: "Superpartiens"</note> Proportion.

<image-desc>In the left margin, a square geometric diagram with internal diagonal lines and horizontal/vertical bisectors, illustrating the "corners and sides" mentioned in the text.</image-desc>

The superpartient proportion occurs when the greater number contains the lesser number plus more than one part of it. For example, 15 contains 9 and 6, where 6 is two-thirds of 9. Likewise, 28 contains 16 and 12, which is 3/4 of 16. This proportion contains various <note>original: "diverse"</note> parts besides the whole number; therefore, it is well represented by the square, which also contains more corners and sides.
