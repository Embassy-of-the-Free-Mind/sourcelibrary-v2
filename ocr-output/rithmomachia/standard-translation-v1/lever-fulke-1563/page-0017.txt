# The Definition

That most ancient and learned game, called the Philosopher’s game, being termed in Greek *Rythmomachia* <note>original: "ρυθμομαχια"</note>, is as much to say in English as "the battle of numbers." Numbers are either even or odd; therefore, the even side is against the odd, each side having a king. When the king is taken by the adversary’s side and a triumph is celebrated within his camp, the game is ended.
