So, 2 compared <note>original: "referred"</note> to 4 is the root <note>original: "rote"</note> of a square; compared to 8, it is the root of a cube. 2 compared to 16 is the root of a fourth power <note>original: "foure squared quadrate"</note>. 2 compared to 32 is the root of a fifth power <note>original: "surfolide"</note>. 2 compared to 64 is the root of a sixth power <note>original: "quadrate of a cube"</note>. These numbers must have the proper algebraic <note>original: "cossicall"</note> signs. Also, one number having various <note>original: "diuers"</note> relationships may have various algebraic signs; for example, 9 compared to 81—being the root—has the sign of a root (℞), but when compared to 3, it has the sign of a square <note>original: "quadrate"</note>, for it is the square of 3 and is signed thus (ℨ). The same applies to the rest that have similar relationships.
