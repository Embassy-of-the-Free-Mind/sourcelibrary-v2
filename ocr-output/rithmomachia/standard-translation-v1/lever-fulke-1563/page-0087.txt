# The Philosopher's Game

## Of Their Movements

The pieces <note>original: "men"</note> move forward and backward, to the right, and to the left, but not diagonally <note>original: "cornerwise"</note>, unless the players <note>original: "gamesters"</note> agree otherwise. The circles <note>original: "rounds"</note> move into the next space, the triangles into the third space, and the squares into the fourth. The kings move like squares. And these are their ordinary moves <note>original: "draughts"</note> while marching.

## Of Capturing Pieces <note>original: "taking"</note>

They are captured by encountering, by eruption, by laying weight, and by siege <note>original: "Obsidion"</note>.

## Of Capturing by Encountering

To capture by encountering is to capture by equality, as has been twice declared before.

## Of Capturing by Eruption

To capture by eruption is when a smaller <note>original: "lesse"</note> number is multiplied by the number of spaces between it and its adversary, and the resulting product is equal to the adversary's value. In this case, it may capture its enemy whether the enemy stands directly in front of it or diagonally <note>original: "cornerwise"</note>.
