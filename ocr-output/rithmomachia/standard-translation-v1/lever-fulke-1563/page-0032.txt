<summary>
This page details the "second order" of pieces used in the Philosophers' Game (Rythmomachia). It focuses on the "superpartient" ratios, which are complex arithmetic proportions used to determine the values of the square and triangular pieces. The table illustrates four specific ratios: superbipartient (5:3), superquadripartient (9:5), supersextupartient (13:7), and superoctupartient (17:9). The resulting values (such as 15, 45, 91, 153 and 25, 81, 169, 289) represent the numerical identities of the game pieces within this specific class.
