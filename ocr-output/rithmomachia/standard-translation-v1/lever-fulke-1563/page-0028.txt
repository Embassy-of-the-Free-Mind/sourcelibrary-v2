# The Philosophers Game.

## Of superparticular proportion.

Superparticular <note>original: "superparticulier"</note> proportion occurs when a larger number contains a smaller number plus one part of that smaller number, which serves as a common measure for the whole. For example, 12 contains 9 plus 3 (which is a third part of 9). Similarly, 6 contains 4 plus 2 (which is one-half of 4). 

This proportion, being the most significant next to multiple <note>original: "multipler"</note> proportion, is best represented by a triangular shape <note>original: "trianguler forme"</note>, which has the fewest lines and angles next to a circle.
