### Of capturing <note>original: "takyng"</note> by Multiplication.

Capturing by multiplication differs from the others. In this kind of play, it works like this: when your piece <note>original: "man"</note> stands so that, being smaller than your opponent's <note>original: "aduersaries"</note> piece, you may multiply your piece by the empty <note>original: "voyde"</note> spaces between them. If the product is equal to <note>original: "all one to"</note> the opponent's piece, you may capture <note>original: "take"</note> him. You do this without moving into his space <note>original: "place"</note>, unless you see <note>original: "espye"</note> him there before you move your piece.
