<summary>
This page is the title leaf for the second part of Sir Walter Raleigh’s "The History of the World." Published posthumously in 1631, this edition was printed in London by John Haviland for the bookseller James Boler, whose shop was located in St. Paul's Churchyard. The page features a prominent woodcut printer’s device associated with the reign of King James I (Iacobus Rex), symbolizing the union of the English (Tudor rose) and Spanish (pomegranate) houses, though by 1631 it served as a standard decorative or heraldic mark for the print shop. The page also bears the ownership stamp of the British Museum from the collection of King George III.
</summary>
