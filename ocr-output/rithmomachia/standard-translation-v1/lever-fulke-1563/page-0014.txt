# To the Reader.

...that I thought these instructions sufficient for those to whom I have written. Yet, because I would be reluctant <note>original: "lothe"</note> to fall from play and gaming into serious conflict <note>original: "earnest contention"</note>, if any man thinks I have done wrong in this work or any part of it, and wishes to do better himself, I am so far from envying his good progress <note>original: "proceding"</note> that I

->will be very glad and<-
->give him hearty<-
->thanks for<-
->it.<-

All things belonging to this game
at a reasonable price <note>original: "for reason"</note> you may buy:
At the bookshop under Bow Church <note>original: "Bochurch"</note>,
In Cheapside <note>original: "Chepesyde"</note> readily.
