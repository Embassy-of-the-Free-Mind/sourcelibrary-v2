->**The Order of the Field and the Arrangement of the Armies**<-
<note>original: "Instructio Aciei et Depositio Exercituum"</note>

<summary>
This page provides a visual diagram of the board for Rithmomachia (The Philosophers' Game), illustrating the initial setup or "encamping" of the two opposing sides. The board is an 8x16 grid. One side (the "Even" side) is positioned at the top, and the other (the "Odd" side) is at the bottom. The diagram shows the specific placement of circles, triangles, squares, and the complex pyramid pieces, each inscribed with mathematical values that determine their movement and capture abilities during play.
</summary>
