<summary>
This page details a further set of mathematical proportions for the pieces in the Philosophers' Game (Rythmomachia). It focuses on the "superpartient" ratios, specifically those where the numerator exceeds the denominator by more than one (e.g., 7/4, 11/6, 15/8, and 19/10). The tables and diagrams provide the numerical values for the resulting game pieces, which include the numbers 28, 66, 120, and 190 (derived from multiplying the base ratio by the denominator) and their squares (49, 121, 225, and 361).
</summary>
