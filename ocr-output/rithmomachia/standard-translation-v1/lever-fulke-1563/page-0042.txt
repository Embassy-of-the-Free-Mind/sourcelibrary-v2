### Of Capture by Addition

When two pieces <note>original: "numbers"</note> are positioned so that they encounter one of their enemies whose value is equal <note>original: "as muche as"</note> to both of them added together, and they are standing in such positions that both could move into its space, they shall capture <note>original: "take hym up"</note> it without moving into its space as soon as the second of those two pieces is set down.

<summary>
This section concludes the explanation of "Capture by Siege" (oblivion) and introduces the "Capture by Addition" rule in Rythmomachia. Capture by Addition occurs when two pieces are positioned such that their combined numerical values equal the value of an opposing piece, and both pieces are within legal moving distance of that opponent's space. The capture is completed as soon as the second attacking piece is placed. The text also notes a variation where an attacking piece might actually move into the captured piece's square if the threat was already established.
