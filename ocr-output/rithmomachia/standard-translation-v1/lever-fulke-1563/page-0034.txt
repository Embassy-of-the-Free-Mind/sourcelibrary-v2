## Of the Kings.

<summary>
This section explains the composition of the "King" pieces in the Philosophers' Game (Rythmomachia). These pieces are "pyramidal numbers," formed by stacking a series of square numbers. The "King of Even" is a pyramid with a base of 36 (6x6), totaling 91 when the smaller squares (25, 16, 9, 4, and 1) are added. The "King of Odd" begins with a larger base of 64 (8x8) and follows the same cumulative logic.
</summary>
