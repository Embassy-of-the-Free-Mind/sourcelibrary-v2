### The setting in array.

The teachers of this kind of playing do not favor <note>original: "allowe"</note> the former method of placement, or any other, as much as the natural placing of every piece under the one from which it arises. So they consist of <note>original: "conteyne"</note> six ranks in length, extending to the furthest edge of the board <note>original: "Table"</note> in this manner <note>original: "sorte"</note>.
