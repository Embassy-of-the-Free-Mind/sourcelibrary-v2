# The Philosopher's Game

The board <note>original: "table"</note> must be a plain board containing 128 squares—that is, 8 in breadth and 16 in length—set forth in two diverse <note>original: "dyuerſe"</note> colors. Or for a plainer understanding, the board is a double chessboard, as if it were two chessboards joined together: the length of two and the breadth of one, of which this is an example:
