# To the Reader

man of severe judgment,
as soon as he
has once read the title
of this book, will
immediately say that I had more
need to encourage <note>original: "exhort"</note> men to work,

<summary>In this preface, the author anticipates that stern critics may question why he has written a book about "play" (games) rather than "work." He acknowledges this potential criticism, stating that unless it stems from a naturally grumpy or narrow-minded disposition, he accepts the point. He expresses that he would gladly write a much longer work if he believed his words could effectively encourage people to be more diligent in their labors.</summary>
