# The Philosophers' Game.

## Of capture <note>original: "takyng"</note> by division.

By division, a piece <note>original: "manne"</note> may be captured when two of its enemies are positioned so that, when one of them is divided by the other, the result <note>original: "product"</note> is the same as the enemy piece standing within their range <note>original: "draught"</note>. Immediately after the second piece is placed, the enemy may be removed. If the enemy was left in danger before being removed, one of the attacking pieces may move into its place. 

For example: Circle 4 <note>original: "round 4"</note> standing in **D** and Triangle 20 standing in **F** may capture the adversary 5 standing in **A** by division, because 4 is contained in 20 five times. Another example: Circle 5 standing in **B** and Triangle 30 standing in **F** may capture their enemy 6 standing in **A**, for 5 is contained in 30 six times.
