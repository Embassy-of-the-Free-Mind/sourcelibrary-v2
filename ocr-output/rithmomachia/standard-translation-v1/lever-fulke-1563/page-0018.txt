## Of the parts of this game.

He who will learn this game in any of the three ways must first be instructed in these six parts: 
1. The table as the field. 
2. The pieces <note>original: "menne"</note> and their numbers as the army <note>original: "hoste"</note>. 
3. The placing of them as the encamping. 
4. The order of play and moving <note>original: "remouynge"</note> the pieces, as the marching and fighting. 
5. The manner and laws of conquering and capturing. 
6. And last of all, the triumph after the victory.
