## ¶ Regarding the Triumph <note>original: "Of the triumph"</note>

The triumph occurs after the King has been completely <note>original: "cleane"</note> captured. It is created in the adversary's camp using your own men as well as the pieces captured from your adversary, or both, in the proportions that have been shown before. It is proclaimed that once those men are placed, they may not be captured, as was sufficiently declared previously. There is no difference between the triumphs, except <note>original: "sauyng"</note> that some will not allow a triumph unless it consists of at least <note>original: "lest"</note> four numbers and two proportions. All three proportions are required for the greater victory, making only two kinds of triumphs.
