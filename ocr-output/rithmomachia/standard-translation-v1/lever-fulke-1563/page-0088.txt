### Of Capturing by Deceit or Lying Weight <note>original: "deceypt or lying weyght"</note>

To capture by deceit or lying weight is to capture by addition. This is not like the method described before, where the adversary stands within the move <note>original: "draught"</note> of two pieces which, when added together, make the exact <note>original: "iuste"</note> number of the adversary. Instead, it is when the two numbers that are to be added stand in the spaces immediately adjacent <note>original: "next spaces"</note> to the adversary. To capture by deceit, look in the table that was set forth for capturing by addition in the first kind of playing.

This page continues the rules for Rithmomachia (The Philosopher's Game), specifically focusing on specialized capture methods. It refers the reader to previous tables for "eruption" (multiplication-based capture) and clarifies the "deceit or lying weight" method. Unlike standard addition captures where pieces move to a spot to capture, "deceit" involves positioning two pieces in the spaces immediately adjacent to an opponent's piece so their sum equals the opponent's value. Finally, it introduces "Obsidion" (siege), where a piece is captured by being completely surrounded on four sides.
