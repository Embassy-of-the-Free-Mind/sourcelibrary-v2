<summary>
This page details the specific numerical requirements for achieving a "Victory of Standards" in the Philosopher's Game (Rithmomachia). Because the even and odd armies have different total values, the number of captured points required to claim a "standard" differs for each side. For the even army to win standards, they must capture the higher-value pieces of the odd army (at 174 points per standard). Conversely, the odd army needs only 130 points to claim one of the even army's standards. A reference table is provided for calculating the even army's standards in increments of 130.
