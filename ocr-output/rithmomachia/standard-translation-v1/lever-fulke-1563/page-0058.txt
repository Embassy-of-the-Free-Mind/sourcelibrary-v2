# Of Musical Proportion

Musical proportion <note>original: "Musicall proportion"; this is now commonly known as harmonic proportion or the harmonic mean</note> occurs when the sum of the differences between the first and middle numbers and the middle and last numbers is the same as the difference between the first and the last. For example: in the sequence 3, 4, 6, the difference between 3 and 4 is 1, and the difference between 4 and 6 is 2. The total difference is 3, which is the same as the difference between the first number (3) and the last (6).

## A rule to find the first number when you have the last two.

Multiply the second number by the third and divide the product by the sum of the difference <note>original: "distans"</note> and the third number; the quotient is the first number. For example, having 6 and 12, I would find the first. 6 times 12 is 72. The difference between 6 and 12 is 6, which, when added to 12, is 18. Divide 72 by 18, and the quotient is 4. Thus, you have 4, 6, and 12 in musical proportion.
