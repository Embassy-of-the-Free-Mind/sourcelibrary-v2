<summary>
This page continues the reference tables for Rithmomachia (The Philosopher's Game), specifically focusing on captures made through division. A capture occurs when a piece's value, divided by the number of empty spaces between it and an opponent's piece, equals the value of the capturing piece (or vice-versa). The table includes a section for "cossic signs," which provides a quick reference for capturing square pieces using their square roots. For instance, a piece with a value of 4 can capture a piece with a value of 16 if there are 4 spaces between them (16 divided by 4 equals 4).
</summary>
