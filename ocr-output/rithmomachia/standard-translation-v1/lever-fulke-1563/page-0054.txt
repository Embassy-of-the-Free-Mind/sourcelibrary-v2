## The Philosophers' Game <note>original: "The Philosophers game"</note>.

For example, if I would know what the middle <note>original: "midle"</note> number is in proportion between 5 and 25: first, I add 5 to 25, which is 30. Half of thirty is 15, which is the middle value in proportion between 5 and 25. Thus, I have 5, 15, and 25 in arithmetical <note>original: "Arithmeticall"</note> proportion.
