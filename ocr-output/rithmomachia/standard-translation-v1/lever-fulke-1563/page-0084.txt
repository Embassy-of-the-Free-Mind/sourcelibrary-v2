# The Philosopher's <note>original: "Philosophers"</note> Game.

In this third kind <note>original: "kynde"</note> of play, one must also consider the table, the pieces <note>original: "men"</note>, their markings <note>original: "marking"</note>, the order of their battle, the movements <note>original: "motions"</note>, their capture <note>original: "taking"</note>, and finally their triumphing.

The table is the same that has been <note>original: "hath bene"</note> described twice already. Yet some will not have it so long; but at the least <note>original: "lest"</note>, it must contain 10 squares in length and always 8 in breadth <note>original: "breadeth"</note>. The longest board is best.

## ¶ Regarding the pieces <note>original: "Of the men"</note>

There are 48 pieces <note>original: "men be .48."</note>, as has been said <note>original: "told"</note>, of two opposing colors. The top <note>original: "head"</note> and bottom are all of one color because pieces once <note>original: "ons"</note> taken are no longer used <note>original: "occupyed"</note> in this kind of play.
