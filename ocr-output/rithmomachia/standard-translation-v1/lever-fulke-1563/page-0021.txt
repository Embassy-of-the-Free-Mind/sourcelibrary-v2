The Philosopher's Game.

## Of the Pieces <note>original: "men"</note>

The pieces are 48 in number. 24 are on one side and must be known by one color, and 24 are on the other side, which also must be marked with a contrary color, such as white and black, blue and red, or whatever other colors you like best. But in the coloring, these three things must be observed: the bottom or lower part of every piece (except the two kings) must be marked with its adversary's color, so that when it is captured, it may change its coat <note>original: "coate"</note> and serve the one to whom it is a prisoner.

The second thing considered in the pieces is their shape <note>original: "fashion"</note>. On either side, eight are circles <note>original: "roundes"</note>, another eight are triangles, and seven (the king making eight) are squares. Their shapes are such: circles, triangles, squares.
