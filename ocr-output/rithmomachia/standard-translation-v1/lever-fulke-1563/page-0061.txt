# The Philosophers' Game.

## Of the Greater Triumph.

**T**he greater victory is when four numbers are brought together which agree in two proportions: either Arithmetical and Geometrical, or else Arithmetical and Musical, or else Geometrical and Musical. The greater triumph consists of these three combinations <note>original: "coniunctions"</note>, of which the table follows.
