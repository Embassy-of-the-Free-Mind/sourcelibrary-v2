The circle <note>original: "rounde"</note> 2 standing in **A** may be captured <note>original: "taken"</note> by the triangle 30 standing in **B** and the square 28 standing in **B**; for if you take 28 out of 30, there remains 2.

### Of Capture <note>original: "takynge"</note> by Multiplication

When two numbers are positioned so that, when multiplied by one another, the product is equal <note>original: "all one"</note> to their opponent's piece <note>original: "man"</note> standing within the range <note>original: "draughts"</note> of both pieces, they may capture <note>original: "take"</note> that piece as soon as the second attacking piece is placed. 

And if they are already positioned that way before a move—having been left so by the opponent—one of the capturing pieces shall move into the square <note>original: "succede in his place"</note> of the piece that is captured, as in this example: 

The circle <note>original: "rounde"</note> 3 stands in **D** and the 5 stands in **C**; these two shall capture the square 15 standing in **A**, because three times five is 15. In another example: the circle <note>original: "rounde"</note> 2 standing in **B** and the triangle 6 standing in **I** shall capture their enemy’s triangle 12 standing in **A** by multiplication, for 2 times 6 is 12.
