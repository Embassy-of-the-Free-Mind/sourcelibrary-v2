# The Philosophers game.

The shape <note>original: "fashion"</note> of all three kinds: its foundations are two squares, upon which are set two triangles, and upon them rounds. But this difference is between the kings: that the king of the even numbers has a pointed top, while the king of the odd numbers is not pointed. The reason depends upon the consideration of their numbers, by which they arise into pyramidal shapes <note>original: "fashion"</note>.

The third thing considered in the pieces <note>original: "men"</note> is the number that must be written or engraved <note>original: "graue"</note> upon them. To learn these clearly for practice, note these short rules.

There are two ranks or orders of each kind of piece <note>original: "men"</note>.

The first rank or order of circles <note>original: "roundes"</note> are the digits, even or odd. Namely, of the even: 2, 4, 6, 8; and of the odd: 3, 5, 7, 9.

The second order of circles <note>original: "rounds"</note> are found by multiplying these digits by themselves—as 2 times 2 is 4, and 3 times 3 is 9. For the even, they are 4, 16, 36, 64; for the odd, they are 9, 25, 49, 81.

The first order of the triangles are found by adding two of the circles <note>original: "roundes"</note> together
