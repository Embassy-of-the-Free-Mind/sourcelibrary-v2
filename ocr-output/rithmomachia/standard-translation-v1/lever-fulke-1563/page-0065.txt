And thus, the first kind of playing is at an end. This is sufficient <note>original: "sufficiēt"</note> to teach you how to play; however, if you would learn to play skillfully <note>original: "conningly"</note>, you must practice playing often. In this way, you shall learn better than by any precepts or rules.

## Of the second kind of playing the Philosophers' Game.

In this kind of playing, there are several things to be considered <note>original: "cōfidered"</note>: the table, the pieces <note>original: "men"</note>, the marking of them, the setting of them in array, their movement <note>original: "marching"</note>, their rules <note>original: "lawes"</note> for capturing <note>original: "taking"</note>, and the manner of triumphing.

### Of the Table.

The table is the same as was first described: namely, a double chessboard.
