## The Philosopher's Game.

## The manner of capturing <note>original: "takyng"</note>.

The pieces <note>original: "men"</note> may be taken seven ways: by **Siege** <note>original: "Obsidion"</note>, by **Equality**, by **Addition**, by **Subtraction**, by **Multiplication**, by **Division**, and by **Algebraic Signs** <note>original: "Cossicall Signes"</note>.

### Of capturing <note>original: "takynge"</note> by Siege <note>original: "Obsidion"</note>.

All pieces <note>original: "men"</note> may be taken by **Siege** <note>original: "Obsidion"</note> when, by four pieces, they are blocked <note>original: "letted"</note> from their ordinary move <note>original: "draughte"</note>, as has been taught before.
