### Regarding the Triumph <note>original: "triumphe"</note>

To take away the tediousness of long play from those who are young beginners, writers of this game have invented diverse kinds of short victories; therefore, they divide <note>original: "deuise"</note> victory into "proper" and "common." Nothing needs to be said here regarding the proper victory, for all things belonging to it are sufficiently set forth in the first kind of playing.

### Regarding the Common Victory

The common victory (they say) occurs in five ways <note>original: "maners"</note>, for men contend either for bodies, goods, quarrels <note>original: "quarelles"</note>, honor, or else for both quarrels and honor.
