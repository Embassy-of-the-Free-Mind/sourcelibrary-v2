# THE MOST NOBLE
## ancient and learned game <note>original: "playe"</note>, called the Philosophers' Game, invented for the honest recreation of students and other serious <note>original: "sober"</note> persons, to pass the tediousness of time, for the relief of their labors <note>original: "release of their labours"</note>, and the exercise of their wits.

->Presented <note>original: "Set forth"</note> with such clear rules <note>original: "playne precepts"</note>, and tables, that all men may understand it with ease, and most men may practice it with pleasure.<-

->by Ralph Lever <note>original: "Rafe Leuer"</note> and expanded <note>original: "augmented"</note> by W. F.<-
