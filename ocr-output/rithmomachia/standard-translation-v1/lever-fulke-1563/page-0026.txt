# ¶ Of Multiple Proportion <note>original: "multiplex"</note>.

Multiple proportion is when a larger <note>original: "great"</note> number contains a smaller <note>original: "lesse"</note> number several <note>original: "manye"</note> times and leaves no remainder <note>original: "nothinge"</note>. For example, 8 contains 2 four times, and nothing remains. Likewise, 16 contains 4, and so forth. 

This proportion seems to best agree with the circles <note>original: "roundes"</note> because one number contains the other and nothing remains, just as the first set <note>original: "order"</note> of circles are numbered.

| (Even circles) | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **(Odd circles)** | **3** | **5** | **7** | **9** |
