### Of the Squares.

The Squares move into the fourth place from their starting position; that is, leaping over two spaces, either straight forward or sideways, such as from the position of A to any of these spaces: L, M, P, or R. When "flying," they may move according to the Knight's move, except they must pass over four spaces, such as from D to Y or Z, etc. This concludes the instructions for the marching and moving of the pieces <note>original: "men"</note>. Note that with their "flying" move they cannot capture <note>original: "take"</note> any piece, but if necessary, they may help to besiege a piece.

### Of the kings' marching.

The kings, because they bear the form of all three kinds of pieces, may move in any direction <note>original: "way"</note>.
