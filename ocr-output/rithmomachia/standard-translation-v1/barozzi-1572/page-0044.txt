In a *Geometric* proportion (last column), the numbers have a constant ratio (e.g., 2, 4, 8 each double the previous number). Identifying these "combined" sets was crucial for achieving the highest levels of victory in Rithmomachia, where a player had to arrange captured pieces into specific mathematical sequences.]]
