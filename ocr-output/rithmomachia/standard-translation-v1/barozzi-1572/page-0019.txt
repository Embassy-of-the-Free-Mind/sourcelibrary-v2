Just as by joining the numbers of the first four white triangles with the numbers of the first four black rounds, the numbers of the four second white triangles were generated: so, by joining the numbers of the four first black triangles already found with the numbers of the first white rounds, the numbers of the four second black triangles will be produced. 

Noting, however, that you must leave aside the number of the first white round, which is 2, and begin from 4—joining the 4 with the 12, the 6 with the 30, and the 8 with the 56. And because one is missing, since we left out the 2, it is necessary to add 10 (which follows in the order of even numbers after 8), joining this 10 with the 90. 

In this way, from the 12 joined with the 4, 16 will be created; and from 30 with 6, 36; and from 56 with 8, 64; and from 90 with 10, 100. And thus we will have found the numbers that are painted upon the other four black triangles, as seen in the following figure.

[[notes: The author is describing a specific arithmetic progression used to determine piece values. By adding a base even number (4, 6, 8, 10) to the "first" triangle values (12, 30, 56, 90), the player identifies the "second" triangle values (16, 36, 64, 100), which modern readers will recognize as the squares of 4, 6, 8, and 10.]]
