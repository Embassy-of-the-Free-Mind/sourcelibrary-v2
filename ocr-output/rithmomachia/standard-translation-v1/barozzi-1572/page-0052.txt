The fifth rule is that when the second-to-last piece is moved to achieve the final **victory**, it must be proclaimed [[notes: Similar to calling "Check" in Chess, this warns the opponent that the game is nearing its end.]], to warn the enemy to remedy the situation if he can; so that later, when he can no longer remedy it, you may honorably move the last piece that completes the final victory.

The sixth rule is that once the final victory has been proclaimed, one can no longer take [[notes: "Prender" (to take/capture) refers to removing an opponent's piece from the board via the game's capture mechanics.]] any of the pieces that are already arranged and positioned to achieve it.
