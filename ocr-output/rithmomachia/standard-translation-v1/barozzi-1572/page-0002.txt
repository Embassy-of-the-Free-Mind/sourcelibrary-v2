# LIBRARY OF PRINCETON UNIVERSITY

### PRESENTED BY
#### THE ESTATE OF

## EUGENE B. COOK

### CLASS OF 1850

[[notes: Eugene B. Cook (1830–1915) was a renowned American master of chess problems and a dedicated bibliophile. A graduate of the Princeton Class of 1850, he amassed one of the largest chess libraries in the world. Upon his death, his collection of over 3,000 volumes was bequeathed to Princeton University, forming the foundation of their specialized chess holdings.]]
