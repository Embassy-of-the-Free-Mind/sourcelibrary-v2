[[notes: In the context of Rithmomachia, "Musical" refers to the **Harmonic Proportion**. A set of numbers is in harmonic proportion if the ratio of the differences between the numbers is the same as the ratio of the outer numbers themselves (for example, 3, 4, 6 is harmonic because 4-3=1 and 6-4=2, and the ratio 1:2 is the same as the ratio 3:6).]]
