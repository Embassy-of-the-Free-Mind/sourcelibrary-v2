Having now found the numbers that were to be written upon the white squares, there remains to find those which must be written upon the black ones. Wishing, therefore, to find the numbers of the first 4 black squares, we will operate in the way that we did when we wanted to find the numbers of the first 4 white squares: that is, we will **join** the four first black triangles with the four second black triangles, and we will have the numbers we desire, which will be these: **28, 66, 120, 190**.

This is because from 12 joined [[notes: The author uses *accozzato*, meaning to couple or join together, to describe the addition of two numbers]] with 16, **28** is born; from 30 with 36, **66**; from 56 with 64, **120**; and from 90 with 100, **190**, as seen in the following figure.

[[notes: In Rythmomachia, "Squares" are one of the three main ranks of pieces (alongside Circles and Triangles). Their values are not arbitrary but are derived through specific ratios. For the black side, these Square values are created by summing the values of the previously established Triangle pieces.]]
