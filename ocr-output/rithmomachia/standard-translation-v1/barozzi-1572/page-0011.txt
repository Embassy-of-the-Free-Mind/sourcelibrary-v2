# PART ONE
## IN WHICH IS DESCRIBED
### THE BOARD,
### AND THE PIECES.

The Board [[notes: The Italian text uses *Tauoliero* (or *Tavoliere*), a general term for a game board, here specifically referring to the unique Rythmomachia grid.]] must be made similar to the chess-
board as far as the white and black squares, into
which it is entirely divided; but as for the shape,
it is different from it, as this must be con-
structed twice as long as it is wide, that is,
8 squares wide, and 16 long, as if two chessboards were joined
together [[notes: While a standard chessboard is an 8x8 square, the "Philosopher's Game" requires more space for the complex numerical maneuvers and the "Great Victory" progressions.]] which is clearly seen in the fol-
lowing figure.

The grid consists of alternating light and dark squares in a checkered pattern, measuring 8 squares in width and 16 squares in height, which the text describes as the equivalent of two chessboards joined together.</image-desc>
