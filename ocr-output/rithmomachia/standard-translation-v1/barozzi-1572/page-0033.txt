By multiplication and by division, pieces are captured in this manner. If two pieces are found—one white and the other black—in such a position that between them there are as many squares [[notes: *Caſelle* refers to the squares on the board.]] as the number of times the value of one is found within the value of the other [[notes: This refers to the mathematical ratio between the two pieces.]]: then the movement of these two can take the larger piece prisoner by multiplication, or be captured by the smaller one by division.
