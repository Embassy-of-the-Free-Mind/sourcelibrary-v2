...which contains within itself many other games, and does not diminish the delight of those, but increases it and makes it more perfect; and not only provides delight and utility to the unlearned and the **rough** [[notes: *Rozi*—meaning those without formal education or simple-minded.]], but also to the **learned** and **subtle minds**. Therefore, no one should hesitate to embrace it and acquire it, to excite one's mind from playful things to more serious and grave matters; and to recreate [[notes: *Recrearla*—in the sense of refreshing or restoring the mind's vigor through "recreation."]] it at times when it feels fatigued and tired from studies.
