...the first white round piece [[notes: *Tondi* or "rounds" are the most basic pieces in Rithmomachia, equivalent to pawns but with specific mathematical values.]], which is the 2, should be placed; and immediately after that, the 4, toward the front part of the board; and after the 4, let the 6 be placed; and after the 6, the 8. Beneath which first 4 white round pieces you will place the other 4 "second" white round pieces according to the order you followed in producing their numbers—that is: beneath the 2, the 4; beneath the 4, the 16; beneath the 6, the 36; and beneath the 8, the 64 [[notes: The "second" row is derived mathematically from the first; here, the second row consists of the squares of the numbers in the first row (2²=4, 4²=16, etc.).]]. Then beneath these you will place the 4 first white triangles, and beneath these the 4 second ones, and beneath these the 4 first white squares. And finally, beneath these, the other 4 second squares, always observing in all of them the order you maintained when producing their numbers. And the same shall be done with the black pieces on the other side of the board. Taking care to place the two pyramids [[notes: The Pyramid is the most complex piece in Rithmomachia, made up of a stack of different shapes (rounds, triangles, and squares) whose values add up to the Pyramid's total.]] in those places where the two squares would go that are inscribed with the same numbers that the pyramids have beneath their bases, as can clearly be seen in the following figure.
