[[notes: A "Geometric proportion" (or progression) is a sequence of three numbers where the ratio between the first and the second is the same as the ratio between the second and the third. For example, in the row "2, 4, 8," each number is multiplied by 2 to get the next. In the more complex "4, 6, 9," the ratio is 1.5 (4 x 1.5 = 6, and 6 x 1.5 = 9). In the game of Rithmomachia, capturing or arranging pieces to form these sequences allowed a player to prove their mathematical mastery.]]
