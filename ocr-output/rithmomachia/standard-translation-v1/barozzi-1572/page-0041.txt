[[notes: An "Arithmetic proportion" (or progression) in this context is a sequence of three numbers where the difference between the first and the second is equal to the difference between the second and the third. For example, in the row "28, 42, 56," the difference is consistently 14. These numbers correspond to the values assigned to the different game pieces—Circles, Triangles, and Squares—used in Rithmomachia.]]
