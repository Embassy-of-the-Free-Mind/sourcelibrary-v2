Having already determined the numbers to be inscribed upon the 16 circular pieces, it follows that we must also find those that should be written upon the triangles. Beginning, then, with the white ones, we shall take the first two white rounds that stand one above the other—namely, the 2 and the 4 that is born from it—and we shall join them together [[notes: In Rythmomachia, the "minor" or triangular pieces are created through a specific ratio (superparticularis). Here, the author simplifies the rule: the triangle's value is the sum of a base number and its square (n + n²).]], and we shall produce 6, which shall be the first number to be written upon the first white triangle. Similarly, by joining the two second white rounds—that is, the 4 and the 16—the number 20 will be born, which shall be the number for the second white triangle. Likewise, from the joining of the 6 and the 36 (the third white rounds in order), the number 42 will be produced for the third white triangle.
