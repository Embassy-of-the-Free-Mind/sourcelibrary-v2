Similarly, if you wish to find the numbers that are written upon the **black triangles**, you shall join the numbers of the **black rounds** together, in the same way you did with those of the white rounds. By doing so, you will easily produce the numbers for the first four **black triangles**, which will be these: 12, 30, 56, and 90. These are produced by combining the first four black rounds with the second four; that is, from 3 with 9 comes 12; from 5 with 25 comes 30; from 7 with 49 comes 56; and from 9 with 81 comes 90. Thus, you shall have the numbers for the first four black triangles, as seen in the following figure.
