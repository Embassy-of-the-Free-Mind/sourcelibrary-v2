# Front Cover

The volume is bound in a historical **marbled paper** featuring a "stone" or "shell" pattern. This decorative technique was created by floating pigments on a liquid bath—often made of carrageenan moss—and transferring the floating design onto a sheet of paper. This specific pattern shows dark "stones" outlined with striking veins of vibrant blue and tan.

[[notes: Marbling was a standard practice in hand-binding from the 17th through the 19th centuries, providing a durable and aesthetically pleasing surface that helped hide wear and tear.]]
