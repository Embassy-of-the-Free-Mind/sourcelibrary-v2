And each of these 48 pieces you shall have painted white on one side and black on the other, and let them be the thickness of a finger. Then you shall have two graduated pyramids constructed, one of five and the other of six levels [[notes: The author uses the word *gradi*, meaning steps or degrees, to describe the stacked layers of these special pieces]], the base or foundation of which shall be a square the size of one of the aforementioned 16 square pieces—indeed, one of those very same pieces. Above this square shall be placed another square of equal height to the previous one, but of lesser width and length. And upon this last square let there be added an equilateral triangle smaller than said last square; upon which triangle another smaller equilateral triangle is placed; and upon the last triangle let a round piece be added, similar to one of the 16 round pieces, but smaller than the last triangle placed in the pyramids. 

So that, up to this point, two graduated pyramids of five levels each will have been constructed—that is, of two squares, two triangles, and one round. But so that one of them becomes six levels, let another smaller round be added above the last round level of one of them, so that in this way one pyramid will be made of six levels. And take care to join these levels together with such artifice [[notes: Here, *artificio* refers to a clever design or mechanical trick]] that those who are playing can remove any level they wish at will, and that the others remain one on top of the other in their proper order [[notes: In Rythmomachia, the pyramids are the most powerful pieces. They are made of detachable layers because, during gameplay, they can be "captured" in parts or lose their value as specific numbers are eliminated]]. Furthermore, each of these levels shall be painted white on one side and black on the other, and thus you will have all 48 pieces used in this game: namely 16 rounds, 16 triangles, and 16 squares—counting the two pyramids as two squares, since they have as their bases two of the 16 squares.
