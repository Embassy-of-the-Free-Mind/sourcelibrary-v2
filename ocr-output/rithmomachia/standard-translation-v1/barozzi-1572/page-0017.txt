Having found, then, the four numbers for the first four white triangles, we shall also find those that must be written upon the other four, if we join the four already found white triangles with the first four black rounds in order: the first white triangle with the first black round, the second with the second, the third with the third, and the fourth with the fourth. That is, the 6 with the 3, the 20 with the 5, the 42 with the 7, and the 72 with the 9. For in this way, we shall produce the four numbers that must be written upon the other four white triangles, which will be these: 9, 25, 49, 81, as the following figure shows us.

Note that 9, 25, 49, and 81 are the squares of the odd numbers 3, 5, 7, and 9.]]
