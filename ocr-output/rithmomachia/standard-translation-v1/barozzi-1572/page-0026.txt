[[notes: **Shape Legend:**
- **Bold numbers** (Rows 1-2 and 15-16) represent **Squares** (*Quadrati*).
- *Italicized numbers* (Rows 3-4 and 13-14) represent **Triangles** (*Triangoli*).
- Standard numbers (Rows 5-6 and 11-12) represent **Circles** (*Tondi*).
- **[Pyramid]** indicates the most powerful piece on the board, composed of multiple shapes stacked together to reach a specific total value.]]

[[gloss: The "Dark" or Black pieces occupy the top six rows, while the "Light" or White pieces occupy the bottom six rows. The four central rows remain empty at the start of the game, serving as the "field of battle" where players move their pieces to capture opponents through mathematical calculations.]]

[[notes: Readers may notice that each row is mathematically related to the one behind it. For example, in the White circles (bottom), the second row (4, 16, 36, 64) consists of the squares of the first row (2, 4, 6, 8).]]
