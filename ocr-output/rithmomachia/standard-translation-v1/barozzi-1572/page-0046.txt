The **Great Victory** [[notes: "Massima uittoria" is the highest win condition in Rithmomachia, requiring a player to arrange captured pieces in the opponent's side of the board to form mathematical progressions.]] is nothing other than to situate 4 pieces in the adversary's field in such a manner that their numbers contain all three of the aforementioned [[markup: proportioni]] (proportions): that is, the **Arithmetic**, the **Geometric**, and the **Musical** together. [[notes: In this context, "Musical" refers to the Harmonic proportion, where the reciprocal of the numbers forms an arithmetic progression. For example, 2, 3, 6.]] But which numbers in this [[markup: giuoco]] (game) are able to contain these three said proportions together, the following table demonstrates.
