He did this for the sake of greater clarification and to the special praise of the **Old Germans**, his ancestors, who at all times proved themselves active and like true or **Bar-Männer** [[notes: Likely "Bärmänner" or "Biedermänner," meaning stalwart, valiant, or upright men of character.]] not only in serious and brave deeds, or in the invention and continuation of the excellent arts—concerning which one may see **Matthis Quad von Kinckelbach**, *chapter 3 and throughout his book on the Glory of the German Nation* [[notes: Matthias Quad (1557–1613) was a geographer and engraver; his work *Teutscher Nation Herrlichkeit* (1609) was a patriotic tribute to German history and culture.]]—but also sought and knew how to pass the time not in a frivolous, but in a good and wise manner, with joyful and entertaining things.
