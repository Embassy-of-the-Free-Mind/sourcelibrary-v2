[[meta: This page continues the description of the Queen piece, moving from her physical appearance to her conduct and her specific movements on the board. In medieval chess, the Queen was significantly less powerful than in the modern game, and her moves were often limited to reflect social expectations of female modesty and staying close to the King.]]

It justifies the Queen's presence in a "war game" as a symbol of domestic loyalty, the production of heirs, and the preservation of the King's honor.]]
