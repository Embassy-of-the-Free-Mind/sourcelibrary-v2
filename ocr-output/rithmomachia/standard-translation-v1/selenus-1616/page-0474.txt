# The Fourth Book / Chapter 10:

**The Black player** moves the **King** to seventeen (to further provide his **Pawn** [[notes: *Soldat*, literally "soldier"]] with a free passage): **The White player** moves the **Pawn** to forty-three:

**The Black player** moves his **Pawn** to twenty-six: **The White player** moves the **Pawn** to fifty-one:

**The Black player** moves his **Pawn** to eighteen: **The White player** moves the **Pawn** to fifty-nine: where it is made into a **Queen** (though to no avail).

**The Black player** gives **Checkmate** with the **Pawn** from ten, and in this manner keeps his promise.
