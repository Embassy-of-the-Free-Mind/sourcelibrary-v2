10. **Rook** [[notes: *Rochen*, the archaic term for the Rook, derived from the Persian *rukh*]] to sixty-four [[notes: h8]]: **White** takes, with the same, the **Black King’s Knight’s Soldier** [[notes: the g7 pawn]]:

11. **Black** moves the **King’s Bishop** [[notes: *Schütze*, literally "marksman" or "archer," was the common 17th-century term for the Bishop]] to fifty-three [[notes: e7]] and traps the **White Queen**:

12. **White** advances the **King’s Rook** (notwithstanding that he might take the **Black King’s Bishop’s Soldier** with the **King’s Knight** [[notes: *Reuter*, meaning "rider" or "cavalier," the Knight]] perhaps to better advantage) to seven [[notes: g1]]: **Black** (provided he does not wish to take the **White King’s Knight** with the **King’s Rook**) [moves] the **Queen** to the **White King’s Knight’s** and **Bishop’s Soldiers** [[notes: the g2 and f2 pawns]] at forty [[notes: h5]]: **White** [moves] the **King’s Knight’s Soldier** (intending to break through from fifty-four [[notes: f7]] with the **Queen**) to forty-seven [[notes: g6]]: **Black** [moves] the **King’s Knight** to the **White King’s Soldier** at thirty-five [[notes: c5]]: **White** [moves] the **King’s Knight** to thirty-nine [[notes: g5]]: **Black** [moves] the **King’s Knight** against the same and against the **White Queen** at forty-five [[notes: e6]]:

13. **White** (provided he does not wish to **give Check** with the **King’s Knight** from fifty-four [[notes: f7]]: in which case the **Black King’s Rook** might be caught and the **Queens** exchanged: and therefore Black would suffer loss in the previous no. 12 by moving the **King’s Knight** to 35 and 45) takes it [[notes: the Knight]] with the same and gives **Check**: **Black** takes the same with the **King’s Bishop**: **White** moves the **King’s Rook** against the **Black Queen** at thirty-nine [[notes: g5]]: **Black** takes with her [[notes: the Queen]] the **White King’s Bishop’s Soldier**, moves the same to the **White King’s Soldier** [[notes: the e2 pawn]], and gives **Check**: (for if he were to take the **White Rook** with the **King’s Bishop**, the **White Queen** would take his **King’s Soldiers** and thus break through: and if he then took her with the **Queen**, both his **Rooks** would be lost to the **White Queen**)

14. **White** moves the **King** to eleven [[notes: c2]]: **Black** [moves] the **King’s Rook** to twenty-four [[notes: h3]]: **White** (so that he is not immediately **Checkmated** by Black’s next move) [moves] the **King’s Bishop’s Soldier** to twelve [[notes: d2]]: **Black** takes the **White King’s Soldier** with the **Queen** and offers **Check**: **White** moves the **King** to three or four [[notes: c1 or d1]]: **Black** takes the **White King’s Rook’s Soldier** with the **King’s Rook**: **White** (so that **Black** does not immediately defeat him with the **King’s Rook** from eight [[notes: h1]] or—if he has advanced the **King’s Bishop**—from five [[notes: e1]]) moves the **King’s Rook** to seven [[notes: g1]]: **Black** (in order not to let the **White Queen** go yet) takes her **Soldier** with the **Queen**;
