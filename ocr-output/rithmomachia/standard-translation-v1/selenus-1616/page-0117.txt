# Concerning the Game of Chess.

[[margin: Chapter 10:]]

Regarding their movement / they all move in the same manner: (indicating that the **Infantry** [[notes: *Fuß-Volck*, or foot-folk.]] / without order and compulsion / are of little use) moving straight ahead: yet / with this distinction: that when they / for the first time / wish to step out of their formation / they may leap forward across one square / into a third / (in order to give the **Major Pieces** [[notes: *Haupt-Steine*, referring to the back-rank pieces.]] / which stand behind them / the opportunity to engage / not only in skirmishes / but also / to scout and block the roads: and to prevent the enemy / from easily perceiving / what one might intend in the battle-formation / or what one means to carry out / against him / by obstructing his sight) they may leap forward: provided / that it is beneficial to the game: for otherwise it remains free for them / on the first move / to step only into the next square.

Whether now this over-leaping of the Soldiers / applies only / to the Soldiers of the King = the Queen = and the **Rooks** / there is no single opinion. However / it is commonly granted / especially to the **Archers’** [[notes: *Schützen*, the pawns in front of the Bishops.]] and **Riders’** [[notes: *Reuter*, the pawns in front of the Knights.]] Soldiers / provided that no check has been called / nor any piece yet taken: this power is also given to them.

Otherwise they stride / only / one square forward / and by this it is indicated / that the Riders / by which all Major Pieces / are here understood / can march and be led / better in the field / than the Infantry.

But when these Soldiers / encounter the opponent’s Soldiers / and other Major Pieces / which stand before them in the next line / and square / diagonally / on their own color / then they can / whether on the right / or left side / take the same away / and step into that square: which then / is the third kind of their movement / and happens forward / toward the corners / just as the Archers [[notes: The Bishops.]] are moved / yet only into the next square / which is of the same color as their own.

It is however the custom / held in several places / to also note here / that / when a Soldier / in the first attack / pushes past another / from the opponent’s ranks / at the borders [[notes: Referring to the squares where two pawns from opposing sides might pass each other.]], neither may pass the other / and use the straight path / forward / without harm / but must yield to the corner square / and take the other Soldier from it.
