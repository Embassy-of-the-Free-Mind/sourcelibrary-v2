but also / in the spirit: One finds many a great man / who is strong / in the body / and yet is sick / in the spirit. Stout-hearted and common people / are good and truthful in times of need / and pleasing in battles. The Knight should also / with diligence protect / his workers / because / even if they do not follow him into battle / they may still carry out / their labor in peace: how can the **Farmer** [[notes: *BawMan* (Bauernmann), referring specifically to the peasant or agricultural laborer.]] work / if the Knight / does not protect him in war: for as necessary as it is for the King / to have Knights / so necessary is it for the Knight to have common people / to work for him. A Knight / of right laws / should also be a fervent protector and preserver.

The Knight on the right hand / is White: the one on the left is black [[notes: This refers to the starting positions of the Knights on their respective colored squares.]]: Their movement is / as each has a nature such / that the white one goes / onto the black field / which inclines into the old fields / as is evident / from the Knights / on the right hand / who is white / and has three departures / from where he stands in his place / one to the right hand / onto the black field / in front of the **Farmer** [[notes: *Ackerman* (Ackermann), the plowman or field-worker pawn.]]: and that is right / for as the Farmer labors / and tills the fields / so the Knight should guard him / so that he may provide for himself / and the Knight / and the horses / with fodder / seed and a helping hand: the second move he takes / as he goes and places himself / in front of the **artisan** [[notes: *Lotter* – in this allegorical tradition (Jacobus de Cessolis), this pawn represents the smith, tailor, or carpenter.
