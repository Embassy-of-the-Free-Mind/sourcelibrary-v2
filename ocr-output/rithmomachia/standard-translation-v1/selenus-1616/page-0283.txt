The **White** player [moves] the same [[margin: 8.]] to the **Black** King’s Soldier and Knight at three [[notes: Square 3 corresponds to c1]]: * the **Black** player must not take the **White** King’s Rook’s Soldier with the Queen, but rather moves the King’s Knight (to save him) to the **White** King’s Knight at twenty-eight [[notes: Square 28 corresponds to d4]]: Although this move now seems good, it cannot hold up: for the **White** player then offers him **check** with the King’s Archer [[notes: *Schütze* (Archer) is the historical term for the Bishop]] from forty-four [[notes: Square 44 corresponds to d6]]: [[margin: 9.]] * the **Black** player plays the King to sixty-one [[notes: Square 61 corresponds to e8]]: the **White** player [moves] the Queen to thirty-seven [[notes: Square 37 corresponds to e5]], offers **check**, and by force wins the **Black** King’s Knight (which was mentioned above in no. 8).
