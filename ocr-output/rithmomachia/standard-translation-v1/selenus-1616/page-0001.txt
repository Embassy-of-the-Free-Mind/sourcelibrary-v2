# Front Cover

->[Exterior Binding]<-

This page represents the front cover of the manuscript. The binding is crafted from aged leather [[notes: or perhaps vellum, a high-quality writing surface made from calfskin, though the dark staining suggests treated leather]], showing the natural wear of several centuries. You can see dark patinas and several small wormholes where insects have burrowed into the organic material over time.

The cover features elaborate **blind-tooled** decoration [[notes: a technique where metal stamps are pressed into the leather to create an indented design without the use of gold leaf or ink]]. The layout consists of:

*   Several concentric rectangular borders.
*   Wide frames filled with intricate, repeating patterns of scrolling vines and flowers.
*   A large, central **lozenge** [[notes: a diamond-shaped decorative frame]] featuring dense, leaf-like (**foliate**) ornamentation and arabesque motifs.

On the right-hand edge, two small metal plates remain. These are the attachment points for the book's original clasps [[notes: metal hinges used to keep a book tightly closed to protect the pages from dust and to prevent parchment from curling]].
