The abbreviations in the home rows stand for: R (Rochen/Rook), Sp (Springer/Knight), Sc (Schütze/Bishop), C (Currierer/Courier), M (Mann/Sage or Secret Counselor), K (König/King), and S (Schleich/Jester or Joyful Counselor).]]

The board, or the table, which they use in Ströpeke [[notes: Ströbeck]] for this game, is divided (as can be seen from the sketch) into **ninety-six squares**, of which it comprises **twelve in width** and **eight in length**. It is positioned in such a way that the outermost square to the right hand in the bottom horizontal row (which contains twelve squares) is **black**, while the one to the left is **white**.

Regarding the **pieces**: In this game, each player has **eight more** than the usual sixteen (making **twenty-four** pieces in total): namely, two **Couriers**; one **Secret Counselor**; one **Joyful Counselor**; and (to complete the eight extra) **four more soldiers** to be used.
