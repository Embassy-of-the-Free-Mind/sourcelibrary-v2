Furthermore, above in the 2nd **Chapter**, for several reasons cited there, it was held by us that this game must have been in use among the ancient Germans for a long time: Who knows then whether the inclusion of the Queens in this combat-game also stems from them?

Should the inventor, however, or whoever else brought the Queens onto the stage / not have looked to the warrior wives / he nonetheless intended to give it to be understood that the figures of women, in a battle or encounter, set the hearts and minds of the soldiers as it were on fire: For outside of the heat of love / **Song of Solomon 8: v. 6. 7.** [[notes: A reference to the biblical verses stating "Love is strong as death...
