### Chapter 10: Of the Game of Chess.

...also through constant haste / and not caring much for lies / and they shall guard themselves / against drunkenness in foreign lands.
The movement [[notes: literally "foot-pace" or *fußgang*]] of the common people / that is / of the **Pawns** [[notes: *Wenden* is an archaic term for chess pawns]], is all the same: for each of them may go from his place / to the third field [[notes: in some medieval rules, pawns could move two squares on their first move, landing on the "third" square including the one they started on]]: for every common man / should at the very least be safe / in his own land: They also go only forward / and not backward: Unless they earn it / so that they come to the **border** [[notes: *Spang* refers to the eighth rank or the edge of the board]] / of their adversaries: then they gain / the dignity / that they become **Queens** / and gain the movement of the Queen / and all her power to take / and to capture / diagonally [[notes: *uber eck*]] / whoever comes so near to them / as is her right / when they go forward: whichever noble man opposes them / from among their adversaries / they may capture or strike out from the corner [[notes: *aus dem winckel*, meaning diagonally]] / where they stand / when those same people go or travel / and one rides against them or attacks them / that is / a sign / that one wishes to rob them: and since it is the right of all people / to defend and protect themselves in times of need / from nobles and from commoners: therefore the right is given to them / that whoever rides against them / so that they must fear for their life / and their property / they may well defend themselves. Also / no one should despise such common people / for one reads / that they have often risen / so that they became **Emperors** / and **Popes** / and that is signified by this / that they gain the power of the Queen in the game / if they / through force or through cunning / penetrate the enemy's ranks / and come to the border: no one should overreach in their power and strength / nor in their nobility / nor in their riches. etc.
Thereafter we say / why the ignoble / who are the **Pawns** / are set before the Noble / on the **chessboard** [[notes: *Schachzabel*]]? And this occurs for the first reason / because the commoners / are like a crown to the Nobles: What could the **Rook** [[notes: *Roch*]] on the right hand do / by which the **deputy** and **administrator** [[notes: *vißtumb und verweſer*]] are signified: if he did not have the **farmer** [[notes: *bawman*]] / who works for him / for what he needs? A nobleman would be / as if escaped in distress / were he on foot and without weapons: what would be the refinement of the Nobles / if they had no **merchants** / who brought it to them: and **craftsmen** / who made it for them? Also / the Princes and Princesses / would often be sick / if one did not help them / with **medicine** [[notes: *Ertzeney*]]: thus they have need / of all the common people / in all...
