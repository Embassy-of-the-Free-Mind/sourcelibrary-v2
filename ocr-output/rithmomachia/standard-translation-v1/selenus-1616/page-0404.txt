# The Third Book / Chapter 12:

...only [besets] the path by the **B. K. Archer** [[notes: Black King's Bishop]], but specifically also wins the **B. K. Rider** [[notes: Black King's Knight]] along with the game: for when the **Black** [player] takes this his [White's] **K. Soldier** [[notes: King's Pawn]] with the **K. Archer**: He [White] captures the same with the **K. Archer**: The **Black** [player takes] the same with the **Queen**: He [White] captures the aforementioned **B. K. Rider** with his **Queen**, and thus obtains two **Major Pieces** [[notes: *Haupt-Steine*, literally "head-stones," referring to major or minor pieces as opposed to pawns]] for one.

[[margin: 18.]] But if the **Black** [player] does not take the **W. Soldier** (which was moved to 45 [[notes: Square e6]] in variation 17.) with the **K. Archer**, but instead moves the **K. Rider-Soldier** [[notes: King's Knight's Pawn]] to thirty-nine [[notes: Square g5]], [attacking] the **W. Queen**: then the **White** [player] takes it with the **K. Rider**: The **Black** [takes] the same with the **K. Archer-Soldier** [[notes: King's Bishop's Pawn]] and moves it to thirty-nine [[notes: Square g5]], [attacking] the **W. Queen**: The **White** takes the same with the **K. Archer** and offers **Check**: The **Black** must cover the **King** with the **K. Rider** at forty-six [[notes: Square f6]]: The **White** takes him with the **K. Archer**, moves the same onto [the square of] the **B. K. Roc** [[notes: Black King's Rook at square 64/h8]] and gives **Check**: The **Black** must cover the **King** with the **Queen** at fifty-three [[notes: Square e7]]: The **White** takes her with the **K. Archer** and gives **Check**: The **Black** places the **King** at sixty-one [[notes: Square e8]]: The **White** [moves] the **K. Archer** from the **B. K. Roc** [square] to forty-six [[notes: Square f6]]: The **Black** [moves] the **K. Roc-Soldier** [[notes: King's Rook's Pawn]] to forty [[notes: Square h5]] (to somewhat delay the **Check** from the **W. Queen** at forty: after which **Checkmate** from fifty-four [[notes: Square f7]] would immediately follow): The **White** takes the **B. K. Roc** with the **K. Archer**: The **Black** moves the **K. Rider** to forty-three [[notes: Square c6]]: The **White** takes the **B. K. Roc-Soldier** with the **Queen** and gives **Check**: The **Black** moves the **King** to sixty [[notes: Square d8]]: (for if He moved him to sixty-two [[notes: Square f8]], the **White** would immediately give **Checkmate** with the **Queen** from fifty-four [[notes: Square f7]]): the **White** gives **Check** with the **K. Archer** at forty-six [[notes: Square f6]]: The **Black** covers the **King** with his **Rider** at fifty-three [[notes: Square e7]]: The **White** gives **Checkmate** from sixty-four [[notes: Square h8]] with the **Queen**.
