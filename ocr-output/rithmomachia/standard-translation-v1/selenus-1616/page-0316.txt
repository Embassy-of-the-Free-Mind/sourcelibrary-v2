The abbreviations represent the colors of the pieces used in the original woodcut illustrations: **SR** (Silk-Red), **SS** (Silk-Black), **WS** (White-Silk), and **WR** (White-Red). **SK** and **WK** designate the Black and White Kings, respectively; **S&** represents the Black Queen. The numbers within the cells (e.g., 42, 52) indicate the position or square number to which a piece has moved according to the 1–64 coordinate system.]]
