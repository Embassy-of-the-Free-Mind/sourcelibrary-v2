-># Preface.<-

-for-word [[notes: Completing the thought from the preceding page: "not indeed to the end that I should bring it word-for-word..."]] into our tongue, but rather I have sought to arrange it so that it may be more easily understood and practiced by our nation.

In order that I may proceed with this work in an orderly fashion, I will divide it into **Six Chapters**, and in them, I shall treat and describe the following:

In the **First**: the form of the **Board** or the Table; and then the **Stones** [[notes: *Steine*, the common term for gaming pieces like those in Chess or Backgammon.]] or pieces which are used upon it for this game.

In the **Second**: the **Numbers** which are marked upon the stones.

In the **Third**: the **Spaces** [[notes: *Felder*, the squares of the board.]] upon which the stones are placed.

In the **Fourth**: the **Movement** of the stones—how they are to be moved [[notes: *Ziehen*, the act of making a move in a board game.]].

In the **Fifth**: the manner and way of **Combat** [[notes: *Kempfens* (modern *Kämpfens*); in Rythmomachia, "combat" involves capturing pieces through mathematical calculations like addition, subtraction, or geometric progression.]]—namely, how these stones should be brought against one another.

In the **Sixth** and **Last**: the various **Conquests** [[notes: *Überwindungen*; the game has several types of victory conditions, including the "Common Victory" and the "Great Victory."]] through which victory is obtained and the game is brought to an end.
