To the Queens / because they serve in this military host / the highest office / of a Field Marshal [[notes: *Feld-Marschalck*. In the 17th century, this was the highest military rank, emphasizing the Queen's power as the King's primary protector and strategist.]] / the inventor has given these qualities: that they / can leap to their Lords' aid / with great haste / and strenuously set upon the enemy. For this reason / because the advantage and ruin of the game rests with them / they must rightly be guarded with all diligence: considering that / once they are gone / one can only with difficulty / and quite rarely / set the game to rights again / or win.

However / it may cause many to wonder / why such a high office was commanded specifically to women?
