### Chapter 10: Of the Game of Chess.

...in the manner indicated / in the following move / can be regained immediately / [one may] exchange. For otherwise the **Soldier** [[notes: The pawn.]] / might not thrive / to become a **Queen** / even if one / lost the actual Queen / in the subsequent moves : instead, he is left / by the opponent / in his furthest / or main line / as if immovable / and therefore harmless / and not driven away. This is further understood / only regarding the four **Soldiers** / who / like **Amazons** [[notes: Female warriors of Greek mythology; here used to describe the pawns on the "Queen-side" of the board.]], wait upon the Queen’s side / and are her handmaidens : Namely, that / after the Queen’s passing / the King / may choose and take / one from their midst / in her place / (provided that she / reached the furthest line / as mentioned / through her bravery / and there / partook of the previous Queen’s power and authority / immediately / because she does not need / to stand / for several moves / in danger and doubt / as to whether she will be taken / or not). 

That those / on the King's side / should not have such an advantage : this is believed to be proven / because it runs against Nature / that a **Male Person** / should be transformed into a **Woman's Image** : as **Franciscus Tidicæus** Ph. ac M. D [[notes: Franz Tidike, a 16th-century physician and philosopher.]], in the *Appendix* of his description / of the **Microcosmi** [[notes: *Microcosmus*, a common Renaissance philosophical concept viewing the human body as a "little world" mirroring the universe.]], pages 637, 638, proves at length : but alongside this he admits / and introduces several examples / from **Rali, Galeno, Plinio, Empedocle Agrigentino, Licinio Mutiano, Ausonio Burdegalensi, Volaterrano, Joanne Langio, Joanne VViero, Livio, Hippocrate, Amato Lusitano, Baptista Campofulgoso** [[notes: A long list of classical and Renaissance authorities, including Galen, Pliny the Elder, and Hippocrates, often cited in early modern texts to provide scholarly weight.]], pages 634, 635, 636, 638, 639 : that **Women’s Images** / have been truly / and by natural causes / transformed into **Male Persons** / and can also be so changed. 

However / (so that we may return again to our Game) / it has been understood / by good players / of old and still today / without distinction / of all / both the King's- and the Queen's-soldiers / indeed even / if the first Queen / is still present / (of which different examples / are to be found in the following books). For the meaning is not / as if that same Soldier / becomes the King's wedded spouse / considering that / in the first rank / only one spouse was assigned to him : but rather / that by him / brave soldiers / no less / than his own Queen / shall be held / in honor.

In ancient times / a Soldier / who was made into a Queen / first / had to leap into the third square [[notes: This refers to an older rule where a newly promoted pawn/queen had a special initial "jump" move.]]: but subsequently / he was moved / over the points of the squares / into the other square.
