6. * The Black [player] / moves the same [the Queen] / (so that he does not lose the Bishop's Soldier / and the Rook [[notes: "Rochen" is the traditional term for the Rook or Castle.]]) / to forty-five: The White [player] / takes / the Black King’s Bishop's Soldier / with his King’s Bishop's Soldier / and moves it against the Black Queen:
7. * The Black [player] / takes the same / with the King’s Rider: The White [player] / moves the King’s Bishop / against the same / to twenty-seven / and thus wins it by force.
8. If, however, the Black [player] / with the Rider / does not take the White Soldier / (n. 7): then he loses / one more Soldier / than the White [player]: But if he takes it / with the Queen: then they both / exchange / the Queens: and the White [player] / takes with the King’s Rider / the Black King’s Bishop's Soldier / gives check [[notes: "Beut Schach" literally "offers check."]] / and thereby wins / the Rook / without any resistance.
9. If, however, the Black [player] / takes the White Soldier / neither with the Rider / nor with the Queen / (n. 7. 8.) / but instead pushes / the same [the Queen] / to forty-six: then the White [player] / moves against her / the King’s Bishop / to thirty-nine:
