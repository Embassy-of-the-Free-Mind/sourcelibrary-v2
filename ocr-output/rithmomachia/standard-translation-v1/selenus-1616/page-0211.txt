# Concerning the Game of Chess.

[[margin: 1. cap.]] [[notes: This refers back to the first chapter or a specific section governing the rules and symbols.]]
The other copperplates [[notes: Intaglio prints or engravings used for illustrations throughout the book]] found here and there throughout this treatise are to be understood in the same manner as these: with the only exception being that, in those plates: **(R)** signifies a Rook [[notes: Derived from the Persian *rukh*, known today as the castle]]; **(Sp)** a Knight or Rider [[notes: The German uses *Sprenger* (leaper) or *Reuter* (rider) for the knight]]; and **(B)** a Pawn, or (as it is called in this description) a **Soldier**.

The abbreviations in the examples are intended as follows: **(des K.)** of the King: **(der K.)** of the Queen: **(des W. K.)** of the White King: **(des S. K.)** of the Black King:
->**(der W. K.)** of the White Queen: **(der S. K.)** of the<-
->Black Queen: **(seines K.)** of his<-
->King: **(seiner K.)** of<-
->his Queen.<-
