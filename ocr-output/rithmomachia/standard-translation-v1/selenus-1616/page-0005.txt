# Privilegium Cæsareum.
[[notes: This Latin title translates to "Imperial Privilege." It was a legal document issued by the Holy Roman Emperor that gave a publisher the exclusive right to print a specific work for a set number of years.]]

We, Matthias, by the Grace of GOD, Elected Roman Emperor, forever Increaser of the Empire, King in Germany, Hungary, Bohemia, Dalmatia, Croatia, and Slavonia, etc. Archduke of Austria, Duke of Burgundy, Styria, Carinthia, Carniola, and Württemberg, etc. Count of Tyrol, etc. 
[[notes: Matthias I was the Holy Roman Emperor from 1612 to 1619. His long list of titles serves to demonstrate his supreme authority over a vast, multi-ethnic territory. "Increaser of the Empire" (Semper Augustus) was a traditional title for the Emperor dating back to Roman times.]]
