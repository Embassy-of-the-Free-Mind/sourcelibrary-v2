Of these pieces / one finds / in the aforementioned Old Manuscripts / the following report:

> That the **Rook** [[notes: *Roch*]], upon the **Chessboard** [[notes: *SchachZabel*]], shall be / a Knight / sitting upon a horse with a **hatchet** [[gloss: cc]] [[notes: *Bele* or *Beil* refers to a battle-axe or halberd]], and shall wear a **hood** / lined with **vair** [[gloss: cc]] [[notes: *vehem* or *Feh* refers to the blue-grey fur of the Siberian squirrel, a luxury material reserved for high-ranking officials and royalty]], and shall have a **rod** / or staff / in his right hand: this signifies the **vicedom** [[gloss: cc]] [[notes: *vistumb*, a high-ranking deputy or administrative official]] and **legates** of princes: Because a King cannot be everywhere / in his Realm / it is necessary for him / to have **stewards** [[gloss: cc]] [[notes: *verweser*, those who "administer" or "manage" the King's authority in his absence]] / who hold his power / who act by his authority / as he himself would do / and when the Realms are too wide / it [[gloss: cc]] is necessary / that there be two of them / one at the right hand / and one [[gloss: cc]] at the left hand / and sides: so that at the **borders** [[gloss: cc]] [[notes: *gemercke*, the frontier regions or "marches"]] and corners / nothing may arise / against the laws of the Realm / and [[gloss: cc]] so that the Realm / suffers no ill / and it be not harmful to the commoners / [[gloss: cc]] and for this reason / they stay at the borders / to see if anything [[gloss: cc]] occurs or gathers nearby / from which damage might come. These [[gloss: cc]] same stewards / shall 1. have **justice** / which shines before all other [[gloss: cc]] virtues: for it happens sometimes / that the Realms are lost / if the subjects are just / and the **officials** [[gloss: cc]] [[notes: *amptleut*]] are unjust / then one believes / the Prince is also unjust. There- [[gloss: cc]] fore the King's stewards / shall be so just / that they [[gloss: cc]] through justice are steadfast and ready / to die / and whatever [[gloss: cc]] is for the common good / and profit / in the Realm / that they shall value more [[gloss: cc]] than their own life: 2. the stewards shall have **goodness** / [[gloss: cc]] so that they have compassion for the commoners / in what they suffer / or what befalls them / in their heart / and also in their deeds / to one [[gloss: cc]] they shall leave / to the other give / for goodness / is a common help- [[gloss: cc]] fulness / which comes from the goodness and sweetness of a gentle heart: 3.
