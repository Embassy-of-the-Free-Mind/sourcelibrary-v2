# On the Game of Chess.

[[margin: 6. cap:]] ...the same / the Queen / to fifty-four: The White player / gives / with the Queen / from thirty-nine / **Check** / and moves her / to the [Black] King’s Bishop’s Pawn: [[note: "Schützens-Soldaten" literally means "Archer’s Soldier." The "Archer" or "Shooter" is the Bishop, and his "Soldier" is the Pawn.]] the Black player / (since He / wishes to give no occasion / for the exchange of Queens: For / if He covered the King / with the King’s Knight / on fifty-three: the Knights / and Queens / would be exchanged) [moves] the King / to fifty-two: The White player / (in order / to relieve his King's Knight) takes / with the Queen / the [Black] King’s Bishop’s Pawn / and places her / upon the [square of] the [Black] King and his Bishop’s Pawn: The Black player / [moves] the King’s Knight / (in order / to protect / his Bishop’s Pawn / since / the White player / could there / cause him / all sorts of trouble) / to forty-one: The White player / takes / with the Queen / the [Black] King’s Pawn: Thus He has / two Pawns / as an **Advantage** / and will, provided He understands it, well maintain his game / because the Black player’s pieces / are somewhat scattered.

8. But if the Black player / does not move the Queen to 54 (no. 7) but rather / (in order / to prevent / the Check / from the White Queen / from thirty-nine) / moves to thirty-two: then the White player plays / (in order to create space for the King’s Knight / and to bring the enemy / in such a manner / into disorder) / his Bishop’s Pawn / to thirty: The Black player / cannot for this time arrange his moves / any better / than that He / moves the King’s Bishop / against the White King’s Knight / on forty-five: The White player / places / the King’s Knight / upon the [Black] Queen / and her Bishop’s Pawn / on twenty-two: The Black player / [moves] the same / to forty-eight: The White player / (in order / to protect / the King’s Knight / and thereby / so far as He is / skilled in the moves: to obtain a better-arranged game / than the Black player) / [moves] the King’s Pawn / to twenty-nine.

Therefore the Black player should / (so that / his King’s Pawn / is protected: And the White player / also / does not move / the King’s Knight / against his Queen / on thirty-six: but rather that his pieces / may be drawn / usefully and conveniently / into the field) / move not the King’s Pawn / against the White King’s Knight / to 28. but rather / his Bishop’s Pawn / to 43. (no. 6.)
