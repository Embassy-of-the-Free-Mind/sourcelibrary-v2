# Of the Game of Chess.

[[margin: 1. cap:]] ...the same with the King’s Soldier: White moves the King’s Rider’s [[notes: "Reuter" or Rider is the period term for the Knight.]] Soldier [[notes: "Soldat" or Soldier is the period term for the Pawn.]] against the Black King’s Archer [[notes: "Schütze" or Archer is the period term for the Bishop.]] at twenty-six and captures it through this move without any hindrance. For Black may not move him [the Archer] away because of the King (since he covers the same from the White King’s Rook [[notes: "Rochen" or Rook. This describes a "pin," where a piece cannot move because it would expose the King to check.]]).

7. If, however, Black does not take the White Soldier with the King’s Archer (no. 6) but instead with the King’s Soldier: then White moves the King’s Rider to the Black King’s Archer’s [square] at twenty-five, thereby preventing Black's intention, and finally wins the game.

8. But if Black does not cover the King (no. 5) but instead moves him to sixty: then White moves the King’s Rider to the Black King’s Archer’s [square] at twenty-five.

9. * Black [moves] the King’s Archer against the White Queen to fifty-nine: White takes the Black King’s Archer with the King’s Rook, moves him to the Black Queen’s [square], and thus captures it without damage or loss of his pieces: For if Black takes the same with the King’s Rider’s Soldier: He [White] captures the Black Queen with his Queen, and rescues it from the Black King’s Archer: If, however, Black takes the White Rook with the Queen: then He [White] takes her with the King’s Rider: Black [takes] the White Queen with the King’s Archer: He [White] [takes] the same with the King’s Rider, and thus obtains two Archers besides the King’s Rider: He can also still use five main pieces [[notes: "Haupt-Steine" refers to the non-pawn pieces: King, Queen, Rook, Rider, Archer.]]: whereas Black now has only three remaining.

10. If, however, Black does not move the Archer (no. 9) but instead (in order to protect the Queen where necessary) moves the King’s Rook to 59: then White moves the King’s Rider’s Soldier to the Black King’s Archer’s [square] at twenty-six.
