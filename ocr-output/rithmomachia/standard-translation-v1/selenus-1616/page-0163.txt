# 2. Chapter: Of the Game of Chess.

[An ornamental woodcut headpiece featuring intricate floral and vine scrolls with a central mask or face motif.]

### The Second Chapter.

## Of the Two Players / in what manner / they shall / employ the aforementioned methods / and how they ought to conduct themselves.

The methods / through which victory in this game / must be obtained / were recounted and indicated in the previous chapter: now it will be necessary / to treat how one should / wisely put the same into practice: Wherein two things are to be considered: (1.) by whom / and then (2.) in what manner / it should and must be done.

Regarding the First: We have already / in the 1st Chapter of the 1st Book / briefly mentioned those / who are permitted to engage in this Game of Chess: Of those now / among them / who wish to lay their hand / to this excellent game / and its moves / there is generally required / (1.) a fine understanding / good memory / an imagination for all sorts of inventions [[notes: "Erfindungen" here refers to stratagems or tactical maneuvers]], / practice / and above all things / a good inclination [[notes: "Zuneigung," meaning a natural talent or a passionate devotion to the game]].

They must also / (2.) when they wish / to take this game in hand / be well qualified for it / and bring with them / a steady, quiet mind / which is free from any / foreign thoughts: considering / they might otherwise fail to achieve victory / and would have to complain / with Catullus,
[[margin: cc]]
> (Nos aliò mentes, aliò dimisimus aures
[[margin: cc]]
> Jure igitur vincemur. Amat victoria curam)
[[notes: This Latin quotation from the poet Catullus translates to: "Our minds were elsewhere, our ears were elsewhere; rightly, therefore, are we conquered. Victory loves care." It emphasizes that chess requires total concentration.]]

They shall / (3.) firmly and resolutely set their sights / not merely upon victory / (of which we treated in the last chapter / of the First Book) / but rather / how it may be obtained with the best methods / and in the most praiseworthy manner.

They must / (4.) know the nature / properties and moves / of all the pieces / the highest as well as the lowest / as surely / as one knows the multiplication table [[notes: "das ein mahl eines," literally "the one-times-one"]] when calculating: Just as they likewise / must have the squares [[notes: "Quartiere," meaning the individual squares of the board]], lines / fields and numbers of the board / in fresh memory / and be able / as it were / to count them on their fingers.

What now, beyond the aforementioned, is to be particularly observed / and is required of the same / that can be gathered from the beginning
