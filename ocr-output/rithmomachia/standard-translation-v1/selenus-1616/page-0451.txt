## 7. cap:

[[notes: The text begins mid-sentence, continuing from the previous page's move.]] ...er, to prevent [the loss], [moves] the **King’s Archer’s Soldier** [[notes: The Bishop's Pawn]] onto the **Black King’s Soldier** [[notes: The Black King’s Pawn]], at thirty: **The Black player** / (to protect the same) [moves] the **King’s Archer’s Soldier** to forty-six: **The White player** / takes / the **Black King’s Soldier** / with his **King’s Archer’s Soldier**: **The Black player** / [takes] the same / with the **King’s Archer’s Soldier**: **The White player** / offers / with the **King’s Archer** [[notes: The Bishop]] / from thirty-nine / **Check**: **The Black player** / covers the King / (provided He does not wish to move him) with the **King’s Archer** / at fifty-three: **The White player** / (to relieve the **King’s Archer**: and to exchange the **Queen**, along with the **Archers**) moves the **Queen** / to thirty-two: strengthens his game in this manner, provides an opportunity for those pieces which might cause him trouble to be removed, and attains / in addition to the captured **Rider** [[notes: The Knight]] / the victory of this game, provided he is careful.

But if **the Black player** / (to prevent this exchange of pieces) / moves the **King’s Rider** / to forty-three: then **the White player** / advances / the **King’s Rider** / to twenty-two: **The Black player** / [moves] the **King’s Archer** / to forty-five: **The White player** / [moves] the **King’s Rider** / to twelve: **The Black player** / [moves] the **King’s Rook’s Soldier** [[notes: The Rook's Pawn]] / against the **White King’s Archer** / at forty-eight: **The White player** / takes / with the same / the **Black King’s Archer** / and offers **Check**: **The Black player** / takes him / with the **King’s Rider**: **The White player** / [takes] the **Black King’s Archer’s Soldier** / with the **King’s Rider**: **The Black player** / moves / against the same / and the **White King’s Rider’s Soldier** [[notes: The Knight's Pawn]], the **Queen** / to thirty-four: **The White player** / (to protect the **Soldier**) [moves] the **King’s Rider** / (out of danger) to twenty-seven: and in this way brings / his pieces / into a good order.
