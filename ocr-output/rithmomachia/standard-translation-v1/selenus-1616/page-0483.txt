Of the Battle of Numbers.

---

## The First Chapter.
### How the Board and Pieces of this ancient game are to be fashioned.

[[notes: The "Battle of Numbers" refers to *Rhythmomachia*, a highly complex mathematical board game popular in the Middle Ages and Renaissance. It was used both for entertainment and to teach Pythagorean number theory.]]

[[meta: catchword: The]]

[[notes: Vocab: **Zahl-Kampf** (Battle of Numbers), **Tafel** (Board), **Steine** (Game pieces/Stones), **Capittel** (Chapter), **Rhythmomachia** (The Greek-derived name for the game).]]

-># The Report.<-

In order that you, my dear reader, may properly understand this report, I have divided it into **Six Chapters** [[notes: Although the author specifies six chapters here, only five sections follow on this page; the sixth likely refers to the "conclusion" or perhaps a subsequent section not listed here.]], in which may be found everything noteworthy, special, and to be observed point by point:

**Firstly**, the form of the **Board**, or the shape as it sits upon the table, and thereafter how one traverses and crosses it in three different ways.

In the **Second**, the **Moves** [[notes: *Züge*; the legal paths pieces take across the grid according to their geometric shape.]], which are to be performed in those three ways.

In the **Third**, the **Leaps** [[notes: *Sprünge*; a specialized capture mechanic in Rhythmomachia where a piece "jumps" over or lands at a specific distance based on its mathematical value.]], by which one captures the **Stones**.

In the **Fourth**, the **Order of the Stones**, how one should seize and attack them.

In the **Fifth**, the **Art and Advantage** of the board game; namely, how one should set up a game against another, and how to place the stones and **Figures** [[notes: *Figuren*; the game pieces are shaped as circles, triangles, and squares to represent different mathematical progressions: arithmetic, geometric, and harmonic.]] most skillfully upon the board, and how to block the stones in their moves, and bring the game to its end and conclusion.
