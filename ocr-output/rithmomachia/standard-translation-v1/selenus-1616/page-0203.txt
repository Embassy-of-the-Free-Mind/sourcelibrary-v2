->Vom Schach-Spiel.<-
[[notes: *On the Game of Chess.* This serves as a running header for the book.]]

[[margin: 7. cap:]] ...makes the beginning with the King's Soldier; but the opponent moves his Queen's Soldier [[notes: *Königin-Soldaten*; refers to the Queen’s Pawn]] against it.

If now, on both sides, the start is made with the King's Soldier [[notes: *Königes-Soldaten*; the King's Pawn, i.e., 1. e4 e5]], then such an opening—on account of the following fourth (for we do not wish to go higher with the division [[notes: *Abtheilung*; refers to the classification or branching of variations]], lest confusion arise instead of order), third, and second move—can occur in eight different ways: the first seven of which shall be detailed in the following chapters of this book until its end; the eighth and last, however (which particularly pleased **Damiano** [[notes: Pedro Damiano (1480–1544), a Portuguese author whose 1512 book was one of the first modern chess manuals]]), shall be detailed in the Third Book, from the first to the tenth Chapter.
