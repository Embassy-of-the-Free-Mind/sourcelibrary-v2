### The Second Book / Chapter 14:

take the **King’s Knight’s Pawn** [[notes: *Reuters-Soldaten*; *Reuter* is the Knight and *Soldat* is the Pawn]]: If he takes it with the **Pawn**: then **White** gives **Checkmate** with the **Queen** from square **fifty-eight**, or (if the **Black King’s Knight** had been moved forward) from **fifty-nine**: But if he takes it with the **Queen** and offers **Check**: then **White** takes her with the **King’s Pawn**: **Black** moves the **King’s Knight’s Pawn** to **forty-two**: **White** takes the same with the **King’s Bishop** [[notes: *Schützen*, literally "Archer," refers to the Bishop]] and moves it to the [square of the] **Black King’s Rook**: **Black** takes it with the **King’s Bishop’s Pawn**: **White** [takes] the same with the **Queen**, offers **Check**, and moves to the [square of the] **Black King’s Rook**: **Black** covers the **King** with his **Rook** [[notes: *Rochen* is an archaic term for the Rook or Castle]] at **fifty-one**: **White** offers **Check** with the **Queen** from **fifty-eight**: **Black** covers the **King** with his **Rook** or (in variation 38) the **King’s Knight** at **fifty-nine**:

[[margin: 38.]]
* If he covers him with the **Knight**: then **White** gives **Check** with the **King’s Rook** from square **four**: **Black** must move the **King** to **sixty-one**: **White** takes the **Black King’s Rook** with the **Queen**: **Black** moves the **King’s Bishop’s Pawn** (to make room for his **King**) to **forty-six**: **White** (to prevent this) [moves] the **King’s Bishop** to **forty-five**: and subsequently gives **Checkmate** with the **Queen** from **fifty-two**: or with his **Rook** from **sixty**, unhindered.

[[margin: 39.]]
But if **Black** does not cover the **King** with the **Knight** (as in no. 38) but rather with his **Rook** at **59** and moves it against the **White Queen**: then **White** gives **Check** with his **King’s Pawn** from **fifty-one**: **Black** must move the **King** to **sixty-one**: **White** takes the **King’s Rook** with his **King’s Bishop** and can thereafter very easily maintain the victory against **Black**.

[[margin: 40.]]
However, if **Black** plays other moves after his **Queen** has been placed on **61** (no. 37) and the **Bishops** were exchanged: **White** still has a good opportunity to carry away the victory.
