-># Report.<-

In order that you, my dear reader, may properly understand this report, I have divided it into **Six Chapters** [[notes: Although the author specifies six chapters here, only five sections follow on this page before the concluding ornament; the sixth may be the final "conclusion" mentioned within the fifth point, or continued on a subsequent section.]], in which may be found everything noteworthy, special, and to be observed point by point:

**Firstly**, the form of the **Board**, or the shape as it sits upon the table, and thereafter how one traverses and crosses it in three different ways.

In the **Second**, the **Moves** [[notes: *Züge*; the legal paths pieces take across the grid.]], which are to be performed in those three ways.

In the **Third**, the **Leaps** [[notes: *Sprünge*; a specialized capture mechanic in Rythmomachia where a piece "jumps" over or lands at a specific distance based on its mathematical value.]], by which one captures the **Stones**.

In the **Fourth**, the **Order of the Stones**, how one should seize and attack them.

In the **Fifth**, the **Art and Advantage** of the board game; namely, how one should set up a game against another, and how to place the stones and **Figures** [[notes: *Figuren*; the game pieces are shaped as circles, triangles, and squares to represent different mathematical progressions: arithmetic, geometric, and harmonic.]] most skillfully upon the board, and how to block the stones in their moves, and bring the game to its end and conclusion.
