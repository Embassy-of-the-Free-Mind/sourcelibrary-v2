### The First Book / Chapter 11

...named: for that same small square in the **White King’s** quarter, upon which his **Rook** stands, is the **White King’s Rook’s** square. Conversely, on the same line, moving upwards into the **Black King’s** quarter, the square where his **Rook** is placed is the **Black King’s Rook’s** square. Furthermore, the small square or field in the **White Queen’s** quarter where her **Rook** is placed is the **White Queen’s Rook’s** square. The same holds true for the **Black Queen’s** quarter. Along the **Rook’s** line, moving upwards, that square which is closest to a **Rook’s** square (so long as a **Soldier** [[notes: *Soldat*, another term used here for the Pawn]] still stands upon it) is called the **King’s** or **Queen’s Rook’s Soldier’s** square. However, if the soldier has been moved from there to another square, it is called the **Rook’s second square**.
