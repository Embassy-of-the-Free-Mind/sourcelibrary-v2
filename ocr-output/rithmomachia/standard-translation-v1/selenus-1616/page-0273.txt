If He [[notes: "He" refers to the opponent playing the Black pieces]], however, does not move the **Rook** out of danger, but instead moves the **Bishop** [[notes: *Schützen*, literally "the Archer," an early name for the Bishop]] towards the White **Rook** onto twelve: then **White** takes the mentioned Black **Rook** with the **Knight** [[notes: *Reuter*, literally "Rider"]] and gives **Check**: He [Black] moves the **King** towards it onto fifty-two: **White** [moves] the **Rook** to two, and in a few moves makes the **King's Rook's Pawn** into a **Queen** [[notes: This refers to the promotion of the pawn upon reaching the final rank]].

[[margin: 22.]] But if **White** does not move the **King** to 19 (see note 21), but instead offers **Check** with the **King's Rook** at fifty-eight,
[[margin: 23.]] and at the same time protects the **King's Knight's Pawn**: * then **Black** moves the **King** to forty-one: **White** [moves] the **King's Knight** to forty-three, and then with the **King's Knight's Pawn** from thirty-four gives the following **Checkmate**.

[[margin: 24.]] But if **Black** does not move the **King** to 41 (see note 23), but to twenty-five: then **White** moves the **King's Knight** to twenty-seven and with it blocks the
[[margin: 25.]] Black **King**: * **Black** moves the **King's Rook's Pawn** to the White **King's Knight's Pawn** at thirty-three: **White** takes it with his **King's Knight**: **Black** moves the **King's Bishop's Pawn** to thirty-eight: **White** [moves] the **King's Knight** to twenty-seven: **Black** [moves] the **King's Bishop** to fifty-five and protects the **Rook** [[notes: The original text says *dero Rothen* (the Red), which might suggest a chess set where the pieces were White and Red rather than White and Black, or it may be a typo for *Rochen* (Rook)]] with it: **White** moves the **King's Rook** to fifty: **Black** [moves] the **King's Rook** (after he has offered **Check** with the same from sixty, and **White** has moved the **King** to eleven) to the White **King's Knight** at twenty-eight: **White** [moves] the same [Knight] to ten and offers **Check**: **Black** moves the **King** to the White **King's Rook's Pawn** at seventeen: the
[[margin: 26.]] **White King's Knight** [moves] to twenty: * **Black** takes the White **King's Rook's Pawn** with the **King**: **White** gives **Checkmate** with the **King's Rook** from forty-nine.

[[margin: 27.]] If, however, **Black** does not take the White **King's Rook's Pawn** (see note 26), but rather the **Knight's Pawn** with his **King's Rook**: then **White** takes the same with the **King's Rook**: now, let **Black** move whichever piece He wishes: **White** can give him **Checkmate** by force (if He places the **King's Rook** on twenty-seven, the **King's Knight** on thirty-five, and finally the **King's Rook** on twenty-five).
