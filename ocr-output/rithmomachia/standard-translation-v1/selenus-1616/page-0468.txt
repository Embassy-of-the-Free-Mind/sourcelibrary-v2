-> Das Vierte Buch/ 10. cap. <-

> » Soldiers / to seventeen: and (so far as / field 35 / is cleared and se-
> » cured by the King's Archer's-Soldier [[notes: The "King's Archer" is the King's Bishop; the "Soldier" is a Pawn. This refers to the King's Bishop's Pawn.]]) to thirty-five: the K. Archers /
> » to twenty: the K. Rook's-Soldiers / to twenty-five: the K.
> » Archers / to thirty-four: the King with the Queen /
> » at once / the latter to nineteen: the former to eighteen. When now
> » the pieces / stand thus / it is called a **Hut**, and the W. King /
> » is safe from **Checks** [[notes: *Schachen*]].

> » The **Other** / is made in the following manner on the side of the **Black** /
> » : He moves / the K. Rook's-Soldier /
> » to thirty-three: the K. Soldier / to forty-four: the K. Archer /
> » over his Knight's-Soldier / to
> » forty-one: the K. Knight [[notes: *Reuter*, literally "Rider"]] / to forty-three: the
> » King / and the Queen / in one move / the former / to fifty-eight:
> » the latter to fifty-nine: and in this way / across the width of the fields /
> » of the first exit / the Queen has moved / into the third field:
> » When now the pieces / stand thus / it is called **the Iron Hut** /
> » and is a good, secure Hut.

> » The **third** is to be made in the following way: The White /
> » moves / the K. Soldier / to twenty-nine: the King /
> » to fourteen: (since he / finds a Soldier / on the same
> » field / he has the power / in the first exit /
> » to push the same forward) and the Queen / at the same time /
> » to twenty-one: and so has / in two moves / also a good
> » **Hut**.

Besides these three / there are many other good Huts: but
through daily practice / one will / surely learn them: Also,
the Huts / (just like the entire Game) are better learned
by **visual observation** [[notes: *Augenschein*, literally "eye-shine" or firsthand sight]] / than from written instructions: Also,
one should not understand it / as if the moves / must be made exactly /
one after the other / but rather / one can / adopt a
different order / and move here / then there / until the pieces /
finally / are arranged / just as indicated /
and as they should stand / in the Hut: Unless / the opponent /
moves his pieces / before one's eyes / and in the way / so that
he could not properly / make the Hut / of his will.
