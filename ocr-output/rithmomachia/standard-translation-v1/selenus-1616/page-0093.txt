Regarding this piece, one finds recorded in the aforementioned old *manuscripts* the following words:

> "The form of the Queen on the Chess-Board [[notes: *Schach-Zabel*, an archaic German term for the chessboard, derived from the Latin *tabula*]] shall be: a beautiful woman in gilded, adorned garments, trimmed with fur [[notes: *Vehem* or *Feh*, referring to the winter fur of the squirrel, traditionally reserved for royalty and nobility]]; and the woman shall stand at the left hand of the King on the Chess-Board, so that the King may hold her with his right hand and <unclear>...</unclear>; Canticles [[notes: The Song of Solomon]] chapter 1, verse 6. She stands, and is also placed, at the right hand of the King so that she may have by grace what he has by nature. It is better to have Kings who inherit by nature and according to the manner of the firstborn, than that they be chosen by the election of the Princes [[notes: *Fürsten Chur* refers to the electoral system, such as that used in the Holy Roman Empire]]. For when the Realm becomes vacant, it often happens through the wars of the Electors [[notes: *Walherren*, the noblemen with the right to vote for a new ruler]] that the Realm remains orphaned for a long time and stays so; thereby the Realm is damaged and Justice is suppressed. It also happens that the Electors look more to their own particular profit than to the common good; and during these matters, the Realm’s property is delayed and thereby impoverished. But if it is inherited, then that same heir is raised from youth for that purpose, so that he may become a good King, according to the will of a wise father and according to the father's customs, deeds, and virtues. Also, the Princes and Lords who are in the land and under the Realm must take care that they do not provoke war in the Realm, nor give away the Realm's property, for the Lord by nature [the hereditary King] will not stand for it. The Queen shall be clothed with five virtues: Firstly, with customs, mature and perfect [[notes: *Alt perig*, likely a variation of *alt-bejahrt*, meaning of a mature or seasoned age]], not easily losing her modesty; for it often happens that a woman quickly forgets shame. 2. The Queen shall be pure and chaste, so that she may be a mirror and an image to other women; and as she is first before others in dignity, so should she be before others in chastity and honor. 3. The Queen should be wise, with modest manners, measured words, and the keeping of secrets; for one rarely finds that women can keep secret matters or carry them hidden; yet that does not become them well. 4.
