-># Of the Game of Chess.<-

->S iij<-

<margin>5. cap:</margin> **Kings:** **The White** [player] offers check with the King’s Rochen [[note: Rook]] from twenty-eight [[note: Square d4]]: **The Black** [player] must—unless he wishes to cover his King with his Archer [[note: Bishop]] on twenty-nine [[note: Square e4]]: in which case **the White** [player] offers check with the King’s Rider’s Soldier [[note: the Knight’s Pawn]] from twenty-three [[note: Square g3]], winning either his King’s Rook or Archer along with the entire game—move the King to twenty-one [[note: Square e3]]: **The White** [player] moves the King’s Rook to the Black King’s Rook’s [starting square] at sixty [[note: Square d8]] and gives a discovered check [[gloss: Ab-Schach]] from the Queen: **The Black** [player] moves the King out of the check and loses the King’s Rook together with the entire game.

<margin>104.</margin> If, however, **the Black** [player] does not move the King to sixty-two [[note: Square f8]] or forty-five [[note: Square e6]] (as seen in nos. 102 and 103), but rather to sixty-one [[note: Square e8]]: then **the White** [player] moves the Queen to the Black King’s Rook’s [starting square] at forty-four [[note: Square d6]]: and subsequently delivers checkmate with the same from fifty-three [[note: Square e7]].

<margin>105.</margin> But if **the White** [player] does not [move] with the Rook to fifty-two [[note: Square d7]] (as in no. 101), but instead offers check with the Queen on twenty-one [[note: Square e3]] and moves her to the Black King’s Rook’s [file]: then **the Black** [player] moves the King to sixty-two [[note: Square f8]]: **The White** [player] takes the Black King’s Rook with the Queen: **The Black** [player] moves the King to fifty-five [[note: Square g7]] (to avoid losing his Rook to a discovered check from the White Queen and a check from the White King’s Rook from sixty [[note: Square d8]]): **The White** [player] takes the Black King’s Archer’s Soldier [[note: the Bishop’s Pawn]] with the Queen: wherefore his Rook, as well as the Black King’s Rider’s Soldier [[note: the Knight’s Pawn]], stand in danger: and **the Black** [player], regardless of what he may move, can be defeated in just a few moves.

<margin>106.</margin> If, however, **the Black** [player] does not take the White Rider [[note: Knight]] (as in no. 100), but instead moves the King to sixty-two [[note: Square f8]]: then **the White** [player] offers <margin>107.</margin> check with the King’s Rider from fifty-two [[note: Square d7]] and moves him onto the Black King’s Rook’s [file]: * **The Black** [player] [moves] the King to sixty-one [[note: Square e8]]: **The White** [player] takes the Black Queen with the King’s Rook and offers check: **The Black** [player] takes him with the King: **The White** [player] moves his Queen (out of danger) to the Black King’s Rook’s [starting square] at twenty-one [[note: Square e3]] and offers check: **The Black** [player] must move the King to sixty [[note: Square d8]]: **The White** [player] takes the Black King’s Rook.
