# Of the Game of Chess.

[[margin: 10.cap:]]

15. \* Black / takes / the White King’s Knight [[notes: *Reuter* — literally "Rider," the early modern German term for the Knight.]] / with the King’s Bishop [[notes: *Schütze* — "Archer," the term used here for the Bishop.]]: White [takes] / the same / with the King’s Rook [[notes: *Rochen* — an older term for the Rook, derived from "Roc."]]: Black / moves / his King’s Rook / or the Queen / to the White King’s Bishop’s [square] / at **fifty-nine**: White [moves] / the King’s Rook / to **three** / and will thus / provided He / knows how to play / likely win the game.

16. If, however, Black / does not take / the White Knight / (n. 15.) with the Bishop / but instead / moves the Queen / to the White King’s Bishop’s [square] / at **fifty-nine** : White plays / the same [Knight] / to **thirty-three** : Black / takes the White King’s Knight / with his King’s Bishop : White [takes] / the Black King’s Knight / with the Queen : Black / gives / Check with the Queen / from **eleven** / and simultaneously defends / her King’s Bishop : White / moves the King / to **five** : Black / (to / prevent / the Check / from the White King’s Bishop / at **thirty-four**) [moves] the King’s Rook’s Pawn [[notes: *Soldaten* — "Soldiers," the term for Pawns.]] / to **forty-one** : White [moves] / the King’s Knight / (to / keep an eye / on the third square) / to **thirteen** : Black [moves] / the King’s Rook / to **fifty-nine** : White [moves] / the Queen / (so that / the Black King’s Rook / from **twenty-seven** / does not harass her) / to **twenty-one** : Black / [moves] the King’s Knight / to the White King’s Pawn / at **forty-six** : White / [moves] the King’s Knight / against the Black Queen / at **twenty-eight** : Black / takes / the White King’s Pawn / with that Knight / and likewise aims / at the White Queen : White / if He takes the Black Queen / wins the following / (let Black move / as He will) / his King’s Bishop / or his King’s Knight / indeed even the entire game.

17. If, however, Black / does not [move] his Knight / to **43** (n. 2.) but instead / moves his Queen / to **fifty-two** : then He will / be better able / to arrange his game : and White / must not / move the King’s Pawn / (because otherwise / his King’s Pawn / would be taken / by the Black Queen)
