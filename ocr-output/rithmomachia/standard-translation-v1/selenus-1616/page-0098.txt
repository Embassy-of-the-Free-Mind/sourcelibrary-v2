# The First Book / Chapter 7:

> » ...[they] shall be humble: for however much / the Man is higher and more / trusted / so much / shall he be more humble: 4. patient with words / and free of penance [[notes: *ledig an buß* here implies being slow to punish or being merciful]] / so that they / the subjects / do not easily torment / nor treat poorly: 5. They shall not desire / excessive Riches: For thus did / the Ancient wise Princes / who desired more wealth / in the Name / and in the lands / or people / than in gold: 6. they shall have / generosity [[notes: *miltikeit* refers to the medieval virtue of "largesse," the open-handed giving expected of nobility]] / and also grant the fiefs [[notes: *lehen*, the land or rights granted by a lord to a vassal]]: for if they do that / then all people bear themselves much more easily / under what they / through the princes / must suffer.
> 
> It is, however, now to be known / concerning the Rooks / when all the stones [[notes: *gestein*, the chess pieces]] / by whatever name they are called / are set in their places / each has its [own] way out from its place / except the Rook alone / for whom one must first clear the way [[notes: This refers to the fact that in the starting position, the Rook is "trapped" behind a row of pawns and cannot move until a path is opened]] / before it may go from its place. And that is because / the Rook / as written above / signifies the Vicedom [[notes: *Bistumb* or *Vistum*, the office of a high-ranking deputy or administrative governor]] / and the Lords' Stewards / or their Administrators. And while they are in the Lord's Court [[notes: *Hof*, the central royal household]] / they have no power: but when they come into their Vicedom office / then they have power / as the Lord himself / has power to do / when he is there: for their power is great: for what they do / they do / by their Lord's strength: and therefore / when they have nothing before them / they go from square to square [[notes: *spang zu Spangen*]] / backwards or forwards / to both sides / to protect / or to harm / if nothing hinders them / only diagonally [[notes: *über eck*]] / they may not go: and that signifies their great power / which they have in war [[notes: *urleug*]] : and therefore they may capture / or strike / and judge / what they will / even the King / who is their opponent / him they may lay low and / rob / of his body and life. etc.
