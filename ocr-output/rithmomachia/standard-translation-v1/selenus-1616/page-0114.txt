# The First Book / Chapter 9:

[[meta: The text continues from the previous page's description of the Knight's initial moves and his relationship to the King and Queen.]]

>> as the one on the left hand / and the other before the King / 
>> as the one on the right hand / just as they / surround the King / 
>> and the Queen / like a crown / when they begin the battles / 
>> and seek the field / and encounter one another / then knightly 
>> strength / reveals itself perfectly in them: For the Knight / 
>> may not be recognized / how he fights / unless it be / 
>> that he shows his strength / through fighting. The strong, 
>> inexperienced Knights / are accustomed to this: First / 
>> when they receive the armor [[notes: *Harnasch*, referring to the full suit of plate armor.]] / 
>> they suffer and turn pale / and blood / comes from their noses: 
>> this sign / is attributed more to virtue [[notes: *frumkait* (Frömmigkeit), which in this period often meant excellence, bravery, or integrity rather than just religious piety.]] / 
>> than to vice: For it is believable / that he / who is first 
>> struck with fear / when he comes to battle / will fight 
>> knightly / and will not turn his back / when he arrives 
>> there / where the fear of death / which has naturally 
>> occurred beforehand / is no longer seen as a present burden / 
>> but rather that he strikes back / strongly in battle / 
>> so that nature / at first lends a hand / to the power of wrath: 
>> For he is of a great heart and mind / who thinks 
>> of a great peril [[notes: *verdamnus* (Verdammnis), usually meaning damnation, but here used for the extreme danger or "condemnation" of the battlefield.]] / 
>> so that he does not yield / but rather shows himself superior 
>> to it / and gives himself firmly to fighting / with the 
>> enemies: and the Knights demonstrate this / as they at 
>> first cannot go / except to three fields [[notes: This describes the Knight's limited mobility when starting at the edge of the chessboard.]] / 
>> and by the ends of the realm: but as they / go out from 
>> the realm's ends and regions / with commanded and 
>> received power / they leap / to as many as eight fields [[notes: Once a Knight moves toward the center of the board, his potential moves increase to eight squares, symbolizing his growing influence and power.]] / 
>> and strike down / the enemies they find: Thus it is with 
>> every person / who feels humble things of himself / 
>> when he is led forth / with 
-> virtues / so he shines more clearly: for whoever <-
-> humbles himself / shall be exalted. *etc.* <-
