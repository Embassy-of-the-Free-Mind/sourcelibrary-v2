# The Third Book / Chapter 5:

89. If, however, the **White** [player] does not offer **Check** with the **Rider** [[note: the Knight]] from 51 [[note: Square c7]] (no. 88) but instead takes the **Black King’s Knight-Soldier** [[note: the Pawn on g7]] with the same: thereby indicating a **Check** from that square and a **Discovered Check** from the **King’s Rook**: then the **Black** [player] must move the **King** to sixty-two [[note: Square f8]] or fifty-four [[note: Square f7]]; (for if he were to place him on sixty [[note: Square d8]], he would be immediately dispatched by the **White Queen** from fifty-two [[note: Square d7]]) the **White** [player] then places the **King’s Rook** upon [the square of] the **Black Queen** at six [[note: Square f1]]: The **Black** [player] takes it with the same [[note: the Queen]] and offers **Check**: The **White** [player] seizes the same with the **King’s Archer** [[note: the Bishop]]: The **Black** [player] must either relieve the **King’s Rider** [[note: the Knight]] or move it out of danger: Since the **White** [player] still has the **Queen** and an **Archer** [[note: Bishop]] to use against the **Black** [player], he can henceforth easily arrange his moves for victory.

90. If, however, the **Black** [player] does not move the **Archer** [[note: the Bishop]] to 45 [[note: Square e6]] (no. 69.) but rather to forty-seven [[note: Square g6]]: then the **White** [player] moves the **King’s Archer** [[note: the Bishop]] toward the **Black Queen** and her **Rook** [[note: the Queen's Rook]] at thirty-nine [[note: Square g5]]: The **Black** [player] moves the **Queen** to sixty-one [[note: Square e8]]: The **White** [player] takes the **Black King’s Rook** with the **King’s Archer**: The **Black** [player] takes the same with the **Queen**: The **White** [player] takes the **Black King’s Rider** [[note: the Knight]] with the **King’s Rook** and moves it toward the **Black Queen**: The **Black** [player] moves the same [[note: the Queen]] to forty-six [[note: Square f6]]: The **White** [player] offers,

91. with the **King’s Rook** from sixty [[note: Square d8]], **Check**: * The **Black** [player] takes it with the **Queen**: The **White** [player] takes the same with his **Queen** and gives **Checkmate**.

92. But if the **Black** [player] does not take the **White Rook** (no. 91.) but instead moves the **King** to fifty-one [[note: Square c7]]: then the **White** [player] offers **Check** with the **Queen** from fifty-two [[note: Square d7]]: The **Black** [player] must retreat with the **King** to forty-two [[note: Square b6]]: The **White** [player] offers **Check** with the **King’s Rider** [[note: the Knight]] from thirty-six [[note: Square d5]],

93. and moves him onto [the square of] the **Black Queen**: * The **Black** [player] takes him with the **King’s Knight-Soldier** [[note: the Pawn on c6]]: The **White** [player] offers **Check** with the **Queen** from thirty-four [[note: Square b5]]: The **Black** [player] must set the **King** upon [the square of] the **White King’s Rook** at fifty-one [[note: Square c7]]: The **White** [player] gives **Check** with the same [[note: the Rook]] from fifty-two [[note: Square d7]]: The **Black** [player] moves the **King** to fifty-eight [[note: Square b8]] or fifty-nine [[note: Square c8]]: The **White** [player] takes the **Black King’s Knight-Soldier** [[note: the Pawn]] with the **Queen** and gives **Checkmate**.
