# The Third Chapter

[[notes: This section explains the opening layout for Rythmomachia, or the "Philosophers' Game." Unlike Chess, which focuses on military hierarchy, Rythmomachia was an educational tool used to teach "Boethian Arithmetic." This system, based on the works of the 6th-century philosopher Boethius, classified numbers by their proportions and relationships. The game was played on a board of 8x16 squares (effectively two chessboards joined together).]]
