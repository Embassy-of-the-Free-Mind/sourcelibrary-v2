->### 1. cap: Of the Game of Chess.<-

[[margin: 1.cap:]]
...described in the aforementioned treatise, or explanation of the Game of Chess / with the following words / (in which he / has been translated into German from various anonymous authors, as many written / and old printed copies of the same / have come into our hands):

The **King** / takes his form / after a living **King** / when he shall sit / in his palace / dressed in purple: .. this signifies / that he shall be / crowned and clothed with virtues / and with grace: with these / the King’s / courage and will / shall be adorned and improved / in these things he shall shine / before other people / just as he appears before them / in his garments: .. He shall have a **Crown** / upon his head: that signifies / his high dignity / for his / dignity / is an honor to his people: for all his subjects / look upon him / and are obedient to him. In his **left hand** / he shall have a **golden Apple**: [[notes: The "golden apple" refers to the *Reichsapfel* or Sovereign's Orb, a symbol of the monarch's worldy power and his duty to protect the realm.]] .. with which he shows / that he is an overseer / or a protector / and a provider / for all his subjects / either by himself / or through his administrators. [[notes: *Verwesern* refers to regents, stewards, or high-ranking officials who govern in the King's name.]] In his **right hand** / he shall have a **Scepter** / that signifies his power / with which he shall subdue the wicked / who cannot / be tamed / by body or goods. ..

First / the King / shall be meek / and from him / mercy shall shine: Second / the King / shall be truthful / in words and in works / so that he / hates lies / at all times: Third / the King / shall be good. For it is impossible / that a good man / should ever be lost / or die an unjust death: But fierce people [[notes: *Freysam leut* refers to those who are bold, cruel, or given to violence.]] / one reads / that they often die an evil death: Fourth / the King shall be just: for kingdoms without justice / are dens of murderers. [[notes: *Mordwinckel*, literally "murder-corners." This is a likely reference to St. Augustine’s famous dictum in *The City of God*: "Justice being taken away, then, what are kingdoms but great robberies?"]] Fifth / the King / shall be chaste / so / that he / shall not sin / or desire / any other women / but only / the **Queen** / Therefore she is placed / at his left side. It is also believable / that a King who is chaste / just and truthful / deserves from God / that he should bear a son / who is equal to him / in the same virtues: for he shall / not decline / when his father's virtue leads him / to virtues / so that he follows him / in character / from whom he has come / in being: For it is / somewhat / very much against Nature / that a King / should have another woman / or have more than one.
