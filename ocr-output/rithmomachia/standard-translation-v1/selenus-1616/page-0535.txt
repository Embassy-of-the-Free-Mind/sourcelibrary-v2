### ->On the Battle of Numbers.<-

*...number of Soldiers, so the individual **Standard-Bearers** [[notes: Latin: *AnteSignani*; those who fight in front of the standards. In this game, these refer to the high-value pieces.]] of the Turks surpass our Christians by 44 Soldiers; so that the entire Army of the Turks is 440 Soldiers greater in number than ours: nevertheless, the Red Counters [[notes: *Calculi*; the circular, square, or triangular pieces used in the game. Here, Red represents the Turks and White represents the Christians.]] express larger Numbers than the White.*

*In the very encounter and beginning of this Playful Battle, the two Commanders shall come to this agreement: namely, that he shall be the Victor and shall triumph who carries away more **Signs** [[notes: *Signa*; standards or trophies, representing captured enemy pieces.]] from the Enemy.*

*But if our Caesar [[notes: Referring to the Holy Roman Emperor as the leader of the Christian side.]] should surpass the Turk himself by Two Signs:*
