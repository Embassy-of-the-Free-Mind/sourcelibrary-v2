### Das Dritte Buch / 1. cap:
[[notes: The Third Book / Chapter 1.]]

...Queen / (provided the Black player / has moved the King / to 60 [[notes: Square d8]]) with the King’s Rook / and delivers / from his Bishop / a Discovered Check-Mate [[notes: *Ab-Schach-Mat* refers to a discovered check that results in mate.]].

[[margin: 26.]] But if the Black player / before He moves the Knight / to 53 [[notes: Square e7]] (no. 5) / captures the White King’s Soldiers [[notes: *Soldaten* (soldiers) are pawns.]] / with the King’s Bishop’s Soldier:
[[margin: 27.]] * then White takes / the same / with the King’s Bishop’s Soldier: The Black player / moves against him / the King’s Knight / to fifty-three [[notes: Square e7]]: The White player / (to protect it) / [moves] the King’s Bishop / to twenty-seven [[notes: Square c4]]: The Black player / [moves] against him / the King’s Knight’s Soldier / to thirty-four [[notes: Square b5]]: The White player / [moves] the same [the Bishop] to eighteen [[notes: Square b3]]: The Black player / [moves] the King’s Bishop / to fifty [[notes: Square f7]] / and thus wins / although the game / is placed somewhat in disorder / the White King’s Bishop’s Soldier [[notes: White's pawn on the c-file]] / by force.

[[margin: 28.]] Now if the White player / captures the Black Soldier / not with his Soldier / (no. 27) but rather / (in order to win a Soldier) / with the Queen: then Black takes her / with his Queen / and offers Check: White / takes the same / with the King’s Bishop’s Soldier: The Black player / moves / the King’s Bishop / against the White King’s Bishop’s Soldier / to thirty-five [[notes: Square c5]]:
[[margin: 29.]] * The White player / (in order to exchange the Bishops / and bring the Soldiers closer together) / [moves] the King’s Bishop / to twenty-one [[notes: Square e3]]: Although this move / appears good and useful / it is nevertheless somewhat dangerous: For / Black / takes away this White Bishop / with his King’s Bishop: White / [takes] the same / with the King’s Bishop’s Soldier: The Black player / moves / the King’s Knight / against the White King’s Bishop’s Soldier / to forty-six [[notes: Square f6]]: White / (to protect it) / [moves] the King’s Bishop / to twenty-seven [[notes: Square c4]]: The Black player / [moves] the King’s Rook / against the White King’s Bishop’s Soldier / to sixty-one [[notes: Square e8]]: White / cannot protect it / (even if He moves the King / to twelve / or thirteen [[notes: Square d2 or e2]]): For Black / advances the King’s Knight / to thirty-one [[notes: Square g4]] / and wins / through this intended move / (after White / has moved / the King) either the White King’s Bishop’s Soldier: or their Rook: or / (provided the King / remained on his field / and the King’s Knight / in order / to protect the fourteenth field [[notes: Square f2]] / had been moved to twenty-four [[notes: Square h3]]) their Bishop.

[[margin: 30.]] Accordingly, the move / * of the White King’s Bishop / to 21. (no. 29) [is a harm...]
