# On the Game of Chess.

[[margin: 13.cap: [[notes: Chapter 13.]]]]
**The Black** / (to further protect the King's Soldier [[notes: "Soldier" (Soldat) was a common 17th-century term for a Pawn.]]) [moves] the same to eighteen: **The White** / the King’s Rider [[notes: "Rider" (Reuter) refers to the Knight.]] / (with which he / [brings] the Black Queen / into his power, if he subsequently moves it to twelve: or / provided that **the Black** / has taken with the King’s Archer’s [[notes: "Archer" (Schütze) refers to the Bishop.]] King's Soldier / moves it to twenty-eight) to twenty-two. Even if / in this latter case / the Black Queen / is moved to forty-two: **the White** / with the King’s Rider / gives **Check** from forty-five / and catches her / either with the King’s Archer / or Soldier.
