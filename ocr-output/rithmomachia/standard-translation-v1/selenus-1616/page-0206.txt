### The Second Book / Chapter 8

...onto the White King’s Rider [[notes: *W. K. Reuter*; the King's Knight]] / on forty-one [[notes: *ein und vierzig*; Square f5]]: **The White player** / takes / with his King’s Soldier [[notes: *K. Soldaten*; the King's Pawn]] / the Black King’s Soldier / and offers a challenge to the same [[notes: *beut derselben einen Kampf an*; this refers to an attacking move or a gambit]]: **The Black player** / takes the same / with his King’s Archer-Soldier [[notes: *K. Schützens-Soldaten*; the King's Bishop's Pawn]]: **The White player** / [takes] the Black King’s Archer-Soldier / with the Queen / and gives check: **The Black player** / takes the same / with his Queen: **The White player** / [takes] the same / with the King’s Rider / and moves him / toward the Black King’s Rook [[notes: *S. K. Rochen*; the Black King's Castle]]: **The Black player** / robs [[notes: *raubet*; captures]] the same / with his King: **The White player** / [takes] the Black King’s Soldier / with the King’s Archer [[notes: *K. Schützen*; the King's Bishop]]: and has thus / won / one Soldier / more.

8. &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; But if **the Black player** / does not move the Queen to 36 [[notes: Square e5]] (no. 7) but rather the King’s Rider / (to rescue the King’s Archer-Soldier) to forty-one [[notes: Square f5]]: then **the White player** / moves / against the Black King’s Archer / the King’s Soldier / to twenty-nine [[notes: *neun und zwantzig*; Square f4]]: **The Black player** / [moves] the same / to forty-seven [[notes: *sieben und vierzig*; Square h6]]: **The White player** / takes / the Black King’s Soldier / with the King’s Archer: **The Black player** / (to make a plan / against **the White player's** Rider / when his King’s Archer-Soldier / is subsequently moved / to forty-three [[notes: *drey und vierzig*; Square f6]] / against him) moves the Queen / to fifty-two [[notes: *zwey und funfzig*; Square d7]]: **The White player** / (to save the King’s Rider / on twenty-eight [[notes: Square e4]]) [moves] the King’s Soldier / to thirty-six [[notes: *sechs und dreyssig*; Square e5]]: But if He moves / the King’s Rook / to three [[notes: Square c1]]: and **the Black player** / [moves] the King’s Archer-Soldier / against this White Rider / to forty-three: then He places / the mentioned Rider / on forty-four [[notes: *vier und vierzig*; Square e6]]: If **the Black player** now / wishes to cause / an exchange / of the Kings' Riders / by means of his King’s Archer: he nevertheless / has / a well-set game / with which he / can easily defeat and overcome the enemy.

9. &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; But if **the Black player** / on the fourth move / does not move the Queen to 46 [[notes: Square g6]] (no. 2) but rather / the King’s Rider / to forty-three [[notes: *drey und vierzig*; Square f6]]: **the White player** nevertheless / makes the attack / on **the Black player's** forces [[notes: *hauffen*; literally "heap" or "host"]] / with the King’s Archer-Soldier / to twenty-seven [[notes: *sieben und zwantzig*; Square d4]]: **The Black player** / takes the White King’s Rider / with his King’s Archer: **The White player** / [takes] the same / with the King’s Rook: **The Black player** / [takes] the White King’s Archer-Soldier / with his King’s Soldier: **The White player** / (so that / the King’s Archer / may obtain / a free attack / against this Black Soldier) moves the King’s Soldier / to twenty-one [[notes: *ein und zwantzig*; Square f3]]: **The Black player** / protects it / with his King’s Rider-Soldier [[notes: *K. Reuters-Soldaten*; the King's Knight's Pawn]] / to thirty-four [[notes: *vier und dreyssig*; Square c5]].
