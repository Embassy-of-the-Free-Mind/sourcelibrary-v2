# Chess - or King-Game.

->From<-

## GUSTAVUS SELENUS,
[[notes: "Gustavus Selenus" was the pseudonym of Augustus the Younger, Duke of Brunswick-Lüneburg (1579–1666). The name is an anagram: "Gustavus" for "Augustus," and "Selenus" from the Greek goddess of the moon, Selene, referring to his seat in Lüneburg (Luna-burg).]]

## RYTHMO-MACHIA.
[[notes: Also known as "The Philosopher's Game," Rithmomachia was a highly complex medieval board game involving arithmetic and geometry. It was often used as a tool for teaching mathematical proportions.]]
