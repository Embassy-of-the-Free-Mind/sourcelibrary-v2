# About the Number-Battle.

The **Twelfth**, A, D, likewise C, or the **difference** of A, C (which is 6) compared against the difference of C, D (which is 2). Likewise B, compared against the differences of A, B and B, C (which is 3). [[notes: A ratio of 3:1, such as 6:2 or 9:3, creates the musical Twelfth.]]

The **Fifteenth**, the difference A, D (8) held against the difference C, D (2). Likewise A, held against the differences A, B and B, C (which is 3). [[notes: A ratio of 4:1, such as 8:2, creates the Double Octave or Fifteenth.]]

The **Tone**, the number B compared with the difference A, D (8). [[notes: In the context of Rithmomachia, the Tone is a 9:8 ratio.]]

The **Fifth**, A, C, likewise B, or the difference A, C (4) compared against D, or against the difference A, D (6). Likewise the differences B, D and A, B (3) compared against the difference C, D (2). [[notes: All these comparisons result in the 3:2 ratio of a Perfect Fifth: 12:8, 9:6, or 3:2.]]

The **Fourth**, A, B, likewise C, compared against D, or against the difference A, D (6). Likewise the difference A, C (4) compared against the differences A, B and B, D (3). [[notes: These result in the 4:3 ratio of a Perfect Fourth: 12:9, 8:6, or 4:3.]]

The **Octave**, the A against D, or against the difference A, D (6). Likewise C, against the difference A, C (4). Likewise D, or the difference A, D (6), compared against the differences A, B and B, D (3). Likewise the difference A, C (4), or the difference B, C (1), compared with the difference C, D (2). [[notes: All these result in the 2:1 ratio of an Octave: 12:6, 8:4, 6:3, or 2:1.]]

The **Twelfth**, the A against the difference A, C (4). Likewise B, or the difference B, C (1), compared against the differences A, B and B, D (3). Likewise D, and the difference A, D (6), compared against the difference D, C (2). [[notes: These result in the 3:1 ratio of a Twelfth: 12:4, 3:1, or 6:2.]]

The **Fifteenth**, the A compared against the differences A, B and B, D (3). Likewise C, against the difference C, D (2). Likewise the difference A, C compared against the difference B, C (1). [[notes: These result in the 4:1 ratio of a Fifteenth: 12:3, 8:2, or 4:1.]]

The **Fifth**, the D compared against the difference A, D (8). [[notes: 12:8 simplifies to the 3:2 ratio of a Fifth.]]

The **Fourth**, A, C, and B, D, likewise the differences A, B and B, D (4) compared against the difference C, D (3). [[notes: 20:15, 16:12, and 4:3 all represent the ratio of a Fourth.]]

The **Octave**, the B, or the differences A, B and B, D (4) compared against the difference A, D (8). [[notes: 16:8 and 8:4 both represent the 2:1 ratio of an Octave.]]

The **Twelfth**, the D compared against the differences B, D and B, A (4). Likewise C, compared against the difference A, C (5). [[notes: 12:4 and 15:5 both represent the 3:1 ratio of a Twelfth.]]
