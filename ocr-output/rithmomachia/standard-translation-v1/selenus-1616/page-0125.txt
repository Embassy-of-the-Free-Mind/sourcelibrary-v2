&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;**The third Pawn** [[notes: *Bend* is an older term for a pawn]] upon the chessboard shall stand before the **Elder** [[notes: This refers to the piece we now call the Bishop, which in medieval chess was often depicted as a Sage or Elder (Alphyn).]], who is to the right hand of the King. The Elder is like a **Judge**, and since war often arises between the people described above, the Pawn shall stand before him in such a form: that he be a man holding a pair of **shears** in his right hand, and in his left a **broad knife**, and at his belt a **writing kit**, and sticking behind his right ear a **quill** for writing. By the shears are signified: tailors, wool-workers, weavers, and all those who deal with linen or woolen cloth, whether they be shearers or weavers, as they trade or work with it through labor or purchase. By the knife are signified: butchers, tanners, shoemakers, tawers [[notes: *Iricher* were craftsmen who prepared white leather using alum and salt, rather than bark tannins.]], furriers [[notes: *Kürsner* refers to those who make or repair fur garments.]], and of [such] handiwork there is much. And by the writing kit are signified: scribes; and by the quill at the ear, that they should be ready at all times when the Judge summons them, to write what he commands them: judgments, or **charters** [[notes: *Handfest* refers to a sealed deed, privilege, or legal contract.]], or whatever is necessary at that time. The craftsmen mentioned above work with all kinds of hides or hair that are taken from skins. To all these same craftsmen belong three things which they should possess: The first is **truth** in their work, against all untruth and injustice. The second is **truth** in their words, so that they never mislead anyone with words, nor promise what they do not intend to do. The third is **truth** in all deeds and justice; these same people should be just, honorable, and of good character, without discord; faithful, without deception or falsehood; friendly and kind, without hatred; and diligent in their work, without delay.
