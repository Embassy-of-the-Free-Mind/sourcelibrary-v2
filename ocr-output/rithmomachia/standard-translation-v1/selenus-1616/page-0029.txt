[[margin: Xerxes Philometer.]]
[[notes: Philometer (or Philometor) is a name often attributed to a legendary Chaldean philosopher who, according to medieval and Renaissance tradition, invented chess to teach morality to a king.]]

[[notes: Evil-Merodach was a historical King of Babylon (son of Nebuchadnezzar). In the "Chess Book" by Jacobus de Cessolis—one of the most copied works of the Middle Ages—chess was supposedly created to cure this king of his madness and tyranny.]]
