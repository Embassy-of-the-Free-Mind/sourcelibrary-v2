# [[markup: Mirrored Title]]

->A Prayer-Book<-
->of the<-

[[notes: This text refers to Saint Ephrem the Syrian (c. 306–373 AD), a prolific Syriac composer of hymns and biblical exegesis. His works were popular in 17th-century Germany as "Bet-Bücher" (Prayer Books) for personal devotion. The fact that this text appears here suggests that the printer, Henning Gross the Younger, was using the same press or paper stock for a devotional project at the same time as this chess treatise.]]
