### The Fourth Book / Chapter 10:

**Black** [moves] the **King** to eighteen: **White** [moves] his **King** to one, where he stood at the beginning: **Black** offers **Checkmate** with the other **Rook** [[notes: *Rochen*, the traditional term for the Rook or Castle]] from square three.

4.) **White** places the **King** on seven: a **Pawn** [[notes: *Fende* or *Soldat*, literally a "foot soldier"]] on twenty-five: **Black** [places] the **King** (to hold back the White **Pawn**) on thirty-three: and four **Pawns** side-by-side on twenty, twenty-one, twenty-two, and twenty-three: So that now, one of these four Black **Pawns** shall **Mate** the White **King** in the twelfth move, either on eight or on six:

So **Black** moves a **Pawn** (from 21) to thirteen: **White** [moves] the **King** to eight:

**Black** [moves] a **Pawn** (from 20) to twelve: **White** [moves] the **King** to seven:

**Black** [moves] a **Pawn** (from 12) to four and makes it a **Queen** [[notes: Promotion—in this era, a pawn reaching the furthest rank was "made a Queen"]]: **White** moves the **King** to eight:

**Black** [moves] the newly obtained **Queen** to eleven: **White** [moves] the **King** to seven:

**Black** [moves] the **Queen** to twenty: **White** [moves] the **King** to eight:

**Black** [moves] the **Queen** to twenty-nine: **White** [moves] the **King** to seven:

**Black** [moves] the **Queen** to thirty-eight: **White** [moves] the **King** to eight:

**Black** [moves] the **Queen** to thirty-one: **White** [moves] the **King** to seven:

**Black** [moves] the **Queen** to twenty-four: **White** [moves] the **King** to eight:

**Black** [moves] a **Pawn** (from 13) to five and makes yet another **Queen**: **White** moves the **King** to seven:

**Black** offers **Check** with the queen just mentioned from fourteen: **White** moves the **King** to eight or six:

**Black** [moves] the first-made **Queen** (from 24) to fifteen and gives **Checkmate**.
