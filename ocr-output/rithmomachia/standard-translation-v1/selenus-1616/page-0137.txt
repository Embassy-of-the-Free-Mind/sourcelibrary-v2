# Of the Game of Chess.

[[margin: II. cap:]]
...of the **Archer-** and **Rider-Soldiers** [[notes: These refer to the pawns standing in front of the Bishops (Archers) and Knights (Riders)]]: the **Rook-Soldiers**, however, protect only one square, namely the third of the **Riders**. Because of this "quartering" of the **Chess-Pieces**, the **Chess-Table** [[notes: *Schach-Tabel*, the board]] is first divided into two **Main Quarters**: one called the **White King’s** quarter, and the other the **Black King’s**. According to the following diagram, each quarter contains four **transverse lines** [[notes: *zwerch-linien*, modernly called ranks or rows]]: the outermost and first is called the **Head-line**; the second, that of the **Soldiers**; the third, the **White** or **Black King’s** third; the fourth, their fourth line, with which the border of each King ends.
