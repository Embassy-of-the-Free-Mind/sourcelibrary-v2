The **Diatessaron**, or the **Fourth**, can be found and obtained from B, or from the difference between A and D (4 or 8), compared with the difference between A and C (3 or 6), or compared with C. [[notes: In Pythagorean tuning, the Fourth is defined by the ratio 4:3.]]

The **Diapason**, or the **Octave**, is understood by holding A against C, or against the difference A, C (which is 6). In like manner, it is found by holding B against D, or against the differences B, A, and B, D (2 or 4). [[notes: The Octave is defined by the ratio 2:1.]]

The **Diapason with the Diapente**, or the **Twelfth**, is established by A against D, or against the differences B, A, and B, D (2 or 4). Also, C, or the difference A, C (3 or 6), compared and held against the differences C, B, and C, D (1 or 2). [[notes: The Twelfth—an octave plus a fifth—is defined by the ratio 3:1.]]

The **Disdiapason**, or the **Fifteenth**, is indicated by B, or the difference A, D (4 or 8), compared against the differences B, C, and C, D (which is 1). [[notes: The Fifteenth—a double octave—is defined by the ratio 4:1.]]

The **Fifth** is B compared against the differences B, A, and B, C (which is 10). [[notes: 15 to 10 simplifies to a 3:2 ratio, the "Sesquialter" ratio of a Perfect Fifth.]] In like manner, D compared against the difference D, C (which is 2). [[notes: 3 to 2 ratio.]]

The **Fourth** is the difference A, C (20) compared with B. [[notes: 20 to 15 simplifies to a 4:3 ratio, the "Sesquitertial" ratio of a Perfect Fourth.]]

The **Octave** is the difference A, C (20) compared with the differences A, B, and B, C (which is 10). [[notes: 20 to 10 simplifies to a 2:1 ratio.]]

The **Twelfth** is B held against C. [[notes: 15 to 5 simplifies to a 3:1 ratio.]]

The **Fifteenth** is the difference between B and D (12) compared against D. [[notes: 12 to 3 simplifies to a 4:1 ratio.]]

The **Fifth** is found in B to C. [[notes: 9 to 6 simplifies to 3:2.]] Also C, or the difference A, C (6) compared with D. [[notes: 6 to 4 simplifies to 3:2.]] Also the differences B, C and A, B (3) compared with the difference C, D (2). [[notes: 3 to 2 ratio.]] Also A compared with the difference A, D (which is 8). [[notes: 12 to 8 simplifies to 3:2.]]

The **Fourth** is A to B. [[notes: 12 to 9 simplifies to 4:3.]] Also the difference A, D (8) held against C, or against the difference A, C (which is 6). [[notes: 8 to 6 simplifies to 4:3.]]

The **Octave** is A to C. [[notes: 12 to 6 simplifies to 2:1.]] Also the difference A, D (8) against D. [[notes: 8 to 4 simplifies to 2:1.]] Also the difference A, C (6), or C, held and compared against the differences B, C and A, B (which is 3). [[notes: 6 to 3 simplifies to 2:1.]]
