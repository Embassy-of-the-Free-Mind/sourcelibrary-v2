Now that the five ways in which the pieces should be taken away—namely **Counting**, **Addition**, **Subtraction**, **Multiplication**, and **Division** (which are shown in the art of arithmetic, but through this game are practiced manifold in the memory)—have been explained: there follows further the sixth and final type, namely **Besieging** [[notes: "Einsperren," literally "locking in" or "besieging."]], which occurs in the following manner.

When a piece of the opponent’s side is so surrounded that it cannot be moved through its own proper movement, nor be rescued by other pieces of its own company (since they cannot remove any of the four pieces which are attacking it and blocking its path): then it is considered as if besieged and locked in, and is removed from the game. For example: If a White [[term: Dreyecketer]] [[notes: "Dreyecketer" refers to a triangular piece.]] stands on L., and four Black pieces stand on M. N. O. P.: then the aforementioned Triangle, provided that no one from its own company comes to its aid, is ensnared and captured. Regarding this sixth type, it is to be remembered that all pieces in this game can besiege and be besieged or surrounded: only the White [[term: Viereckete]] [[notes: "Viereckete" refers to a square piece.]] piece marked with 153, and the Black square piece—or the bottom level of the Black [[term: Turnes]] [[notes: "Turnes" or "Tower" refers to the Pyramid, the most complex piece in the game, made of stacked shapes.]] marked with 190—are lost or won through this besieging.
