>> *Closing in, Besieging, or Interception, is carried out thus: Four*
>> *Pieces, in whatever way they are placed, whether in a straight line*
>> *or in a circuit, like Knights,* [[notes: The author uses the Latin "Sonipedes," which literally means "noisy-footed ones," a poetic term for horses. In gaming terms, this refers to the leaping move of a Knight in chess.]] *if they [surround] an opposing Piece, thus*
