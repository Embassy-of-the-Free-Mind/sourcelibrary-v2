### On the Battle of Numbers.

<sig>Nnn 2</sig>
<meta>catchword: Hanc</meta>

(Buxer: fol: 25. b. & 44. b.) [[notes: Reference to the works of Gustavi Seleni (Augustus the Younger) or his sources like Buxerius.]]

[[margin: He calls this mode of fighting a Meeting, or a Capture by Equality.]] [[notes: Although the margin mentions "Equality," the text clearly describes "Addition." In early mathematical games, the "meeting" of multiple numbers to equal another was often grouped under similar terminologies.]]

Through **Addition** / the stones / are taken or lost / as follows:

Two stones / with lower numbers / can capture one / with higher numbers / provided that their **combined sum** / matches the other’s number / and they / are moved into such spaces / from which they / according to their proper movement / can reach and hit / the space in which / the one with the higher numbers / is present : These two stones, however / remain on their own spaces : For example / if the **White Round Stone** / with 16, were on C: and the **White Triangular Stone** / with 9, were on D: and the **Black Round Stone** / marked with 25 / stood on E : then this Black stone / because / the two White stones' numbers / make 25 / and can reach the place / in which / the Black one / stands / is taken away from E : but they [the white stones] remain / on C and D. [[notes: Unlike Chess, where a piece replaces the one it captures, in Rythmomachia, pieces often capture "from a distance" if their mathematical value and their move-distance (range) align with the target.]]

The following numbers / win / and are conquered / in this game / by counting together : And there stand / on the left side / the two numbers / of the White company / which can slay a Black stone; or of the Black company / which can slay a White stone : On the right hand, however / the number / of the stone / which / because of the two aforementioned numbers / can be taken.
