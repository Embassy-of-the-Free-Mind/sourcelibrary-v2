# The Second Chapter

[[image-desc: Four numbers (5, 9, 13, 17) positioned above four squares. Each square contains a number (25, 81, 169, 289) with a small dot centered below it. [[notes: These represent the square values ($5^2, 9^2, 13^2, 17^2$) that belong to the white square pieces in the game.]]]]

The numbers of the remaining **White Square Stones** are found in the following way: One begins with 5 and takes four unequal numbers [[notes: "Unequal" here refers to odd numbers]] that follow one another, yet in such a way that one odd number is always skipped in between: namely, 5, 9, 13, 17. Thus, 7, 11, and 15 are not used. These numbers are then multiplied by themselves [[notes: squared]] and written upon the four last Square Stones as 25, 81, 169, and 289.

->(Buxer: fol. 14. a.)<-
[[notes: This refers to the work of Claude de Boissière, also known as Buxerius, a French mathematician who published a famous manual on Rythmomachia in 1556.]]

[[image-desc: A mathematical table detailing numerical progressions. The numbers in the final two rows (Required Superpartient and Required product...) are enclosed within square boxes, each with a dot positioned at the bottom center of the frame.]]

| | Third. | Fifth. | Seventh. | Ninth. |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| *Parts to be increased according to the ratio of the denominator.* | 5 | 9 | 13 | 17 |
| *The Increase.* | 10. | 36. | 78. | 136. |
| | *Two-parts over three.* | *Four-parts over five.* | *Six-parts over seven.* | *Eight-parts over nine.* |
| *Required Superpartient* | 15 | 45 | 91 | 153 |
| *The required product from the addition of the superpartient and the increase.* | 25 | 81 | 169 | 289 |

[[notes: This table demonstrates how the square values are derived using "superpartient" ratios. In Rythmomachia, a superpartient ratio is one where the larger number contains the smaller number plus multiple fractional parts of it (e.g., 25 is 15 plus two-thirds of 15). The "Increase" row added to the "Required Superpartient" row results in the final value of the square stones (25, 81, 169, 289).]]
