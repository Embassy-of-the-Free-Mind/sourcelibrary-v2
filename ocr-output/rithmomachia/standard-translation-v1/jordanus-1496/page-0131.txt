# Jacques Lefèvre d’Étaples’ Summary of the Two Books on Arithmetic by Saint Severinus Boethius, Dedicated to the Magnificent Lord: Gian Stefano Ferrero, Bishop of Vercelli.

Among the mathematical disciplines—without which all other learning is obscured—Arithmetic claims the first place for itself, as if it were the parent, leader, and mistress of the others. To be properly understood, just like the other disciplines, it requires certain aids. Among these, one must first fix in the mind a general **formula** [[notes: A "formula" here refers to a visual map or systematic classification table]] of the subjects it deals with; next, the definitions of individual terms; then the qualities and properties of numbers; and after the properties, the source from which all things are demonstrated.
