1/2 M [[notes: Likely an abbreviation for "half Morocco," a style of bookbinding where the spine and corners are covered in Morocco leather (goatskin), while the sides are covered in cloth or marbled paper.]]

**Incunabula** [[notes: From the Latin word for "cradle" or "swaddling clothes," this term refers to books printed during the infancy of the printing press, specifically those published before the year 1501.]]
**Jordanis** [[notes: The Latin genitive form of Jordanus, referring to Jordanus de Quedlinburg (c. 1300–1380), a prominent German Augustinian friar and author whose sermons were among the most widely read texts of the late Middle Ages.]]
