### Victory of body.

It is an easy thing to see that just as the body is that upon which wealth depends, so also the numbers depend upon the **pieces** [[notes: *Tables* refers to the physical gaming counters, similar to those used in backgammon]] of this game. Therefore, whoever shall have first won—by the rules or by the preceding combats—a certain quantity of pieces determined and proposed at the players' pleasure, without having regard for the characters or the values of them, shall have victory of body.

### Example.

Two players have proposed that whoever shall have first won four pieces through the preceding batteries [[notes: *Bateries* refers to the various methods of capturing an opponent's pieces, which the author likens to military engagements]], shall have won the game. Whoever then shall first have four pieces, whatever they may be, shall have victory of body.

### Victory of wealth.

Just as the wealth of many can often be possessed by one or more, so also the numbers of several pieces can be contained in one or more. Whoever then shall first have the sum of the number proposed and determined at the players' pleasure, without having regard for the quantity of the pieces, shall have victory of wealth.
