## On the application of numbers to the pieces.

Here it will be necessary to repeat what we have said concerning
proportions in Arithmetic: namely, that there are
three simple proportions: which are, multiple,
particular, and superpartient. [[notes: These terms refer to the "Pythagorean" ratios used in the game. "Multiple" (e.g., 2:1, 4:1), "Particular" or Superparticular (where the larger number contains the smaller plus one part of it, e.g., 3:2), and "Superpartient" (where the larger contains the smaller plus several parts of it, e.g., 5:3).]]
