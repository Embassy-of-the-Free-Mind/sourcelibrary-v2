Thus, the beginning of the proportion’s name will show you how many times you must retake the part of the **requesting number** written above; and having retaken or multiplied it, the **retaken number** will show you the quantity which, when joined to the **requesting number**, will always yield the proportional **required number**, as clearly appears by the example.
