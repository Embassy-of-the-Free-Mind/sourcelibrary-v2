# RYTHMOMACHIA, GAME.

[...ber 9 be in space A, it follows] that my two numbers 15 & 6 can go into space A [[notes: The "space" (*loge*) refers to a square on the game board.]] and that the one which is 6, being subtracted from the other 15, will leave 9, a number equal to that of the adversary which is also 9; thus, when I place the last of the two pieces in L or B, I shall take the adversary’s nine. This serves to take the proper numbers for subtraction in the present table.
