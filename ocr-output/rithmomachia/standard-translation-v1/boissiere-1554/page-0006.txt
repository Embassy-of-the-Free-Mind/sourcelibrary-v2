# TO THE MAGNANIMOUS AND IL-
## *lustrious Knight, Lord Antoine Escallin des Emars, Knight of the Order, & Captain General of the King’s galleys in the Western & Eastern seas, Claude de Boissière wishes Health, with the increase of all happiness.* [[notes: Antoine Escallin des Emars (c. 1498–1578), also known as Captain Polin or the Baron de La Garde, was a famous French naval commander and diplomat. The "Order" refers to the Order of Saint Michael, the highest chivalric order in France at the time. "Western and Eastern seas" (Ponant and Levant) are the traditional French terms for the Atlantic and Mediterranean coasts.]]
