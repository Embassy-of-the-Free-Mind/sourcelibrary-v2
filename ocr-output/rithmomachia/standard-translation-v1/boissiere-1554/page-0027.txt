### RYTHMOMACHIA, THE GAME
[[notes: Rythmomachia, also known as "The Philosophers' Game," was a complex mathematical board game popular from the 11th to the 17th centuries. It was used to teach Boethian number theory.]]

*tient: Consequently, coming to the third*
*requiring number, which is 45, in the rank of the evens,*

*thus choose a number that contains the afore-*
*said 45, and 4 fifths more. Now the fifth part*

*the four-fifths, which are 36, to-*

[[notes: The author is calculating a ratio of 81:45. In Renaissance math, "superquadrupartient fifths" means the larger number is equal to the smaller number plus four-fifths of it: $45 + (4/5 \times 45) = 45 + 36 = 81$. This simplifies to a 9:5 ratio.]]
