->RYTHMOMACHIA, THE GAME<-

...cut out two square pyramids, of which the bases or feet are equal to the other square pieces [[notes: These "square pieces" refer to the square-shaped counters mentioned on the previous page]], constructed in such a way that they have 6 tiers: that is to say, 6 squares placed one upon the other, always diminishing in size as they reach toward the summit and point of the pyramid, like steps. And then, the summits and points of these shall be cut and shortened, so that the one for the **even numbers** has 6 tiers remaining, and that space remains atop its summit to be able to later write two numerical characters [[notes: In Rithmomachia, each side has one Pyramid. The "Even" side's pyramid is usually made of six squares (36, 25, 16, 9, 4, 1), and the "Odd" side's pyramid is made of five (64, 49, 36, 25, 16), though variations exist]]. Similarly, for the other pyramid of the **odd numbers**, there must remain five tiers, with space above them at the summit to be able to contain three digits.
