1. The **Rounds** (one space diagonally) can move to **E, B, D,** or **C**.
2. The **Triangles** (two spaces in a straight line) can move to **G, F, H,** or **I**.
3. The **Squares** (three spaces in any direction) can move to the outer perimeter: **P, L, R, N** (straight) or **Q, K, O, M** (diagonally).
The additional letters **Y, V, S, T, X, Z,** and **&** illustrate the intermediate squares or alternative paths available during these maneuvers.]]
