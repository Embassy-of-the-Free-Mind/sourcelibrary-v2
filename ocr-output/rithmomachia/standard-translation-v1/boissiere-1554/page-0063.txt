->RYTHMOMACHIA, THE GAME<-

### Example.

Certain players have determined that whoever should first reach 100 would have won the game. If, therefore, one can obtain the two pieces [[notes: *Tables* refers to the physical gaming counters, similar to those used in backgammon or chess]] 64 and 36, which equal 100, or indeed a single piece of 100, without regard for the number of pieces provided the total value is present, he shall have the **victory of wealth**.

## Victory of Lawsuits.

Just as lawsuits [[notes: *Proces* (processes/lawsuits) is used here as a metaphor; the game was often called the "Philosophers' Game" and used social and legal analogies to explain its mathematical rules]] in themselves are nothing but papers and paintings containing wealth, similarly the characters on the pieces represent nothing but writing containing the numbers. Whoever shall first reach a prefixed value in a certain quantity of the said characters [[notes: *Caracteres* refers to the individual digits or numerals inscribed on the pieces]] at the players' pleasure, without having regard for the quantity of pieces, shall have the victory.

### Example.

The players have preordained that whoever shall first reach 100, or some other such value, in eight characters shall have won the game.
