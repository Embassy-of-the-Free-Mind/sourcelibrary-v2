# RYTHMOMACHIA, THE GAME
## On Geometric Proportion.

Geometric proportion occurs when the differences that the two outer numbers have from the middle number are in the same ratio as one of the outer numbers is to the middle. For example: 2, 4, 8. Here, you see that the difference between the first number (2) and the middle (4) is 2; similarly, the difference between the last number (8) and the middle (4) is 4. Therefore, it is clear that the two differences, 2 and 4, exist in a double ratio, just as the middle number 4 relates to whichever of the two outer numbers you choose (2 or 8); for 2 and 4 are in a duple ratio, and 4 and 8 are likewise.

[[notes: In the context of this game and Renaissance mathematics, a "Geometric Proportion" (what we would call a geometric progression) is defined by the relationship between the numbers. In the author's example of 2, 4, 8: the difference between 2 and 4 is 2, and the difference between 4 and 8 is 4. The ratio of those differences (2:4) is 1:2, which is the same as the ratio of the numbers themselves (2:4 or 4:8).]]
