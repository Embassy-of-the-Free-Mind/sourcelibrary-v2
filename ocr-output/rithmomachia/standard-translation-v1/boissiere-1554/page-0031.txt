...surface base and first foundation, one must place six grains [[notes: "Grains" refer to individual units or counters used to visualize the numbers]] in the first row of one of the sides. If you wish to multiply 6 by 6, as we have said in Arithmetic, you will have 36 grains, which will be the square base and foundation: having 6 on all sides. This square of 36 grains will be the first square, called the **base** and foundation of the pyramid: therefore, mark 36 at the bottom base of your pyramid as your first base. 

Afterward, to produce your pyramid by **tapering** it [[notes: *en eguisant* literally means "sharpening," referring to the narrowing of the piece as it rises toward a point]], the grains placed above the first ones for the second base must be fewer in number and quantity. And because there were 6 grains in the first row of the previous base, we will put only 5 on the side of the second base. We will arrange them in their square, as we showed in Arithmetic, containing only 25 grains. Such will be the second level or base. 

Mark then 25 at the second level or base of your pyramid; consequently, as you build your pyramid higher by tapering it, the grains placed above the second step or base must be of a lesser quantity than the said second: and because there were 5 in its first row, you will only put 4 in the first row of this third base: which, in their **squaring** [[notes: *quarreure* refers to the area or the formation of a square]], will produce 16.
