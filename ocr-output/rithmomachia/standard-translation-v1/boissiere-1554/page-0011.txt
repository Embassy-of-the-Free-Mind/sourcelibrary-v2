->RYTHMOMACHIA, THE GAME<-

*...could be a recreation for troubles, without however wasting one’s time, or straying from the limits of virtue. This game, although it was held in great esteem among the ancients and drawn from the harmonies and proportions of the subtle and ingenious Pythagoras [[notes: Pythagoras (c. 570–495 BC) was an ancient Greek philosopher who taught that the universe was built on mathematical ratios. This game was often attributed to his school in the Middle Ages and Renaissance.]]: Today, however, it is no longer in use because of the enigmatic interpretations in which, until now, it has been hidden and obscured. But seeing the utility and profit coming from it, I have set myself the duty of describing and unfolding it in such a way that not only the sorrowful may find recreation in it, but also all people of virtue, by recreating themselves within it, may acquire the habit of being able to promptly discern every proportion and harmony. In this world, there is nothing more fitting or subtle for better contemplating the quantities and admirable things in every kind of creature [[notes: In this context, "creature" refers to all of God's "creations"—the entire physical and natural world which was believed to be structured by divine geometry and music.]] than these things. Now, therefore, we shall proceed to declare it according to its order and tenor.*
