### To obtain the superparticular numbers in the field of odd numbers.

This operation does not differ from the preceding one, namely that to find the **requesting numbers** [[notes: "Requerants" are the intermediary products used to determine the mathematical proportions of the pieces]] one must multiply the first and smallest of the **odd numbers**, by the first and smallest of the opposite field [[notes: The "opposite field" refers to the even numbers side of the mathematical table]], and to obtain the **required numbers** [[notes: "Requis" are the final values that will be written on the game pieces themselves]] one must multiply those same opposites by themselves. 

But you must note that you will not take for the opposite the first number, which is 2; rather, you shall begin at the following number, 4. By multiplying the 3 [from the odd field] by this 4, you will have 12, the first requesting number; and that same 4 multiplied by itself will yield the first of the required numbers, 16.
