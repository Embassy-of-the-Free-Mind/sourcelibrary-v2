## Another kind of arrangement to easily find the numbers:

->And, Firstly.<-

### To find the required values of the multiples.

Multiply (as we said above) each of the **requerants** [[notes: The "requerants" (petitioners) are the base numbers used to generate higher-order pieces in the game.]] by themselves, and you will have the required multiples; just as 2 times 2 makes 4, and so on for the others.

### To find the required superparticulars

Add one unit to each of those of the first order which make up the **requerants** [[notes: In Rythmomachia, a "superparticular" ratio is one where a number contains another number plus one unit of it (e.g., 3:2, 4:3). These are formed by adding 1 to the base multiple.]].
