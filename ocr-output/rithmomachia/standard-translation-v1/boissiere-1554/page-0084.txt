### Another pleasant way of playing.

Following the report of **Thomas Randolph**, [[notes: An English diplomat and politician who studied at the University of Paris in the 1550s.]] a studious man at the University of Paris, a copy of the game of **Rythmomachia** was shared with me by a very kind person, **Thomas Toclife**, [[notes: Likely Thomas Topcliffe, another English scholar or traveler present in the academic circles of Paris at the time.]] an Englishman, and certainly a man of great erudition and mastery of the disciplines.
