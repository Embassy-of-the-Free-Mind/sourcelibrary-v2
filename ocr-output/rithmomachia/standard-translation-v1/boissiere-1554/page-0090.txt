### Capture by equality or encounter.

This capture does not differ from the one we showed above regarding encounter. [[notes: "Encounter" (rencontre) refers to a capture where a piece moves into a position where its own value exactly matches the value of the opponent's piece it is targeting, provided the distance is correct.]] Therefore, you shall use the same numbers.

### Capture by ambush, or Addition or Subtraction.

Having added the value of two pieces together or subtracted one from the other: If the result is equal to some piece of the adversary located in any space [[notes: The original term *loge* (lodge) refers to the squares or cells on the game board.]] where each of the two [capturing pieces] can reach it by their regular movement, the adversary's piece shall be captured. 

**Example of addition:** let there be the **Round 16** in **D** and the **Triangle 9** in **F**, which are two pieces of the even-numbered side, [[notes: In Rythmomachia, one player plays with "Even" numbers and the other with "Odd" numbers. Here, the author is providing an example where two "Even" pieces combine to capture an "Odd" piece.]] and let the **25** of the odd side be in space **A**. It will follow (inasmuch as the two even numbers **9** and **16** are worth **25**) [[gloss: b]] that this **25** shall be captured when the last of the two pieces, **9** and **16**, is placed in **D** or **F**. And for this capture, the numbers we have previously adapted for "ambush" will be convenient.
