->Victory of Honor.<-

Since honor is a singular good that never leaves the body, whoever shall have a certain quantity of pieces [[notes: *Tables* refers to the physical gaming counters, similar to those used in backgammon or chess]] accompanied by a certain value of numbers—which quantity of pieces and value of numbers shall be prefixed and ordained at the players' pleasure—he shall have the **victory of honor**.

Example: The players have determined that whoever first reaches 100 using eight pieces shall win the game. Whoever, therefore, has these eight pieces: 2, 4, 6, 8, 4, 16, 45, and 15; or indeed 3, 5, 7, 9, 25, 9, 12, and 30—which both equal 100—shall have the victory of honor.
