### THE GAME OF RYTHMOMACHIA

[[notes: Rythmomachia, or "The Battle of Numbers," was a complex board game used in the Middle Ages and Renaissance to teach mathematics and music theory.]]

...between them that, in comparison to another, does not generate harmony and joy; and they must be arranged around some square or tile of the game board [[notes: The *tablier* is the grid-based board, often 8x16 squares.]] upon four other squares corresponding to the four corners of the middle square, whichever one it might be. These victories I shall present in order in the following table on the left-hand side [[notes: *Senestre* is the archaic term for left.]]. And so that one may better contemplate the three proportions [[notes: The three "medieties" or proportions are Arithmetic, Geometric, and Harmonic.]], I will arrange each in its place on the right [[notes: *Dextre* is the archaic term for right.]] directly across from the said victories. To this arrangement, if anyone wishes to raise an objection, they should have a little patience, for after the arrangement of these, we shall answer them.
