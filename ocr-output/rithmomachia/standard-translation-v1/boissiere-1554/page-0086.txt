OF THE PHILOSOPHERS.

...that end so that if it is later captured, it may wear the livery of its victor. [[notes: In this game, "livery" refers to the allegiance of a piece; when the most powerful piece—the Pyramid—is captured, it is often "converted" to the opponent's side rather than being removed from the board.]]

## Of the Pyramides.

Let the two lowest bases of the Pyramids be square, the next two triangular, and the remainder always tapering as it draws upward: however, the very short pyramid will remain truncated, and will not be pointed at all, as is shown here. [[notes: The Pyramid is a composite piece made of several smaller pieces stacked together. The "Even" side's pyramid is called "imperfect" or "truncated" because it lacks the final pieces to reach a single-digit point at the top.]]
