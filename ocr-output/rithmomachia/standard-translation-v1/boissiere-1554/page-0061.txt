->RYTHMOMACHIA, GAME<-

[illu]strated with its crown and glory: for the edification of the combatants, we shall propose all types of victories, and shall state that in the present combat there are two genres of victories: namely, **common** and **proper** victories. **Common victories** are those which are not specific to the game itself, but are instead permitted to train the unrefined [[notes: *Rudes* refers to beginners or those not yet versed in the mathematical principles of the game]] and make them capable of later practicing the **proper victories**. And since they are easier, we shall declare them first. Meanwhile, I will pray the more learned to bear with me as I accommodate those who are unrefined and unpracticed in this game.

## Of common victories.

We have five species of victories, which are: **victory of body**, **victory of wealth**, **victory of lawsuits**, **victory of honor**, and **victory of lawsuits and honor together**. **Victory of body** regards only the pieces [[notes: *Tables* refers to the physical gaming counters, similar to those used in backgammon]] without having regard for the characters or their values. **Victory of wealth** regards only the values, disregarding the number of pieces or characters. **Victory of lawsuits** [[notes: *Victoire de procès*—in this context, "lawsuits" or "disputes" refers to a victory based on the "characters" (the individual digits or numerals) marked on the pieces, rather than their summed mathematical value]] regards only the plurality of characters, without consideration for the value of them or the quantity of pieces. **Victory of honor** regards the quantity of pieces and the value of their numbers.
