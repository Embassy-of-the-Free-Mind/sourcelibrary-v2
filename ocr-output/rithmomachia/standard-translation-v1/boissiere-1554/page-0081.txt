We have shown previously how victories must be achieved in the camp of the adversary; however, if it should happen that not all the pieces [[notes: The original French uses *tables*, a common term for game pieces or counters in the 16th century.]] required for victory are found arranged in the enemy's camp, but a portion of them remain in your own: I advise you that it will suffice if half or the greater part are located there. Note also that the captured pieces will serve to complete your victory alongside the others located in the adversary's camp.

Furthermore, we do not wish to detract from the liberty of the players: if they wish to limit or determine their games differently than we have, let them do so. For if games were not at the pleasure of the players, they would not be games, but rather a constraint.
