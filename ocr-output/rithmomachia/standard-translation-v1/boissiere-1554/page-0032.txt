After this, you shall continue until the summit, always placing one step or base upon the other, decreasing the side of each base by one grain [[notes: "Grains" are the individual units or counters that make up the area of the square pieces]]: So that your first base, with 6 grains on its side, the second [has] 5, the third 4, also the fourth will have only 3, the fifth 2, the sixth 1. From which 6 sides you will mark the squares or bases, as we have shown, and as we will show hereafter in an illustration: It remains to mark the surface of it, or the top of the summit, where previously you had left space to apply two numbers; to mark that place of the summit, gather the square numbers [[notes: A "square number" is the product of a number multiplied by itself, such as 6x6=36]]: Namely the 6 steps or bases of the preceding squares placed in the steps of the pyramid, which are 36, 25, 16, 9, 4, 1: and you will have 91, the suitable number for the summit of your pyramid. And because you already had one of the square tables from the row of **even** numbers, marked with the same number 91: you shall remove that table and put this pyramid in its place, and thus nothing will be superfluous [[notes: In Rithmomachia, the Pyramid is a special piece that represents the sum of several squares. By replacing the 91-square with the 91-pyramid, the total value of the set remains balanced]].
