RYTHMOMACHIA, GAME

## Strategies for Victory

The arrangement of the triumph must be done by regular movement and not by jumping like the knights [[notes: In Rythmomachia, pieces move according to their shape: circles move one space, triangles move two, and squares move three. The "knight" (chevalier) refers to a specialized move similar to Chess, which is forbidden during the final positioning of a victory sequence.]].

When you arrive in the adversary’s field to triumph within it, set out a great number of pieces [[notes: "Tables" in the original; these are the numbered counters used by the players.]] around those with which you wish to triumph, to prevent the adversary—having been warned of your triumph—from later hindering your victory. I have previously warned you and proposed the pieces of numbers proper for victories: as for **square**, **triangular**, and **pyramidal** numbers, you may see more of them in our *Arithmetic* [[notes: The game is based on Pythagorean number theory. Players used "triangular" numbers (like 6, 10), "squares" (like 4, 9), and "pyramids" (complex pieces made of stacked numbers). The author is likely referring to a separate textbook he wrote.]].
