# ->OF THE PHILOSOPHERS.<-

| | |
|---|---|
| **D C B A** | **A B** *diapente* [[notes: A Perfect Fifth (ratio 3:2).]] |
| **2 3 4 6** | **B C** *diatessaron* [[notes: A Perfect Fourth (ratio 4:3).]] |
| **4 6 8 12** | **A C** *diapason* [[notes: An Octave (ratio 2:1 or 4:2).]] |

**A D** *diapason* & *diapente* [[notes: An Octave plus a Fifth, forming a Twelfth (ratio 3:1).]].
The differences **C D** & **D A** *disdiapason* [[notes: A Double Octave (ratio 4:1). The "difference" refers to the value obtained by subtracting one number from another to find a new proportional relationship.]].

**A C** *diapente*. **A D** *diapason*. **A**, in respect to the difference **A C**, *diapente* [[notes: Here the author compares the value of A (12) to the difference between A and C (12 minus 8 equals 4). The resulting ratio 12:4 reduces to 3:1, representing the diapason and diapente together.]]. **C**, at the difference **C D**, *disdiapason* [[notes: Comparing C (8) to the difference of C and D (8 minus 6 equals 2). The ratio 8:2 reduces to 4:1.]].
