# Omnis serratilis pyramide sue basis vno altior: addit super eam: pyramidē cuius basis tetragona est: basi alterius equilatera. <margin>31</margin>

# Omnis serratilis pyramide sue basis duobus altior: eidē triplus esse probat̃ <margin>32</margin>
