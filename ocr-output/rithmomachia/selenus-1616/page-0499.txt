A perspective engraving of a Rythmomachia (The Philosophers' Game) board and its pieces. The board consists of an 8x12 checkered grid. Centered on the board is a rectangular formation of twenty-four geometric pieces arranged in four rows and six columns. 

The pieces are categorized by shape and inscribed with numerals:
- Columns 1 and 2 (from the left) contain circular discs.
- Columns 3 and 4 contain triangular prisms.
- Columns 5 and 6 contain square cubes.
- One piece in the fifth column of the third row is a multi-layered stacked pyramid, with the number "91." inscribed on its base.

The numerals are Western Arabic numerals, each followed by a period.
