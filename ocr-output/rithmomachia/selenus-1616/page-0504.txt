An engraving of a Rythmomachia board and pieces, showing the starting configuration for one side of the game (the "Even" side). The board is a grid of dark and light squares. The pieces are depicted as three-dimensional geometric solids: circles (flat cylinders), triangles (prisms), and squares (cuboid blocks), plus one multi-layered pyramid. Each piece is inscribed with a number.

The pieces are arranged in the following layout:
- **First (outer) column:** Four square blocks numbered 25, 81, 169, and 289.
- **Second column:** Two squares (15, 45), four triangles (25, 20, 42, 49), one tiered pyramid (91), and one square (153).
- **Third column:** Two triangles (9, 6), four circles (4, 16, 36, 64), and two triangles (72, 81).
- **Fourth (inner) column:** Four circles positioned in the central rows, numbered 2, 4, 6, and 8.

The engraving uses hatching and cross-hatching to denote shadows and the dark squares of the board.
