<image-desc>An elaborate architectural engraved title border. At the very top center, the figure of Pythagoras reclines, labeled "Pythagoras." above his head. He points to a large triangle (a Pythagorean table) containing rows of numbers. To his left and right are tiered towers composed of numbered blocks (powers of 9 on the left: 81, 153, 289; powers of 10 on the right: 100, 190, 361). On the left side of the page, a bearded scholar stands in an arched niche, holding a compass and a book. On the right side, a younger man stands in a similar niche, holding a scroll inscribed with numbers. The central text of the title page is framed by these niches. Below the text is a floor with a checkered pattern representing the Rythmomachia game board. At the bottom, an ornate cartouche decorated with three grotesque mask-like faces contains the publisher's imprint.</image-desc>
